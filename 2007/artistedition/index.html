THE CONCEPT

"I'm convinced that a return to the basics is needed /
I like blizzes that burn long like DVS pieces..."
- "Southside Revival"

No vibrant hip-hop scene can exist without a community of graffiti writers, visual artists and graphic designers who do with colors and canvases what we do with beats and rhymes. In essence, doucmenting and proclaiming our existence and experiences in a cultural act of defiance and, at times, survival. From the burners underneath I-5 to downtown art galleries, a quiet reniassance in the Town is on the horizon.

In solidarity with the torchbearers of the 206's hip-hop art scene, Blue Scholars and MASSLINE present the limited Artist Edition Handstyle Tees. Each month, a new t-shirt will be released featuring a design from a different artist. Each artist will take the original "Blue Scholars" handstyle (designed by Sneke - DVS Crew) and run with it, adding their own characters and styles. Through collaborating with the Town's own talented well of artists, we bring the audio and visual together as a smoke signal to let everybody know that a revival is truly underway.

Only 206 shirts will be printed for each design and will be available exclusively on the www.bluescholars.com website and in select Seattle boutiques. A portion of the proceeds from the T-shirt sales will go toward the Blue Scholarship fund, which will be awarded at the end of the school year (details to follow). Check out each artist's bio and the story behind their design. And please visit their website and keep an eye on these artists as they (and hopefully we) continue to do big things.
