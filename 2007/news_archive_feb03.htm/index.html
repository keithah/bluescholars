[feb 7 2003]

Welcome to the grand opening. Apologies for the three months of inactivity here at bluescholars.com. Sabzi and I are hard at work on our as-yet-untitled upcoming album. When is it dropping, you ask? Good question. I know I've been giving inconsistent answers to many inquiring folks. Told some of you that it would be dropping this month, told others sometime before the end of the year. I think I told somebody that I wasn't sure except that we're trying to get it out in time for Sabzi's college graduation, so we can hopefully go on a regional tour and do shows in the summer. So let's just say that we're shooting for a mid-summer release.

Check out the media section for some mp3's available for download. Keep checking back for updates. One love.

- geologic
