[mar 15 2003]

Chicaaaaago! Caught me a cold in the frosty city while Sabzi landed a box full of club records from DJ D-Double's surplus. After a sleepless whirlwind through University of Illinois-Chicago, Evanston, the Baha'i Temple, San Soo Gap San (famed Korean BBQ joint), some club that D-Double spins at, and finally Jona's Ark in Stickney, we're back and back to work on this music thing.

The UIC show was love. Got to meet lots of good folks doing positive work. The UIC campus is like some crazy architectural experiment. Some say many of the buildings were designed to minimize the effectiveness of potentially large crowds of protesters. Even the small windows are reactionary. The huge megadorm is off the heez if you like dorms.

Met a brother named David who lived in Bremerton back in the day and used to listen to some of my old tapes. He said that I inspired him to start writing. That's money, yo! Keep doin' your thing, David!

Peace to Jill and the UIC folks for the hospitality and putting on the event. Peace to Elaine for offering to be the fan club president. Good lookin' out to our big sis Jona for hookin it up and HAPPY BIRTHDAY! Thanks to D-Double, Rexford and Voltaire for housing us. Good to see fam again: Q, Universe Neo, KP, Norm, Marlon Esguerra, Robert Karimi and other folks that make Chicago my favorite city other than Seattle.

On another note, thanks to all yall who downloaded the two tracks we've posted in the media section - "Blink" and "Morning Tea." The feedback is much appreciated. Expect a couple more internet-only tracks in the upcoming weeks.

Happy 6th Birthday to isangmahal arts kollective!

One Love,
