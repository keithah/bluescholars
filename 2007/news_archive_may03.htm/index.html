[may 19, 2003]

Before we get to the good news, a quick rant: public studios are a great thing, even if some uncourteous b\astards who also use it break the equipment and make it nonfunctional for people like us who just want to lay down some vocals. For three weeks, to be exact. This individualistic attitude toward collectively shared property is just the type of thing you'd find in an ultra-capitalist society. Say word.

The good news is that we've graduated from fabric softener pop-guards and shitty mics, and we're still on track for a July release for the upcoming self-titled BLUE SCHOLARS EP. We're a little over halfway done, and it's looking like we'll be blessing yall with 9 hot tracks, including the anti-war anthem Blink and the mellow-type Morning Tea, both featured in the MEDIA section.

Catch Geologic in rare asshole form on the Brainstorm 2 Emcee Battle DVD. Ignore the part where he blows it in the quarterfinals.

Congratulations to brothas Kiwi and Bambu of Native Guns, both takin' vows this spring with their respective queens.
