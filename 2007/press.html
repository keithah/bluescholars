07-2006 | BIllboard
"The Blue Scholars have become a local phenomenon in Seattle. In six months, the hip-hop duo's MC, Geologic, says he and partner DJ Sabzi have blown through an initial pressing of 5,000 copies for recent EP 'The Long March.'"


03-2006 | Vibe
"The Seattle-based duo built up enough of a following in the Pacific Northwest for a national release of their debut, which shows depth in its melodically driven, Latin-flavored instrumentals and intellectually stimulating rhymes."


01/02-2006 | XLR8R
"Drawn in part by their parents' blue-collar work ethic, Seattle's MC Geologic and DJ/Producer Sabzi of Blue Scholars dropped their magnetic self-titled debut last year without any cameos, collabos or the support of a big name label.


"01/02-2006 | URB
"Seattle hip-hop twosome breaks out of the bedroom leagues but doesn't lose the bedroom sound. A notable display from MC Geologic as he mingles personal themes with political observations, and beatsmith Sabzi scores points with unpretentious tracks like "Blue School" and an uplifting "No Rest For the Weary."


06-2006 | The Stranger
We Got Next: Mass Line's Multi-Culti Boom Bap
"Fledgling Seattle record label Mass Line is a natural conclusion to a set of events that have added a whole new moment to local hiphop. "


06-2006 | Seattle Weekly
Music For the Masses
"And though the city has yet to see the fruits of Mass Line's labor, the founders should be praised for taking this initial step. It's easy for intelligent people to sit around and bemoan society's ills; it's another thing to make an effort toward fixing them."


05-2006 | Seattle Weekly
And The Winners Are…
"There's no stepping down from their position at the moment, so it'll be interesting to watch them step it up in '06 and beyond..."


12-2005 | Seattle Weekly
Proletariat Blues
"With a new EP and a demanding live schedule, Blue Scholars are the hardest-working hip-hop band in Seattle."


06-2005 | The Stranger
Round 2: Boom Bap Project vs. Blue Scholars
"The group's political commitment is matched by a strong commitment to place-to Seattle's streets and neighborhoods"


12-2004 | Seattle Post-Intelligencer
These four local bands are on the cusp of bigness
"Blue Scholars... recognizes that, paying tribute to the spirit of conscious hip-hop, the Seattle scene and the struggles of the working class."
