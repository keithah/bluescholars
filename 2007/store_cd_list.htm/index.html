Image not recovered Brainstorm 3 DVD
Released: September 2004

$10.00 + $3.50 S&H
Historical form — submissions are disabled.

Fifty-six emcees face off for the title of Brainstorm 3, respect, and the winner-take-all $2500 prize at the prestigious annual freestyle rap battle. The competition reaches a new level with representation from 12 states and two countries. Defending champion Presence seeks to repeat against former foes such as Geologic and Surge Spittable along with up-and-comer Bad Luk. Rhode Island's Frankie Riptide and a lethal California contingent of NoCanDo, MC Orukusaki, Bo-Rat & Franco also have something to say about who gets served. Ultimately only a judging panel that includes Canibus, Jake One and J-Live holds the power to Brainstorm.

Host: One Be Lo
