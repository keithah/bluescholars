7 Questions with Macklemore

- bluescholars DOUBLE FEATURE

Image not recovered — mack monkey for press

If you’ve been following these posts for the past week, you might have noticed we’re presenting a section titled ’7 Questions with Blue Scholars’ to introduce our close friends and opening lineup for our Seattle DOUBLE FEATURE event.

We understand how boring it can be to read an interview that touches upon a musician’s bio, how long they have been an emcee, and the generic/over-used “what inspires you?” question.  What we really want to do is shine light on process; the intangible parts in between that drive us from beginning to end, versus analyzing the final product.

And with that we present Macklemore: Seattle-based emcee and spiritually connected being that is ever-present while balancing music and life’s dealings. Although, it wasn’t always this easy.  From his sophomore LP The Language of My World, to VS (his latest collaboration with Ryan Lewis), the contrasting colors of subject and lyrical evolution are nothing short of inspiring.

In this order: check The Town music video, center yourself, and prepare for his performance on Saturday, March 27th at The Showbox in Seattle.

1. What’s the last movie you watched? Movies take too long.  Nowadays I’m watching Deadwood.

2. A short review? Deadwood is a show that I watch when the rest of the world is watching Lost.  It takes place sometime in-between pilgrims and gang bangers.

3. What did you think the year 2010 was going to be like 20 years ago? 20 years ago I was 7.  The only thing “Future” I was thinking about [was] this one fine vanilla beauty named Meredith that was in the first grade with me, Bo Jackson shoes and 4 square.

4. How did you meet Blue Scholars? I first met Geo probably around 2003-ish.  I had a bootleg copy of their first album a couple months before it came out, so I was familiar with the music before I really knew them as people.  Their CD release party was at the Vesper Lounge, and there couldn’t have been more then 75 people there.  I’m pretty sure I had met Geo before that, but I was smoking weed professionally back then and my memory sucks.  I had long hair, and that is somewhat embarrassing.  When my hair gets long it is curly.  Respect.

I don’t remember when I met Saba officially, but he does have old MySpace emails I sent him using blackmailable hip-hip phrases like “we should collab” and “come on man, hit me back!”

5. Describe your stage performance in three words. Honest, Engaging, Passionate.  But put those in caps, so it should read something like HONEST, ENGAGING, PASSIONATE.

6. If you had to pick a film genre to classify your music, what would it be? Documentary.

7. Protagonist or Antagonist? I just rap.  A little of both I suppose.
