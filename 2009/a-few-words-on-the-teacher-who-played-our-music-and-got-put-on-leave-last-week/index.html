A Few Words On The Teacher Who Played Our Music And Got Put On Leave Last Week

- bluescholars News

Image not recovered

Audio clip: Adobe Flash Player (version 9 or above) is required to play this audio clip. Download the latest version here. You also need to have JavaScript enabled in your browser.

Last Wednesday, we received a couple emails saying that a teacher in Spokane played, and printed the lyrics to, our song “Commencement Day” (The Long March EP, 2005) to his Senior English class on the first day of school. Awesome. The bad news? He got put on administrative leave for it. Aghast, we blasted the news, at the time still unconfirmed by the school or media, on our own and the Blue Scholars Twitter and Facebook accounts, which was followed by a wave of responses online.

After the Spokane School District confirmed the incident, media outlets hit us up to talk about the ordeal. Online comment boards caught on fire. Local nighttime news and classic rock radio host Bob Rivers interviewed us. We ended up declining or missing other interview requests as it got overwhelming.

From an unfortunate situation, it was good to see people getting riled up, whatever shade of opinion, over the issue of education. With all these divisive, manufactured “issues” (i.e. where a mosque should or shouldn’t be built) dominating the airwaves, it was a breath of fresh air to see folks talk about something much closer to home for most of us.

Our primary objective was to make sure that this incident didn’t come and go without at least a few of you knowing about it. At the same time, we wanted to respect the teacher’s privacy in the matter as all this attention can either positively or negatively affect his livelihood. The school district put out an official statement saying that it was the profanity in the song (five shits and one fuck) that was the issue, and not the song’s blistering criticism of such things as school censorship. We got word that students have organized a petition to challenge the situation. That’s wassup. Now, let’s see what happens. In the meantime, hip someone who hasn’t heard about the matter to what’s happening.

Shoutout to the good folks in Spokane keeping us posted on the matter. Shoutout to all the teachers who play ours, or any hip-hop artists, lyrics in class to spur relevant discussions in class. And shoutout to you, who cared enough to get in the conversation. As school’s just getting back in session, keep it going. We hope it all gets resolved soon and the teacher gets back in the classroom, where they belong. And if not? Well, I guess we’ll find out if that happens (but lets hope not).

“It goes one, for the…”

-Geo, Blue Scholars
