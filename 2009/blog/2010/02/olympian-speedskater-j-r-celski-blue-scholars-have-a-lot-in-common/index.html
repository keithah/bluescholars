Olympic Speedskater J.R. Celski & Blue Scholars Have A Lot In Common

Posted on February 24, 2010
Filed Under Uncategorized | 2 Comments

The Seattle Times published a story about the bronze-medal athlete, exposing J.R. Celski’s love for underground Seattle music and his hope of spreading the word via his Twitter account.

“I really would like to see it put on the map, and one of the ways that’s going to happen is if people talk about it.”

While casually mentioning Blue Scholars and a few of our close friends, such as Macklemore and Grynch,  Celski comes clean about some basement beat-making of his own, while working towards organizing a benefit concert with his favorite Seattle musicians under one roof. Dare we say a Blue Scholars x Celski collab? It’s not impossible.

Check The Seattle Times article here. Maybe we can get Celski to attend next month’s ‘Double Feature’ show at The Showbox? He’d love it. After all, a few of his favorites will be on one stage.
