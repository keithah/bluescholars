Blue Scholars Weekend: DOUBLE FEATURE x Encore Show Recap

Full article text captured in an archived WordPress feed on 20100413023239. The original page HTML was not recovered. Inspect this feed capture.

Seattle: First off, thank you for your energy, spirit and support during such an epic weekend of Blue Scholars shows.  The DOUBLE FEATURE sold-out shows on Friday & Saturday proved to be an evening of audio visual treats – DV One gave us a signature mix of goodies as he dug in the YouTube crates (Soul Train clips!), and we premiered our visual accompaniment to classic, new and remixed tracks.  We’re going to publish a list of the movies we remixed, so If you’re interested in checking any of them out,  stay posted.

Sunday’s Encore Show was truly an encore – we brought back Macklemore, Bambu, Gordon Voidwell and added Vitamin D & DJ Soul One to the bill to complete a weekend that was – in short – filled with momentum that’s carrying us all way into this weekend’s shows in Eugene & Portland (did you get tickets yet?).

Maybe the photos will bring you back to last weekend, or maybe they will prepare you for our upcoming shows.  Whatever the case may be, trust this: the photos below are just a glimpse of the energy that was felt in the crowd and throughout the entire 3 day marathon.  Thanks again, Seattle, and enjoy!  Courtesy of http://www.jkoephoto.com/
