Results Are In: The Winner of “The Future” Photo Contest Is…

Posted on March 22, 2010
Filed Under Contests, The Music | 1 Comment

Image not recovered — 6_DreamOn

Paul Masatani with his photo titled “Dream On”. Here are a few words from Geo and Saba.

Geo: “There’s something both nostalgic and darkly present about this one. Also, Disneyland used to be so futuristic to me. I recently went back to take my son and realized that it hasn’t really changed much.”

Saba: “Disney is loved by some and hated by others. Either way, I like this photo because it captures the eeriness of an increasingly irrelevant past and hints at the crumbling of old world institutions.”

Congratulations, Paul!  You just won yourself entry into one of our upcoming shows, or a bundle of music packaged with love from yours truly.  If you decide to attend a local show, I hear the DOUBLE FEATURE is almost sold out….
