1 of 2: Oregon Recap – EUGENE

Full article text captured in an archived WordPress feed on 20100413023239. The original page HTML was not recovered. Inspect this feed capture.

We’ve played WOW Hall in Eugene a few times now, and each time back has been hotter, sweatier, and smokier than the last. I’ve been telling the homies for a long time how crazy these kids are but, even then, Friday was unexpectedly rambunctious.

Eugene was on one. Especially for an all-ages show stacked with four DJ/MC combinations and no hypemen. Grynch, Bambu & Macklemore (and their respective DJs Nphared, Phatrick & Ryan Lewis) all killed their sets, and got their crowdsurf in during our “North By Northwest” encore. Shoutout to the young homie Marc Mercado who did some live painting during the show and gave Grynch this painting, which he will supposedly understand if and when he ever decides to drop some acid.

Thanks, Eugene. You really know how to make us feel at home. Check the photos from Sukoski Photography, below.
