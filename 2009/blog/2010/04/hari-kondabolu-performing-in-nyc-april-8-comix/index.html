Blue Scholars and Hari Kondabolu go back. Way back. Back before time. Back before Massline Media formed in ‘06, when gas price medians rose to $4.06. He did a short film with Zia called Manoj. The Indian dude you used to see at all the shows. Before he moved to London for a minute, we met in Queens to visit to find the best Dosas in it. And then he moved back home to NYC, he rocked Blue Scholars shirts on late night TV. And we’re pleased to say, the day after today, our dude is making moves in a major way. When you see him at Comix, remember that’s my brother! Rocking a headlining set with Black Dynamite’s brother, better known to all as Baron Vaughn. So go get your tickets at www.comixny.com. Early!
