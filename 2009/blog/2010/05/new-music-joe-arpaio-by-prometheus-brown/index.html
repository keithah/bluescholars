NEW MUSIC: Joe Arpaio by Prometheus Brown

Posted on May 2, 2010
Filed Under The Music, music | 5 Comments

Image not recovered

Joe Arpaio, the self-proclaimed “America’s Toughest Sheriff” of Maricopa County, Arizona and open admirer of the Ku Klux Klan, is the architect behind his state’s new Racial Profiling Act (SB1070). He’s been doing his thing for years in AZ, except now he’s shielded from any legal repercussions. Nearly twenty years ago, Public Enemy recorded “By The Time I Get To Arizona,” in reference to the state’s refusal to recognize Martin Luther King, Jr.’s birthday as a holiday. Twenty years later, that Bomb Squad flip of that ol’ Mandrill sample still knocks, and those lyrics still resonate. Especially on the eve of a day like May 1. Historically known as International Workers’ Day, but in recent years also an annual march for immigrant rights, which we’ve participated in every year since 2005 (footage of our 2007 performance is cut into our “Back Home” music video).

Yesterday’s march in Seattle began at Judkins Playfield (St. Mary’s Church – 611 20th Ave. S), and we were in the Bay Area in solidarity performing at People’s Park. Isulong!
