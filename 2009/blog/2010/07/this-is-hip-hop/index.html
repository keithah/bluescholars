This is Hip-Hop!

Posted on July 11, 2010
Filed Under Misc | 6 Comments

These days, it seems that everywhere I turn (on the Internet) I discover a new blog or writer or some other kind of self-appointed media person that publishes content as if they are some sort of authority on the subject they’re writing about.  Be it “breakdancing” or “global music” or “political issues” or “American Hardcore” or “women” (whatever that means!)… their ideas are presented so beautifully and with such authority, but when I read close, I’m always disappointed to find that they really have no idea what they’re talking about.

This issue always baffled me because I couldn’t really understand why a person would run their mouth about something they had such little knowledge of, and ultimately mislead the groups of people that follow them.

Then, after my friend Saman sent me a link to this video, it all made sense.  Peep it below!  It comes directly from a curriculum called “How Most People Unfortunately Learn About the World”

THIS IS HIP-HOP! from Airwave Ranger on Vimeo.
