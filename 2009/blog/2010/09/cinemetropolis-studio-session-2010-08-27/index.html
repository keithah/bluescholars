CINEMETROPOLIS STUDIO SESSION 2010-08-27

Full article text captured in an archived WordPress feed on 20101013214200. The original page HTML was not recovered. Inspect this feed capture.

Image not recovered

Last month, shortly after Cougfest, the homie Thig Nat from The Physics was gracious enough to open the studio during his lunch break for an afternoon session. While Thig headed back to the day job, Saba and I ran into some technical difficulties and ended up not recording a damn thing.

At least some cool photos came out of the fail, thanks to photographer Greg Nissen and his Leica M2 (50mm Summicron Collapsible, Neopan 400 @ 1600, Ilford DD-X, 13 minutes 68F). In light of our audio engineering impotency, we ended up chopping it up over rangefinders, his hometown of Eugene, Oregon, and Thai Food. Cool dude. Even let a brother borrow his Leica M3 for a test run.

Later, we jetted back to Zia’s spot on Beacon Hill near where we recorded The Long March & Bayani, recording off the same setup we’ve done for years, and banging out three tracks in one night. Felt like old times. Lesson of the day: record where you’re most comfortable and familiar (and then post photos of yourself in a more professional studio to give off the image of #raplife).
