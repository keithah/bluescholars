The five hour drive to Wazzu (I don’t care what anyone says, I’m gonna call it that for the rest of my life) is always worth it. Rival territory for Huskies like ourselves, but one thing I know is that this school parties like a mug. Makes sense, as there’s not much else to do in Pullman. We’ve seen it firsthand several times on past trips, but this time we came through for Cougfest-sans-Coolio with a whole mob of 206ers: Macklemore & Ryan Lewis, Grynch, DJ Nphared, Sol, Lace Cadence & Neema to name a few. Lots of stagediving and trees everywhere. In short, it was memorable night. So memorable that I aint even upset at leaving the extra rolls of film at the hotel before the show. In fact, it’s probably best that I did.

Now, the five hour ride back? That’s another story.
