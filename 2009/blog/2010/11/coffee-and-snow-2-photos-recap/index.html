Coffee and Snow 2: Photos & Recap

Full article text captured in an archived WordPress feed on 20101218164754. The original page HTML was not recovered. Inspect this feed capture.

Image not recovered

So it snowed last Monday. Perfect time for the sequel to the Coffee and Snow video we did in December 2008. I wrote and recorded the track Monday night to one of Sabzi’s old beats and sent the vocal files to him. On Tuesday morning, Zia, along with Sam and Bronwyn, made it up the hill to my crib in South Seattle. We shot all afternoon completely unscripted, and by Tuesday night Saba mixed the vocals into a new beat. Solid. Zia began editing Tuesday night and uploaded a 9 second clip on YouTube that wasn’t meant to be seen by anyone but Saba and I but instead was discovered and blasted on tumblr within minutes by some crafty tumblrfolk. On Wednesday afternoon, Saba linked up with Anthony (aka FALCO) in New York to shoot the rooftop scene. Zia continued editing. I chilled and ate 3 bowls of arroz caldo. Thursday morning, the video was done and uploaded to YouTube before crashing at 99% uploaded. Second upload attempt worked. Then, I developed and scanned the roll of film I shot with while Zia shot me. And here they are.

Photos taken w/ Leica M3 + Leitz Summicron-M collapsible 50mm f/2 + Ilford HP5+ @ ASA 800, 10 min in Ilfotec DDX.
