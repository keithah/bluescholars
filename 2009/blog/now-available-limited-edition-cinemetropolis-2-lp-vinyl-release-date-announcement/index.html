NOW AVAILABLE: LIMITED EDITION CINEMETROPOLIS 2-LP VINYL, RELEASE DATE ANNOUNCEMENT

You spoke, we listened, and yes: we will be pressing the next album, Cinemetropolis, on wax for all you vinyl heads out there.

We’ve added a couple new pledge tiers for the Cinemetropolis Project. For $30, you can get the Cinemetropolis 2-LP vinyl, along with a free digital download of the album weeks before its official release date. For $70, you can get the vinyl along with that limited edition poster, CD, and the digital download.

We are fans of analog technology. Big fans. However, its production costs have risen as its consumer demand dwindles in this rapidly digitizing world. So the vinyl version’s first pressing will also be its last. There will be 500 vinyl copies made, and once they’re gone, they’re gone forever!

Last night, we played a few tracks on Cinemetropolis on KEXP’s Street Sounds (shoutout to Mr. Lar Mizell Jr.), and also dropped the official release date of the album is: June 14th. Don’t sleep! And in case you missed it, you can hear the live on-air interview and Cinemetropolis track previews at KEXP.org. Hit up the KEXP streaming archive and set the listen time to Sun, Mar. 27, 2011 at around 8 p.m.
