MINORITY FEST in BROOKLYN

December 10, 2009

Image not recovered — Das Racist

(the 3rd best picture of Das Racist on the internet)

If any of you are in the New York area tomorrow night, Sabzi is going to be a part of a little festival presented by his friends Das Racist called MINORITYFEST.

Performances!

Ali Wong, Sheng Wang, Hari Kondabolu, Kumail Nanjiani, Das Racist (ft. Sabzi), Gordon Voidwell, Po Po, and Despot!

Discussion!

Panel comprised of a racially diverse array of artists, academics, comedians and writers discussing what they do and issues they face as artists of color. The panel will be moderated by Himanshu Suri and Ashok Kondabolu of Das Racist.

Should be fun.  Doors are at 7pm (December 11th) and the show is at Glasslands.

PHOTOS: GO! MACHINE Part 2

December 7, 2009

Image not recovered — 63220023 (1)

Went back to The Crocodile on Saturday night for Day 2 of Go! Machine. This time equipped with a Minolta Hi-Matic AF2 I chanced upon earlier in the day at Value Village for $2.99. Didn’t have time to cop some film, so I dug around the crib and found an old roll of Kodak Max 800 (@ 400 ISO) that expired in 2006. One annoying little quirk about this camera: the flash (which is a mighty one) takes about 10-15 seconds to charge up. And it gives an annoying continuous beep if you’re too far away from a subject. So every shot was up close. Some too close–the flash turning some of yall into pasty ghosts. Many were pasty ghosts to begin with. I drank significantly less than the night before and the camera was too big to fit in the pocket and occasionally forget about. So I got trigger happy and ran out of film before Mad Rad hit the stage. All good, though. That’s something you have to see for yourself anyway. Go forth and see more photos after the jump:
