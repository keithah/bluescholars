PHOTOS: Minneapolis

November 23, 2009

Image not recovered — 53840023

I’ve always dug Minneapolis since my first trip to the Twin Cities in 2003, when the homie Bao Phi brought me out to co-headline Equilibrium, a monthly spoken word event held at The Loft. Anyone who was there that night heard snippets of verses that would eventually make it onto our (Blue Scholars) first album. And it’s been love every time I’ve come back to rock shows at 7th Street Entry or First Ave. This past weekend, Bao invited me back to rock Equilibrium again. So I took a red-eye flight on Friday night/Saturday morning, grabbed lunch at Midtown Global Market, ran into the Seattle Seahawks at the hotel I stayed at, hit up First Ave. to check out the Brother Ali show, rocked a solo set at The Loft with Sandra Garcia Rivera, grubbed hard in Hmongtown, rewatched the Cotto-Pacquiao fight in my hotel room, and flew home. THE STRANGER GENIUS AWARDS 2009: Zia Mohajerjasbi

November 13, 2009

Image not recovered — Genius_Film-570

We mentioned earlier that the man behind our music videos, Zia Mohajerjasbi, found out that he’s the recipient of The Stranger’s annual Genius Award for Film. The awards ceremony goes down tonight at the Moore Theater, and here’s a bit of the article in this weeks Stranger, written by Charles Mudede:

There are three reasons why Zia Mohajerjasbi won this year’s Genius Award in the film category. The first and leading reason: Through a technical knowledge of film production and sharp artistic instincts, he has been able to democratize “the beautiful image” (described by Roland Barthes in his essay on Greta Garbo’s face) and make it accessible to the people. The second reason: He is developing a new cinematic language for the city. The third: He is the first filmmaker in Seattle with a cosmopolitan project that’s authentic rather than superficial, realistic rather than ideological.
