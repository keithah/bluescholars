Belhaven Meridian

October 12, 2009

Shabazz Palaces – “Belhaven Meridian” [OFFICIAL] from Kahlil Joseph on Vimeo.

check out the latest visuals from Shabazz Palaces, a new project by Ishmael Butler (bk to some as “Butterfly“) + art direction by the homie Maikoiyo.

its stated intention is to be a short film / music video done partly in homage to Charles Burnett’s “Killer of Sheep.”  (you can peep an old Pro Brown review here)

good to see folks trying to innovate and bring deeper meaning to the artform.

Hawaii Photo Series #1

October 6, 2009

not long ago, i purchased a Konica C35 EF3 from some guy on eBay.  i loaded it with a roll of 35mm Fujicolor film and headed to Hawaii.  when the roll was complete, i accidentally wound it too far and pulled the film entirely out of the shell canister.  why? because i’m not a photographer and i have no idea what i’m doing.

somewhere in KTUH, i hid in a completely pitch black room and wound the film up with my hands and stuffed it in another canister and wrapped it with electrical tape.  a day later i developed the pictures at a Longs Drugs on King street in Honolulu and captioned most of them them with Honozooloo’s green broad tip Sharpie in my wack handstyle at the Chinatown-ish Zippy’s around 2am.  that was the night of the New Day Kids benefit at Manifest, the Song For Ourselves show, and the same night that Bambu drank too much and kept singing “Birthday Sex” but with different words that i’d rather not repeat.

GOORIN BROS. x BLUE SCHOLARS

October 5, 2009

a friend we didn’t know we had until recently named Dominique called us the other day and told us about a new store her camp was opening up in the University District.  their company is called Goorin Bros.

they make all types of stylish hats for the hat wearer that wants to be stylish.  it turns out a bunch of our California friends grew up with these guys too, so when they told us about their new store we just had to support.

but hmmm… what to do?  i know!  let’s do a 15 minute set of only brand-new-never-recorded songs (and maybe an OOF! track or two) at the in-store party this weekend!
