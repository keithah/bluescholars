Blue Scholars Performing at 2010 Capitol Hill Block Party (July 23-25)

June 3, 2010

Image not recovered

Seattle! The complete Capitol Hill Block Party lineup has been announced, and Blue Scholars will be playing the mainstage on the festival’s third and final day (Sunday, July 25). MGMT, Atmosphere and The Dead Weather are some of the big draws, but it’s dope to see the biggest showing for Seattle hip-hop at CHBP, with Macklemore, Victor Shade, Shabazz Palaces, THEESatisfaction, Grynch, Spaceman, State of the Artist, Helladope, Mad Rad, Fresh Espresso, Champagne Champagne, Stahi Bros. (Vitamin D & Maineack B), The Physics, Candidt, and Truckasauras all scheduled to perform.

For full lineup and ticket purchases go hither: www.capitolhillblockparty.com.

June 4: Blue Scholars in NYC – Hyphen Magazine Release Party

May 28, 2010

Image not recovered

It’s been awhile, New York. Our last show, at the Bayanihan Festival in Queens last June, got rained out. Aside from Geo’s performance at the Duck Down CMJ showcase, our last show was at Webster Hall on the night before the 2008 election.

That’s all about to change, starting next Friday. We’re playing the Hyphen Magazine release party at The Delancey which you are all invited to attend for only $10 (tickets here).

Hyphen is back in the concrete jungle after seven years to celebrate our issue #20, The Inside/Out Issue. The BIG 2-0. Holy. We know you missed us, and we missed you more!

Massive Monkees Day 2010: May 28 & 29 feat. Prometheus Brown

May 24, 2010

Image not recovered

Massive Monkees is celebrating their 11th anniversary with this weekend. You can catch Geo performing with Big World Breaks at the pre-party at The Crocodile on Friday night (also featuring Double Lo 7: One Be Lo & Yin). On Saturday, the annual crew battle goes down at the Cirque Events Center (formerly known as DV8). Both are all-ages events, so bring the whole family through. For more info visit MassiveMonkees.com or the Massive Monkees Day 2010 Main Event Facebook Page.
