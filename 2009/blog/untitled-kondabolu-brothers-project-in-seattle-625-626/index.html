June 23, 2011

Untitled Kondabolu Brothers Project in Seattle (6/25 & 6/26)

- Geo Events

Seattle: you mightv’e caught Hari at Sasquatch! or at his recent headlining runs at Comedy Underground or Kirkland Performance Center. You may have seen Ashok aka Dap onstage rapping at a Das Racist concert. But have you ever seen them together on the same stage at the same time? Doing a hilarious improv talk show? No, you haven’t! But this weekend, for the first time ever in Seattle, you can.

And we’ll be guest interviewees on Saturday’s show. Info and ticket links below:

———

The Untitled Kondabolu Brothers Project in Seattle! (18+)
Theatre Off Jackson
Seattle, WA

The Legendary Kondabolu Brothers of Queens, Hari (Comedy Central, Jimmy Kimmel Live) and Ashok (‘Dap’ from “Das Racist”) bring their talk show, with little to no scripting, to Seattle’s Theatre Off Jackson (409 7th Ave. S) on Saturday June 25th and Sunday June 26th.

Tickets are $8 in advance and $12 at the door. (Ages 18 and over.)

Past topics have included: Peak Oil theory and the end of the world, the Illuminati, Blipsters, 7 foot tall Indo-Canadian basketball players, Germans and facial hair.
