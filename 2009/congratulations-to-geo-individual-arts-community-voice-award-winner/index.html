Congratulations to Geo, Individual Arts Community Voice Award Winner!

- Hollis Uncategorized

Tonight, Geo will be awarded the Community Voice Award: Individual Artist by Seattle stronghold International Examiner, the Northwest’s only non-profit Asian American newspaper. Big homie Ian Dapiaoen (aka DJ Daps1) contributed the article below to commemorate the work that Geo’s done in various communities since he was an exhibit coordinator at the Wing Luke Museum and a member of the isangmahal arts kollective as a youth. (Click on the picture to enlarge and read!)

Whether it’s talking story, politics and justice and inciting action within his lyrics that reach thousands upon thousands, or facilitating workshops to build young artists and activists across the country, Geo is an invaluable community asset and we’re glad he’s getting the shine he deserves! His art and his community work has inspired and propelled countless people to read up, speak out, pay attention and build movements. Send props and appreciations his way next time you see him!
