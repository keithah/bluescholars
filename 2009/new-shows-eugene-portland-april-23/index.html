NEW SHOWS: Eugene & Portland, April 2/3

- bluescholars Events, Shows, Tour, Tourdates

Image not recovered — OREGONFINAL

OREGON: Please stand up and join us as we share the stage with friends Macklemore & Ryan Lewis, and Bambu & DJ Phatrick, rolling six-deep on stage in both Eugene and Portland on April 2nd & 3rd, 2010.

We’re three weeks away. Get your tickets and get prepped: Check Bambu’s new video – Slow Down – featuring Prometheus Brown, and just in case you’ve slept-in, Macklemore’s The Town video.

FULL LINEUP

Blue Scholars
Macklemore & Ryan Lewis – http://www.myspace.com/macklemore, http://www.ryanlewisproductions.com/
Bambu w/DJ Phatrick – http://www.myspace.com/bambumusic, http://www.djphatrick.wordpress.com/
Grynch (Eugene) - http://getgrynch.com/
