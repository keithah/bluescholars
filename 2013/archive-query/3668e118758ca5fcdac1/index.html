Click the photo to listen to the live audio of my conversation w/ Eddie Huang and Sabzi at Town Hall on Feb. 5 (or here). We talk about his new book Fresh Off The Boat, immigrant-ass parents, law school, sly racist shit people say to Asian folks and how to deal with it, sports, food, and rap.

If you can relate to any or all of those topics, you would be wise to invest in a copy of Eddie’s book Fresh Off The Boat. It’s the best memoir I’ve ever read by someone who is younger than me and still alive.
