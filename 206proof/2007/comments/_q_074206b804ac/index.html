    whats your definition of a fan these days? A lot of these rap guys in the Northwest swear up & down that they have fans.. but when I come to your shows and the 3 people screaming for you the loudest are: 1) your girlfriend 2) your cousin 3) your bestfriend.. is that really a fan? I mean.. they sure as hell wouldn't be coming to support you if you weren't: 1) fuckin' em 2) related to 'em 3) friends with 'em.. you could be the hottest muhfucka spittin it, but I guaran-damn-tee Becky Sue wouldn't be right up front screamin all your words if you weren't banging her throat out every night. A fan to me is someone who knew you because of your talent, recognized it, and supports you because of it. A real fan loves your music and has never even formally met you. I got a couple of those. (Chill, it's only facts, people.)

    I'd say a small portion of the NW scene actually have fans. I won't name names, you should already know.

    What I also notice is that people who knew you before the music tend to try and use their friendship/relation to their advantage: "why can't I get a FREE cd?", "why I gotta PAY to get in?", "when you gonna introduce ME to Tech N9ne?" I lost damn near all 3 (refer to 1-3 in first column) in the past 4 yrs because of me doing this music thing and all I have is fans. They say when you're down and out, your friends will encourage you to keep pushing, but I have fans that do that for me by letting me know they bump my shit.. I think I'll stick with them right now.
