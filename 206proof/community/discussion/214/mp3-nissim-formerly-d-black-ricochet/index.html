MP3: Nissim (Formerly D.Black)- "Ricochet"
  • Sportn_Life November 2012
    Posts: 14

    In
    celebration of our 10 year anniversary, Sportn’ Life Records has
    another new, unreleased track from a man who is no stranger to the
    public.  He may go by another
    name but his music still speaks to his immense talent. Nissim (formerly
    D.Black) has returned to making music and is excited to deliver the same
    positive message he always has, but with a new sound. 


    “Ricochet”,
    is the debut single from OEMCo, the newly formed music company by
    Nissim. The track, written and produced by Nissim, was completed in
    about 8 hours, with the most difficult part being the eq’ing process to
    get the tribal/ ambient sound he was going for. The song is inspired by a
    principle taught in teachings of Emunah.  The
    ideal is that no one needs to stress about anything they don't have or
    that they've tried to acquire. They don't need to make quick or fast
    decisions to poses a desired thing but, rather they should let it be,
    and if the divine sought for them to have it, then it would be
    theirs.The message is clear, “wait it out, pray on it and if it’s meant
    for you, it will come in its due time”, says Nissim.
