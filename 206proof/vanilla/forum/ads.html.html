206Proof.com

Local Artist Advertising

If you are interested please send an email to ads@206proof.com

We are offering free advertising to local artists. All local artist advertising will be on a first come first serve basis. All ads will run for exactly one month before being taken out of circulation to make room for others. We have allocated one free slot in the sidebar, with which no more than 15 ads will be rotating randomly. If we have more requests for ads, we will start scheduling them on a month to month basis. NO ADS FOR SHOWS OR EVENTS WILL RUN HERE. YOU MAY advertise for new releases, general advertising such as a link to your website or myspace, etc.

Ad Format

200px x 200px - this can be split by you but must all be contained in a single graphic which only one link will be applied to.

.gif(non-animated) or .jpg files only. - we may compress your files slightly to ensure proper loading speeds.

Your ad may not contain text smaller than 10px

Do you need an ad created?

Our in-house designers can create an ad for you. You must supply all text and images.

We charge a flat design fee of $250 - you will retain all rights to the ad created, so you will be able to use it wherever you like.

If we have to adjust your graphic to meet our specs we will charge a $75 graphic fee. You will be notified before being charged.
