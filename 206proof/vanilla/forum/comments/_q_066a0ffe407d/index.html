    Plump DJs are London based production and DJ duo Lee Rous and Andy Gardner whose energetic, quirky productions and skilful, pounding DJ sets have acquired a loyal and admiring fan base the world over.
    The Plump DJs sound is revered for its blurring of the boundries between electronic musical styles taking in Breakbeat, House and Techno.
    From the now legendary Laboratoire de Plump in the deepest darkest depths of seedy Soho the Plump DJs have produced and unleashed four major artist albums to date, Eargasm, A Plump Night Out, Saturday Night Lotion and Headthrash, all hugely acclaimed by both fans and music press alike.
    The Plumps have also enjoyed major success both in the clubs and in the charts with over 30 of their own releases and remix commissions from the likes of BT, Orbital, The Freestylers, DeeJay Punk Roc and Mint Royal to name a few.
    After experiencing such longevity on the scene , what is next for the Plump DJs? As well as continually touring the World including Asia, USA and Canada the boys are soon to launch their own record label Grand Hotel which will focus on releasing their own productions and other cutting edge electronic artists.


    REID SPEED
    Play Me Recordigns / Los Angeles
    www.soundcloud.com/reidspeed
    www.facebook.com/djreidspeed
    www.myspace.com/reidspeed
    www.twitter.com/reidspeed

    Reid Speed is a DJ sought far & wide for her party rocking, fast paced, multi-genre dancefloor smashing sets. She was first known to many through her Breakbeat Science drum & bass CDs, but as 1/3 of Breakdown and 1/2 of Beep (her electro/fidget/bassline projects) she has pushed to the cutting edge of EDM. With over 10 records released in 2008, a new mix CD out now on Moist Music, a new label launching (Heart Beats) and many more musical offerings in the works, 2009 looks to be an even bigger year for this energetic crowd favorite. She currently holds residencies at House and the Boom Room in LA and travels far & wide on a weekly basis to reach her audiences.
    In the past year, Reid's mixes have has been featured on the BBC Radio 1 (Annie Nightingale's Best Mix of 2008), Power 106, Groove Radio, Sirius, Dogs On Acid, Missingtoof, Discodust, I Heart Comix, Hype Machine, Crux Da House, NerdyFrames, Media Contender, and more. In her 13+ year career she has released 3 classic mix CDs, dozens of free mixes for the fans, DJ'd for network television shows, judged a Rockstar DJ contest (Beauty & the Beats), and played countless parties, festivals, raves and nightclubs, movie premiers, museums... and even DJ'd a protest on the lawn of the US Capitol building.
    She is or has been sponsored by Shure, Native Instruments, Pioneer & Stanton, and fashion labels Triple 5 Soul, Matix, Sinful, Junkytees, Thief, and Grid. Through the years, Reid has played industry parties for Scion, Diesel, Puma, Pioneer, Numark, J Lindbergh, Beautiful Decay, Dub Frequency, URB, Mixer, BPM, Tiger Beer and Sparks. She has been featured in countless mags like Paper, Nylon, Gear, Flaunt, The Fader, VICE, Tokyon, Karma, URB, BPM & XLR8R, appeared in the "Concentric Beats" documentary, "Sabotage- The Movie", and in her own short cartoon DJ superhero spot on Showtime. Her tracks and mixes have also been used by Puma, Addidas, Showtime, Frisky Radio, Karma Magazine, PlanetDNB, and countless other places worldwide. Truly a multi-talented dynamo, this DJ/ Producer/Artist/Blogger paradigm shifter has already shaped a genuine resonance with the world, and her popularity will only continue to grow.
