    What's good and fresh?

    A long-delayed (due to so many things around me) album has finally been released last night.
    NITRODUCTION is an introduction to a collection of 57 instrumental beats in various styles and sounds.

    I was going to burn CDs and give out copies to people I met whenever I go to shows or venues. (which I had done for my past instrumental projects) But I decided to release it in a digital format first to reach more people in the NW as well as other parts of the planet.

    What you're about to hear is 57 'less-than-2-minute' tracks, just to give you an idea how the beat would sound like. Stricly a first come first served basis. Arrangement, mixing and others will be negotiated and done.
