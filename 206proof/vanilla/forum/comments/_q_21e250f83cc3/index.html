    I just got back from a party and I was chilling with this guy who was in charge of the music. He puts on a song called "Stick N Move"(No, not Neema's song) which I am featured on.

    I pipe up and say.....

    Me: "Hey I recognize that song!"

    Dude: "Really? You know my cousin? He does the chorus and the first verse."

    Me: "Yeah I know. I did the second verse. My name is DJ Heist. How did you get the song? It hasn't been released yet?"

    Dude: "I produced it. You're not the guy on the second verse!"

    Me: "Ummm....YES IT IS!!!

    Dude looks me point blank in the face and says....
    "Look homie. I know you ain't on this song. Why you lying????

    And with that, I shook my head and walked away. Couldn't believe it. One of the best verse I've ever written and I don't get the credit for it! Guy wasn't even giving the slightest thought that I might actually be the dude on the second verse. I know it was a small thing, but it bummed me out none the less!


    AIN'T THAT ABOUT A BITCH!!!

    So my point is.....has this ever happened to any of my fellow Seattle Hip Hop artists??
