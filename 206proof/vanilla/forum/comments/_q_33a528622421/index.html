    Use this:

    1 1/2 cups flour
    1 cup peanut butter
    1/4 cup oil
    3/4 cups agave (don't substitute sugar, they will be too crumbly. You can use honey if you eat that)
    1 tsp. vanilla

    Mix up everything but the flour, then add the flour in two parts. Roll the dough into 2-inch balls, place 2" apart on ungreased cookie pan. Bake at 400°F for 10 to 12 minutes.Seattlest, Sound Magazine
