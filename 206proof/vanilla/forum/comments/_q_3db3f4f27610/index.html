    At only 23 years of age Rusko is already the most sought after and recognized dubstep artist in the world. His genre defining releases combined with high energy live and DJ sets have seen him booked at nearly every major festival from Europe to the Americas and in between.

    Veering away from the dark, serious side of the sound, Rusko brought a highly driven energy and fun approach to the dubstep massive and turned the scene upside down. His sound appeals to many people outside of the dubstep world. His tracks see play from the likes of Pete Tong, Switch, Diplo and Santogold. Similarly, his production projects with The Prodigy, Buraka Som sistema, A-Trak, Kid Sister, Leon Jean-Marie Diplo, Drop the Lime and the Scratch Perverts set him apart from the mold. Further accolades including starting labels Storming Productions, Dub Police, Sub Soldiers and touring alongside fellow top dubstep gunner Caspa.

    Rusko is like Dr Dre, Timbaland and Switch all rolled into one with a serious overdose of bass. His sound continues to develop with different BPM’s that lean to jungle, house, hip hop or electro, either way, whatever BPM or tempo…you can immediately tell a Rusko track.
