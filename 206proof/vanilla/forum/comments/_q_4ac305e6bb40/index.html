    Quantizing

    I did a little exercise and thought i'd share it with you guys. People always come up to me like "you really hate quantizing?" Short answer is an emphatic 'Yes'. Long answer is 'no... not really - with some qualifications'. But I do get tired of listening to people's shit that sounds either a) identical to everything else they've made or b) identical to everything else that's out there. I've come to the conclusion that people either can't hear, don't care about, or are just scared of jumping out of their comfort zone a little bit. Not trying to be high and mighty here, just thought some of you guys that post your beats here regularly might find it interesting.

    Heres the premise: taking technically the exact same drum pattern and giving it a bunch of obviously different grooves. You should be able to hear the differences.

    Straight 50% MPC
    1/16 (triplets) 90s NY
    Mixed Swings
    No Quantize (tapped out)Fuck a Quantize
