    from Myspace -
    In February 2007, Wizdom's first full length solo debut effort, entited, The Book of Wizdom, was released. The album features New Balance (Nate Burleson of the Seattle Seahawks), Octane & Illite (Subterraneous Records), Macklemore, Epidemmik (production), and BMC, with cuts by Risk 1 and Waystylez. March 30, 2007 the CD release party for the album was held at the Nectar Lounge in Fremont, a suburb of Seattle. Over 350 people came out to show support and watch as a new Seattle artist started to gain notarity. Articles written in The Stranger, The Seattle Times, and Sports Illustrated online, as well as reviews by John Book (of okayplaey and music for america) okayplayer.com, and Platform8470 showcased this "new" talented emcee.

    Wizdom is currently finishing his sophomore release, Music: Soul of the Man with producer Epidemmik of Wash DC (beats for Akir). The album is set for a mid-summer release and features Grynch, New Balance, Cancer Rising, Bishop-I, Thig Nat of the Physics, Ike James and Octane. Look for information about this coming soon!

    Wizdom - Invisible
