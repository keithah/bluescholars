    I wouldn't argue with you there. I just can't help but reflect on the countless high heavin' adventures and treasure hunts that went with my childhood. Hook me up with a plastic gun or a toy sword, and it's all over.

    The dopest spots I can remember:

    Pain Field Park (big toy & sandbox where I buried my treasure, and it's still there)
    Meadowdale Park (huge forest where we'd play crazy war games, especially after I saw the movie "Predator")
    Wilcox Park (excellent basketball court, swing set and more forests to explore... hell, I even found a Red Ranger action figure there during the height of my Power Rangers obsession)
    Lynnwood Rec Center (I took swimming lessons there, they kept the water warm all year round, and the ceiling could open up on hot summer days)
    Funtasia (very seldom does a single closed environment pack so much fun, and I mean honest-to-God play all day fun)
    Edmonds City Park (a wading pool, big toy, tires, swings, etc., as well as a huge tree slung low to the ground that looked almost like a shrub... you could spend days hiding inside that thing)

    The downside? They tore down my old elementary school (Lake Stickney Elementary) and built some ritzy ass houses. I miss the ginormous playground there.

    Okay, I'll admit that sounded corny as hell, but what else do you expect from someone who lived there ten years ago??I'll bring the fire till you're soakin' in your seat...
