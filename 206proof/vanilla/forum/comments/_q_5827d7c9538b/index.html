    here is some old school 206 heatery!this cut is by Frame(now known as framework)& crafty 1..the name of this song is called "keep ya moving".. Frame & Crafty 1 were a part of a crew called DSP which stood for "dope style productions"..DSP was funky as fuck!they had one of the dopest producers that i've ever known and he went by the name of Voice 1. however Voice 1 did not produce this song.this song was produced by Eaze of LSR(LoveSick Rhymers).at the beginning of the song you'll hear crafty 1 say Dj F.E. in the place. Dj F.E. was actually Frame who provided the cuts on this song.this song came out in 1992 and was not an actual album or anything.this was just underground seattle ish which was floating around the town,being dubbed from cassette to cassette.if i'm not mistaken this was frame's first song he ever recorded.listen and you'll hear Frame shout out old school 206 B-boys T-Baby(R.I.P)),Breeze(R.I.P), Solo Doe,Single DOE,D2000,Aoron Dickson,& Ugly D..this song will go down as a town classic in my books.this shit still sounds dope till this day.it's hard for me to believe that this was recorded 17 years ago!!dam!well enjoy....
