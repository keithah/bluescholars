    Well, I'm from New York so I was there...

    Alright so, basically I'm in class and get kicked out for telling this chick to look in her text book for the answer to a test question instead of asking me, in turn my teacher kicks me out thinking I'm talking. I go out into the fore-room and there's a disconnected TV in there that I reconnected, wired for cable reception and turned on channel 4 which is NBC back home. By then, I just saw smoke from the first building and was hearing about what happened...when I looked out the window, I saw madddddddd smoke. I looked back on the TV just in time to see the second plane strike, and I looked back out the window and saw even more smoke. I walked back in my classroom and said "I think we need to go home."

    My teacher came out an started yelling at me for wiring the TV back up and exclaiming he was gonna write me up when he stopped himself, saw the Trade's and said..."What's happening?" And I said, the Trade center was just hit by two planes. Immediately we heard crying an all my classmates ran out, I go in the hallway and one of the teachers is just bawling on the floor...saying "My Husband works in the south tower" and she couldn't reach him. I picked my stuff up and literally, literally RAN home...never being more scared in my life. I looked up and like...every plane I saw I got so scared of, I didn't know what was going on doods. I remember getting home and my mom just sitting on the couch with her hand over her mouth, my little sister was still in school...

    I looked back out the window and just, remember seeing everything in black and white it seemed...there was mad smoke over in our area too. I just spent the whole day with my mom's watching in shock

    Finally my sister gets home, all three of us are watching and I remember my mom looking at the building and saying "Lord please not now, don't let this happen now" starting to tear up. That's when the first building completly collapsed...My heart literally stopped, I never in my life, would think something of that magnitude would happen around me. I swore I felt the tremor or after shock. My mom at this point was just crying her eyes out, when I looked back the TV screen the second building came down...and I looked back outside and heard nothing but sirens, my mom was done

    I couldn't say anything, any words I had to say wouldn't come out...I just saw the worst thing possible on TV, and thousands of people just...gone. Chambers street was completly covered in dust, sut and smoke. Everytime on this day, I always get mad shook up...scary shit for real man.

    It's way different watching that shit now, and like...hearing, feeling and smelling that shit. The next morning just smelt like sulfur, it just stunk...badly, I thought I was smelling just dead bodies and fire. I remember being depressed for like 6 months over it

    Damn, Rest In Peace to all the families and my homeboy's mom.
