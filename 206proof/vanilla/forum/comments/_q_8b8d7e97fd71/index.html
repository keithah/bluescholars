    COME CELEBRATE WITH US!

    It's finally here! The long awaited EP RELEASE has arrived!

    Token Folk will be officially releasing "Introducing" the EP on September 16th at the White Rabbit In Fremont / Seattle. Physical copies will be available for all paying guests at the door.

    *_* YEP you heard it! $5 GETS YOU INTO THE SHOW AND A FREE CD!!!!! *_*

    We have a fantastic line up this evening full of GREAT people, good tunes, raw spit, and fabulous vibes!!

    SIDE PONY: If you haven't seen these ladies yet, I am not sure what rock you've been hiding under. Side Pony has been massaging the town into their playland with super fun and smirking jams. Most people probably know them for the joint "Thicky Thick Anthem" as it is one of those tracks that you will find yourself singing days after you hear it. Catchy indeed, but please do not think, by any stretch of the imagination, that disqualifies the ladies from lyrical integrity. Best believe their style won't fit into no skinny minds....unless they're hella stretchy!! You will see what I mean....or you will continue hiding under your rock.

    GREG & JEROME: I once saw Greg & Jerome be announced prior to their performance with the warning "these guys are from the East Coast...show these cats love". Being a midwest baby, the statement stuck out as odd. Then later it made more sense. There aren't cats doing what Greg & Jerome are doing in Seattle. The warning was to the listeners dwelling in their safe-house of swag and party rap, as if to say "don't be skeered! You ARE allowed to think". These cats spit with so much energy that you will walk away sore (in a good way...like that burn after a really healthy work out). Spitfire ak47 mouth equipped, the duo will consume every last available space in your brain with content and charisma. Listen for my favorite track "Pink Elephants" produced by OC Notes.

    THIRDEYETHEORY: But WEIGHT! There's more! Having just released their latest mixtape "Sorry For The Weight" (get it FREE here), these cats are hungry. The mixtape features town familiars such as Graves, ENDRWON, RockWell Powers, Abadawn, UnLimited Mc, AC, and (someone you may have heard of) IFTHEN (of Token Folk). But the truth is, they didn't really need any features since KNOWN and DUMBFOUND are two very proficient spitters themselves. Come see these cats tear it up live ...you will be pleasantly satisfied with yourself for having actually shown up on time for an event. This line up offers no weak links!

    and as with every TOKEN FOLK headlined show...we will be opening the mic up at at the end of our set for the CYPHER!!! who got frees?

    The White Rabbit
    513 North 36th Street
    Seattle, Washington 98103
    (206) 588-0155
