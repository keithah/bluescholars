    i went.

    shop boys sucked. opened with rockstar, made reference to it in each subsequent song.

    baby boy da prince sucked a little less, because he (i swear) did a headstand and while he was on his head did the up and down booty clap. it was pretty ballsy. but his music still sucked.

    mike jones was alright, but it was sad how obvious it was that he could've been one of the big stars on the bill a few years ago. now, he's not cutting it. his new single was unveiled: "ya dig." it sound just like party like a rockstar.

    t-pain sucked. he only sang a little and mostly danced around. he was a way more animated performer than i expected, so that was cool, but he definitely did not do the "hey, i can actually sing and don't need a voice computer to be cool" thing he should've. instead, he let his hits play and sang over them.

    bone was super appreciative to still be famous. they are experiencing a gap-filling commercial resurgence, but artistically i find their new album to be not so good. they were the first group on stage to have a real dj (dj ice), and he juggled two records with one hand. i was impressed. the only time the entire crowd felt the same vibe: crossroads. hands in the air made the place look like amber waves of grain.

    nas' set:
    hh is dead
    dre track off hh is dead
    ny state of mind
    ether (for two bars)
    blackpresident
    represent
    if i ruled the world
    got urself a
    oochie wally
    james brown loop (the one from street dreams' intro) with a long, badass verse i couldn't place
    then he played smells like teen spirit and, no joke, said, "put your hands in the air for my nigga kurt cobain." he said rock is dead, too.
    hate me now
    one mic

    nothing bad (oochie wally is questionable) but there were so many songs i wanted to hear from him. it was the first time i saw him. and LES was his dj.

    luda's set was everything it should've been, but i was bored. i can't explain it. i was not a bit excited to see chingy on stage and less than charmed to hear bobby valentino brag about the pool party he was going to have later at the W.

    BTW: my "all access" pass did not get me backstage where the dope chicken was, but to the bogus vip section where the gross pork was.

    that's it.Matson on Music
