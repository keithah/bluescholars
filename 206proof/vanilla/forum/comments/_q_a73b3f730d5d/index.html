    Not rigged.

    I chose the judges and paid them to do it. They chose their own scores. I never gave them a list of who the top three should be.

    For the record, Waves of the Mind was my favorite group in the battle. They were my favorite performance and Biz is one of my closest friends.

    As the organizer of the event, if it was rigged, they would have won.

    Also, I chose the judges based on who has been critical of MY music. Meli, Sam, Matson and Promo have all given me advice or critiques at some point through some medium. I followed it and they also noted that I had stepped my game up in the certain areas they suggested. For this reason, I selected them. That, and since we are clearly not "buddy buddy," I was hoping to avoid the rigged word coming into play. They were also part of the event before the lineup was set. I felt they stuck to their guns and did well.

    As long as I am here, I would to thank all the acts that participated. You made the night pop off and I thought it was really fun. I was impressed by everyone and I look forward to playing a real show with longer sets and less acts with everyone. I apologize for it running late (as predicted by most Proofers), in particular to Taybot, who missed getting in the Top 3 by 1.5 points with a Freestyled short set. I am also sorry to Stationary Drifters that the CD Player skipped.

    A few things went wrong, but I had fun. I hope everyone enjoyed themselves. Most important apology comes for my t-shirt. It was my intention to appear as a character from the TV show Arrested Development. David Cross's character had a business card that said ANALRAPIST on it. The "Convicted Rappist" shirt was a joke -RAPPIST as in rapper, not rapist- but this was in more poor taste than I originally thought. So, sorry to the women that affected. I didn't have mean intentions there. Just trying to generate laughs.

    Positives: everyone got to do what they do and they did it well! Wiz stole my costume thunder, the Mario outfit was the hit of the night. Fresh Ave impressed me very much with their energy. The top three of Fridge, Asun and Notion seems fair to most people. Notion had the best joke of the night: "I feel like I am about to have sex with the crowd; this will only last 12 minutes and then I will be judged." I thought it was cool to see drums, guitars and all the other little tricks people pulled off to really rock their sets.

    Purpose: these groups got to play for 424 people. (NOT the attendance record, capacity is 450)

    Hidden Agenda: the "scene unity" everyone is calling for... happened. We were all there, all types of folks, and for the most part, got along and had fun.

    It was a learning experience for me. I will fix the kinks. There will be a followup. It's already in the works...

    Thanks for those who participated (judges and acts) and those who attended.Type on Myspace x Download Mustache Immaculate and Well Known Secrets FREE
