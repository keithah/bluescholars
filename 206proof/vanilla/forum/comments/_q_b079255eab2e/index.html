    I went the last two years and it is well worth going. Regardless of whether or not you have Timbaland level production or not, you can pick up some nice little jewels from the panelists, make connections, and also see what else is out there. I would think that part of succeeding in this game is knowing who you're competitors are so this would give you exposure to others that you wouldn't normally have exposure to. I saw alot of producers choppin it up with others to do collaborations. One of the 10 producers that were picked to perform in the beat battle were actually two producers (that came together as a duo) from different cities that met at a conference and decided to come together to do work. You get to chop it first hand with the best in the game, and they don't have an arrogant attitude. Mr. Porter is one of my favorite producers and he sat at the bar with me, in between sessions, and dropped some jewels for like 20 minutes. I wasn't bugging him, he wasn't like "yo this kid is buggin the shit out of me" he was real cool. Where else can you get the opportunity to do that, to be that close to the people that we look up to. It is well worth it man!!! You gotta do it!!
