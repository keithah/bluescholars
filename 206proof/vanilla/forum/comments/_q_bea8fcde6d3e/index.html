    Mine wasn't a waste of time if I look at the experience as the ultimate award. It wasn't a financial boon or anything. I don't own property because I had a hit.

    The arts hasn't so much been a choice as it it's been, "What the fuck else am I gonna do?". I'm lazy and I hate people too much to offer any greater service, much less be a greeter at Wal Mart or "expedite requests" in a call center. I'm a creative. That's what I've got to offer the world whether the product is viable or not. Once in a while it finds a way of taking care of me.

    If I were a motivated superballin' grinder who just happened to make music well, that sure would be cool but the reality is far less awesome. I'm just an artist.

    The public aspect of music was always my least favorite. Half the time I'd be doing shows or interviews I'd feel disgusted. I'm not enough of a people person to go all the way in music. Luckily it's not my lifeline. It's probably the least of my talents and I got it out of my system at the right time in life. I still make it but there's no driving compulsion to do it on a timeline or release all of it.

    And I'd don't think anyone would ever accuse me of being a self-promoter.

    I'm probably not the person to be responding to this though. I suspect there are a shitload of people who are going to regret, even be ashamed of the demands they've made on music.

    But my own truth is that choosing to abandon your artistic inclinations because it might not pay off is like saying you somehow deserve more than all of the artists before you who died insane and penniless. American pop-culture has mislead alot of people into thinking money is a good reason to become and artist. That's retarded. Doing it for any other reason than, "Because I have to" is not a very good idea. I resent the idea that an artist's worth is supposed to be reflected in their bank account. That's a very recent, localized and fleeting understanding of the value of an artist.

    That's just my experience.Why the hell does it need to be about "Teams" Lar?
