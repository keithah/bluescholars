    THE KID ESPI - TWO STEPS (f/ GEO)

    (Produced by Kruse)

    The Kid Espi is a rapper from the Northwest who, like almost every rapper in the northwest, makes music as a passion while maintaining a day job and personal relationships.

    The Sweatshirt Dinner Party EP features production from Sapient, Terminill, Kruse and Flowtope. Names that all ring throughout the Northwest and have collectively produced for everyone from Mr. Fab, Techn9ne, Living Legends, Aesop Rock and Slug of Atmosphere.

    The Sweatshirt Dinner Party EP also features guest appearances from Pigeon John, Geo of Blue Scholars, Grayskul, Debra Arlyn and members of Espi's crew The Garden.

    The Album will be release on iTunes and CD Baby in June.For music check out
