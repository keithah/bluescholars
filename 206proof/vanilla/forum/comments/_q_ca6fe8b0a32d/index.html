    Wiz - sorry, I probably shouldn't use the word hater. Let me edit that. My bigger point is that there are millions of people in this country who buy music, and we're just trying to get a couple hundred thousand of them. If the rest of them don't like the music that's fine.

    When I release an album I like it when people feel passionately about it, but passion is two sided. One of the great things about Common Market and Blue Scholars is that people react strongly to their music. It is really true that if I release an album and no one dislikes it I know immediately it won't be a success. But when I get the sense that there are people who LOVE it, and a bunch who will slam it (and really don't like it), I know all of that will create a buzz, and that tension and buzz will play into the whole marketing campaign.

    Fact is, the new Common Market will get them national attention, the group will tour nationally, and they will be the most popular group out of the Northwest besides maybe the Blue Scholars. Outside of Seattle they may even top Blue Scholars. It'll be fun to watch. Tobacco Road will definitely be the most important hip hop release out of the Northwest this year.
