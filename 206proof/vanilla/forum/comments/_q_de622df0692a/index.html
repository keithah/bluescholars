    Heres how you do it:

    in your comment make a simple bbcode link to the YouTube Video or MP3 File. Here is an example:

    YouTube Video:
    [url=http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=frUPnZMxr08]Sleepless in Seattle: A Horror Story[/url]

    OR

    MP3 Audio
    [url=http://www.somedomain.com/song.mp3]Check out this song[/url]

    Make sure you submit your comment as BBCode and 206proof will do the rest. Have Fun. (you will not see the video or the hear audio in the preview of your post)

    If you do have problems with this functionality please submit your issue as a post in the Technical Category. If the problem persists please email support. Their email address can be found in the About 206Proof section.
