    Saturday Morning Cartoon(SMC) is a Seattle Hip Hop duo consisted of Jonnie Storm(MC/Vocal) and Nitro Fresh(Beats/Talkbox).

    SMC just released their first mixtape called "ABC's & 123's" (46min, 19 tracks).
    All tracks were recorded in a short period of time. Being mostly improvisational (freestyle rap, talkbox, additional synths), this is possibly one of the most unique and fun mixtapes you'll experience. =)
