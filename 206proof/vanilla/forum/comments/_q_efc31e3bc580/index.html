    OH MY GOD, Holy Crapper. This has got to be the craziest and most entertaining shit yet from our favorite hometown villain. Wow, I was left totally speechless at first. Seriously, I didn’t even know what to say or think after reading it. You absolutely have to go check out the newest online interview with Seaclipse. It is by far going to probably become a "Seattle classic" for even our future grand kids to enjoy if left online. When I read the interview I almost fell out of my chair from some of the over-the-top shit he was saying. For months now I’ve been reading so much funny crap about Seaclipse on here that I had to finally sign up tonight, just so I could weigh in with my thoughts and comments on the dude.

    http://www.theflowlive.com/index.php?c=seaclipse_not_sleeping_in_seattle

    After reading the "Flow Live" interview I have to admit. Although thus far I’ve never really been much of a Seaclipse fan, as strange as it may seem I’m beginning to at least respect this dude’s hustle. In fact he might have made a few valid points in what he was saying about not being involved in the Seattle scene compared to being involved in other some cities. Regardless of what everyone thinks about him, I gotta give him some props for talking mad crazy shit in the interview. I had some great laughs. Even though he never once mentions the "206Proff Community". I completely think some of what he was saying was possibly aimed at some the people who are on here that seem to take such pride in consistently hating on the dude. LOL! But just go read it for yourself and you can be the judge. Also another thing I want to say is maybe he isn’t the best rapper from Seattle or maybe he is. I’ll just leave that up to the KUBE 93 listeners or something. Nonetheless he’s got my vote as the most interesting and entertaining "shit talking hip-hop artist" to ever come out of the mean-streets of Seattle, lmfao. Ya got to love it. And I’m sure most of us can agree that with out a doubt the dude always has some outrageous shit to say, you can bank on that. Realizing that so many of you dislike Seaclipse for whatever reason that may be, I hate to say it, but I’m giving some sincere thought to supporting this guy now. But more on the basis of seeing someone from Seattle actually make it big and reach out to the national masses other then Mix-Alot. Not to worry, I have other local favorites that I'm pulling for as well who are also doing their thing. To be very frank with all of you, the buzz surrounding Seaclipse from my circle of friends isn’t all that bad, even in light of all the wild ass rumors that are perpetuated about him on here. I don’t know if any are true or not, and although some very hilarious, I’m the type of person who chooses to judge someone based on factual knowledge and real information, vs. just rumors, gossip or propaganda. Sorry to burst anyone’s bubble, but I must add that as of yet I still haven't seen any real evidence or come across any sort of credible or truly reliable sources in respect to the "seaclipse rumors". Between myself and my friends none of us have ever really listened to much of Seaclipse music other then on his semi-boring myspace page. Even still, most of my friends including myself are starting to believe that this fucking guy might actually have a shot at it, and some of the songs aren't really that bad. Plus I can’t argue with the fact that the man is seemingly making some descent career moves beyond just the northwest, and I can't really say that for to many other Seattle artists, just a small hand full of them. So in closing, let me finish my rant by reminding you that I’m not necessarily a Seaclipse fan, nor have I ever been, until maybe now. But one thing I am, is a true fan of Seattle hip-hop, and with that said I can appreciate any Seattle artists be it Seaclipse or anyone else trying to do their thing and represent us to the rest of the world. With respect to all of you reading this, I understand it might seem like I’m trying to come to his defense or something like that, but with as much crazy shit as he usually has to say in his interviews I don’t think Seaclipse will be requiring any of my help with that. I’m sorry to disappoint anyone viewing this discussion, but I’m not defending him in the least. I'm sure he can defend himself. All I’m doing is simply stating my opinion, and bringing some marginal balance and fairness to what looks to be for the most part a pretty one sided "gang-up style effort" by most of the people on here who talk bad about the guy. So I suppose I’ll just have to go against the grain on this one, and go on record as showing my new found support for Seaclipse. Go get 'em dude! Seaclipse for President. Lol, yeah whatever. Peace and Happy Blogging my friends.

    I can’t wait to see what’s next from good ol' SeaCrazy, opps I meant SeaClipse.
