    it's all subjective Joey. Here are my thoughts from a judging perspective:

    1) I don't give a fuck who the crowd voted for.
    2) I voted for the beats I that I liked better in each battle. Obviously that's completely subjective, but that's how it is. I factored in everything about the beats when making my choices, from sound quality to style, to extra shit added to 'em, etc. But in the end, it comes down to my personal choice. There's no definitive way to say one piece of art is "better" than the other, so if anyone was looking for me to pick the beats that they liked best, you came to the wrong place. All I did was pick the ones that moved me.
    3) For the record, Blast Off's beats were my 2nd favorite collection of the night and were dope all night (the one with the Big L vocals might have been my favorite beat of the evening). I just preferred Ohmega's personally. No hard feelings to anyone I voted against.
    4) I don't really know any of the people in the battle that well, so there was no "homie" shit from my end. Npha's probably the dude I know best out of everyone and I had to vote against him in the first round cause I liked Defone's beats better. Shrug.

    I was talking to J. Moore about the battle before I had any idea who was in it and he mentioned that no matter how you do it, someone will always disagree. True words. It's art, it's completely subjective and neither the crowd's pick, nor the judges', should be taken as gospel.

    Vote for what you dig best...that's what I did at least.

    Sidenote: Shout to Mack for putting on a great event, packing it out, and having Exile and Day up here. Also, I was impressed with the producers.
