    PMP is a site like Soundclick where folks buy and sell teh beats. What makes it not like Soundclick is that A) there is a $300 annual membership fee and B) because of that the level of quality is a lot higher. For you beatmakers who think you can fuck with projects on a major level you can sign up and do track dumps as well for major artists, higher profile indie artists, but unless otherwise noted in very clear terms it's assumed you will be paid for your work. My membership has already paid for itself in spades so I would recommend anyone doing beats on this guy who isn't trash to hop on board. Shit is hyper competitive though, because you have guys like me basically vying for sales against the likes of Large Professor, Marco Polo, Ty Fyffe, Warren G, Jake One, etc. It's tough but I would definitely recommend it.

    You can sign up for a buyers account (which is free) and just listen to unreleased tracks by major producers all day long if you want, easy way to give your work day a soundtrack. If you do sign up please check out www.pmpworldwide.com/mtk and peep some of the stuff I have posted up there and nowhere else.
