# Original material and restoration tooling

The recovery and static-build code is newly prepared tooling. The original Blue Scholars, Prometheus Brown and Sabzi photographs, logos, writing, music, video and design remain attributable to their respective creators and rights holders. An Internet Archive capture is not proof of a public-domain or redistribution license.

Keep historical bylines, captions, copyright notices and credits in recovered source. Verify permission before publishing original material. Do not mark the entire repository with a blanket license that purports to relicense third-party artwork or writing.

Photos in `source/community-media/` are separately licensed under the individual CC BY 2.0 or CC BY-SA 3.0 licenses identified on their Wikimedia Commons records. The supplemental page preserves attribution, source links and each license link, and identifies the images as unchanged. These licenses do not cover other images or historical site content.

No original font files are included in this package. Font downloading is excluded by the acquisition tooling; original font declarations remain available for provenance in raw source, but font-face rules are removed from generated CSS. Typography has not been visually verified.

No video/audio media files are included. Preserved provider IDs and links are not recovered recordings and do not establish current playback availability.
