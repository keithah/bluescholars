# Blue Scholars feed-linked recovery — October 18, 2026

This report covers the saved WordPress feed material and source-linked Wayback lookups. It is a bounded recovery pass, not a claim that every Bluescholars.com capture or every external archive has been exhausted.

Nineteen original RSS/XML response snapshots are preserved under [`recovery/captured-sources/2026-10-18/feeds/`](../../recovery/captured-sources/2026-10-18/feeds/). Their capture dates, original/replay URLs, byte counts, and hashes are in that folder’s `index.json`. Parsing those snapshots yields 128 normalized article URLs from 190 item observations. Each saved item has `content:encoded`; this is original feed content and does not replace a recovered page response.

The feed sweep exact-indexed source-linked article, gallery, and media URLs in paced batches. It recovered 75 dated HTML responses and followed them into page-linked assets. Wayback returned 21 successful dated responses for the Blue Scholars store graphics; these represent seven distinct image bodies (807,150 bytes). Several URL spellings and capture dates resolve to duplicate images, so the manifest preserves each URL/date observation and the checksum makes deduplication visible. Two earlier Tumblr photos and the item-specific B.L.A.P. flyer also remain in the tracked source manifest. All newly saved response bodies retain their original URL, requested and resolved capture timestamp, content type, byte size, SHA-256, and local file path in [`manifest.json`](../../recovery/captured-sources/2026-10-18/manifest.json).

The updated static candidate renders 1,030 recovered pages, including 12 pages whose article body comes from the saved full-content feed material when no full original page response was available. It includes 2,265 marked placeholder routes, one derived reading view, and 1,079 local source assets. Feed-derived pages display their source and capture provenance. No unobserved article text or replacement photography was invented. The complete site remains an unreviewed recovery preview.

Exact-URL lookup results for the remaining feed-linked routes are checkpointed in `recovery/state.json` and summarized in `recovery/feed-index-report.json`. The last sweep queried 43 remaining URLs and found no additional capture rows; one prior exact-index request remains deferred after an archive error. Several image URLs seen in recovered 2010 galleries had no exact Wayback rows. A separate Common Crawl exact image check returned HTTP 504, so it produced no new media. Four feed endpoint replays returned XML or other non-HTML bytes where the current page recovery validator expects a full HTML page; these are feed responses, not evidence that the feed content is absent. An extensionless NetSolHost link also replayed HTML when the record had been misclassified as an image.

The root and `/bluescholars/` candidates were rebuilt and both offline checks passed with zero errors. Automated verification does not establish visual fidelity: `visual_fidelity_verified` and `publish_approved` remain false. The update has not been deployed.

Resume with `.venv311/bin/python -m restore --root . index-feeds --max-urls 30 --delay 1.2 --timeout 30 --retries 2` and `.venv311/bin/python -m restore --root . recover-feeds --max-files 30 --delay 1.2 --timeout 30 --retries 2`. These commands use the saved exact-index checkpoints and do not replace earlier source variants.
