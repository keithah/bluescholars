The Program
a while ago we started talking about what to do for the New Year. after debating about where to play a show and who to put on it, we concluded that there was just way too much goin on in the town to limit an end-of-the-year celebration to just a couple of acts on the night of Dec 31st. besides, i'm sick of working on New Year's Eve!

soooo, we decided to do a series of shows at a good intimate venue and put the whole Town on and call it The Program. it goes as follows:

The Program - December 18 - 22, 2007

Tue Dec 18
Blue Scholars
Secret Special Guest
Unexpected Arrival
Siren's Echo
DJ DV One

Wed Dec 19
Blue Scholars
Common Market
D. Black
Sleep of Old Dominion
Can-U

Thur Dec 20
Blue Scholars
Saturday Knights
Khingz
Grynch
DJ B-Mello

Fri Dec 21
Blue Scholars,
Dyme Def
J. Pinder
GMK
DJ Jake One
 
Sat Dec 22
Blue Scholars
Cancer Rising

DJ Bless One

Neumo's
925 E Pike, Seattle WA
8:00 pm Doors
Advance Tickets $15; $18 DOS.

Tickets at TicketsWest.com

***All Shows ALL AGES except Saturday December 22 (21 +)

Limited Presale with the password of "KEXP" starting October 8th. General public onsale starts October 12th at 10:00 a.m
here.

and there you have it. if you've been a long time fan of Northwest hip hop, come thru! if you're new to the scene, this week will be like a complete sampler of the best and brightest our town has to offer.

saba
