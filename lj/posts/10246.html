Thursday | 1st December 2005
ONLY THREE DAYS LEFT!

Nuff respect due. Last night, your boy Sabzi entered the third installment of the Big Tunes beat battle hosted by Vitamin D. With Maseo, ?uestLove and the town's biggest producers in the house, and against a solid lineup including Bean One and Smoke, our resident beatsmith dropped heater after heater and came home with the crown. Big up!

***

BallerStatus.net says the Blue Scholars LP is a "pretty solid effort."

Meanwhile, Oregon's The Daily Vanguard spits hot fiyah at us, claiming "You don't need a message to make great music" and that "classic hip-hop albums don't have big social messages." Guess It Takes a Nation of Millions to Hold Us Back and By All Means Necessary aren't classics.
