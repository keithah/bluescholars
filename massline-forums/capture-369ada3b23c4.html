Thanks.  The insomnia thing came from my memories of high school.  I really did stay up late, write, make beats and practice djing till the long hours of the night.  Also having the old radio show we had in high school didn't help.  (it originally was on at 2-4am Saturday night aka Sunday morning... later it ended up moving to 11-1am on Friday nights.)  Regardless, this project was really an ode to my memories of when I was a young b-boy.  In fact, "T.E.C.H." and "Wild Style" I originally made my senior year in high school.  I dusted them off, rewrote some of the lyrics and just slightly changed the beats.  (I actually ripped them straight off the old 4-track tape I made in 98.)   HAHA!
