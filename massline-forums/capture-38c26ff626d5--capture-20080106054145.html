statistics aren't as "objective" as people like them to be, I think is the point. Depending on the way a question is ask, the way they're allowed to be answered, who is asked questions, how the answers of the answers of questions are answered, etc, will all lead to a certain statistic. All those variable are decided on in a subjective way, making the whole process of gathering and presenting "objective" statistics an "subjective" act. That's not to say that statistics are worthless, just that they should be heavily scrutinized.
