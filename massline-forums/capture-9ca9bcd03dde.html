11  MASS LINE FORUMS / By Any Means / SUPPORT RAJNII - STOP POLICE BRUTALITY!!! on: April 09, 2007, 01:49:00 pm
Transmitting live from South Seattle, WA.

Teaching artists should not be sent to jail for asking about the well-being of their students.  The madness has to stop.  Come through tomorrow!!!

TUESDAY, APRIL 10TH @ 2:30PM, AT KING COUNTY CORRECTIONAL FACILITY, 500 5TH AVE

Rajnii's personal account is below... if you know my music, you know Rajnii.  If you know Rajnii, you know there aren't that many people more committed to justice, truth-telling and raising up the next generation than him. 



ON APRIL 5TH APPROX 3:30PM ON RAINIER AND HENDERSON OUT SIDE RAINIER BEACH HIGHSCHOOL I WITNESSED A HUGE GROUP OF POLICE JUST OUTSIDE THE GATE OF LAKE WASHINGTON APTS.
 
 AS I WALKED TOWARD THE SCHOOL I SAW ONE OF THE CAST MEMBERS IN THE PLAY I'M DIRECTING, TIA HOLIDAY AND SHE TOLD  ME HER CLASSMATE WAS BEING ARRESTED FOR SPITTING OUT A PIECE OF GUM.
I TURNED TO SEE ONE MALE OFFICER PLACING THE GIRL IN CUFFS INTO THE BACK OF A SQUAD CAR WHILE TWO OTHER MALE OFFICERS STOOD BY THE OFFICER (I CAME LATER TO KNOW WAS OFFICER NELSON I WAS CONCERNED THAT THE GIRL'S PARENTS SHOULD BE NOTIFIED). AND VERY SLOWLY SO AS NOT TO APPEAR A THREAT I APPROACHED THE OFFICERS TO FIND OUT THAT INFORMATION.  OFFICER NELSON WAS NOW IN THE FRONT SEAT ON THE RADIO.  I SAID EXCUSE ME OFFICER I AM A TEACHING ARTIST DIRECTING A PLAY HERE AT RAINIER BEACH AND WOULD LIKE TO KNOW WHAT THE GIRL IS BEING CHARGED AND AM CONCERNED ABOUT HER SAFETY.
I WAS ASKED WHAT MY RELATIONSHIP TO THE GIRL WAS.
I SAID SHE IS ONE OF MY STUDENTS' CLASSMATES.
I WAS TOLD TO BACK UP BY OFFICER NELSON.
I DID BACK UP.
I ASKED AGAIN.
I WAS TOLD BY OFFICER ATOFAU TO BACK UP AS WELL.
I DID.
I ASKED AGAIN.
OFFICER NELSON GOT OUT OF HIS VEHICLE
GRABBED MY RIGHT ARM BEHIND MY BACK AND SLIGHTLY TWISTED IT AND HELD IT FOR SEVERAL SECONDS AS IF TO INSTIGATE SOME RESPONSE OR RESISTANCE.
WHEN I DID NOT RESIST
I WAS RELEASED AND TOLD TO PUT MY HANDS ON THE HOOD OF THE SQUAD CAR.
I THEN ASKED THE GIRL IN CUSTODY WHAT SHE WAS BEING CHARGED WITH.
SHE DID NOT RESPOND.
OFFICER NELSON ASKED ME MY NAME I GAVE IT.
AND TOLD HIM I HAVE NO RECORD AND REITERATED I AM A TEACHING ARTIST DIRECTING A PLAY HERE AT RAINIER BEACH.
OFFICER NELSON PROMPTED OFFICER ATOFAU TO CHECK ME FOR DRUGS AND WEAPONS.
MY POCKETS WERE EMPTIED AND MY BAG TAKEN
I WAS THEN TOLD BY OFFICER NELSON I WAS GOING TO JAIL TOO.
I ASKED FOR WHAT
AND WAS TOLD FOR INTERFERING.
I SAID THIS IS RIDICULOUS.
I JUST WANT TO MAKE SURE THE GIRL IS SAFE AND THAT HER PARENTS ARE NOTIFIED I HAVE NO RECORD AND I HAVE COMPLIED WITH YOUR REQUESTS.
I WAS PUT IN CUFFS AND TAKEN BY OFFICER ATOFAU TO THE OTHER
SQUAD CAR.
I YELLED TO TIA TO TELL BRANDIE FLOOD THE TRAINER ON TOBACCO THAT I HAD BEEN UNJUSTLY ARRESTED AND TO NOTIFY MY MOTHER.
I STILL HAD NOT BEEN READ MY RIGHTS OR GIVEN A CLEAR INDICATION OF WHAT MY OFFENSE HAD BEEN.
I WAS TAKEN TO THE SOUTH PRECINCT AND HELD IN CUSTODY FOR ABOUT 30MINUTES I BELIEVE
BEFORE MY RIGHTS WERE READ TO ME BY MY OFFICER ATOFAU.
SARGENT MCGINNIS AND OFFICER ATOFAU RETURNED TO GET
MY VERSION OF WHAT HAPPENED.
OFFICER NELSON AND OFFICER MCRAE CAME TO EXPRESSS NELSON VERSION OF WHAT HAD OCCURRED TO JUSTIFY MY ARREST.
I WAS TAKEN TO KING COUNTY APPROX 6:30PM STILL UNAWARE OF WHAT MY EXACT CHARGES WERE.
MY BELONGINGS WERE TRANSFERRED TO A PLASTIC BAG AND I WAS TOLD I WOULD HAVE TO RETRIEVE MY PUMA BAG FROM A SITE ON AIRPORT WAY.
 I WAS TOLD TO CHANGE INTO RED JAIL CLOTHING AND GIVEN A
SACK LUNCH AND PLACED IN A CELL.
FOR THE NEXT 5 1/2 HRS I WAS  TRANSFERRED FROM CELL TO CELL AND FINALLY RELEASED AT MIDNIGHT TO DISCOVER I HAD A HEARING FOR OBSTRUCTION OF A PUBLIC OFFICIAL.
THIS WAS THE FIRST TIME I WAS INFORMED OF WHAT I HAD BEEN ARRESTED FOR.
PLEASE ATTEND OR WRITE A LETTER OF REFERENCE
to rajniie@yahoo.com
Please call KOMO KIRO ALL MEDIA SOURCES
FLOOD THEM WITH THIS STORY
 - FOR QUESTIONS ON A CHILD'S BEHALF
 A TEACHER MUST BE ARRESTED?!
MY HEARING IS ON APRIL 10TH @2:30PM 500 5TH AVE KING COUNTY CORRECTIONAL FACILITY.
PLEASE SUPPORT!
I GREATLY APPRECIATE IT!
 
PEACE,
 
RAJNII
