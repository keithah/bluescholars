Little Brother - Getback
« on: October 16, 2007, 02:56:03 am »
i'm pretty impressed with this record so far.  been bumping it for the last two days and if you ask me it's a huge step up over The Minstrel Show.  what stands out to me is how refreshing LB sounds without 9th Wonder.  i wasn't sure if it could work and my nostalgic side wishes there were a couple more 9th beats but man, they really went in a more musical direction with the production...almost like a Kanye-lite kind of thing at some points...and it works!  Big Pooh is heavily slept on if you ask me...kinda like a lyrical everyman type rapper and hands down Phonte is one of the best in the game right now.  LB touches on mad different topic including their break-up with 9th on my favorite track "That Ain't Love".  i dunno but man...i think this album is gonna stay in rotation for a minute for me and it's making me geeked for when they come to town next month.
