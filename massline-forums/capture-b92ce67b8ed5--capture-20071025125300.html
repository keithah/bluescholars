so the unthinkable happens in beautiful Bristish Columbia. Our tour mini van gets broken into!

anyway. we receive a call from reception saying someone has broken into our car around 9pm - just 30 minutes b4 soundcheck!!! iam like WTF. run downstairs and go to the inside carpark and sure enough, our chrysler voyager(mini van) back window has been completely smashed through. so im sweating working out whats taken and whats not.
gone -
a bag of merch(like 50 records, cds, tee shirts),; another bag full of jay (tour driver) clothes; ab rudes bag of shoes and my computer bag are stolen, luckily my computer aint inside as i took it to the room. they say we need to get to look at the cctv footage before they can do anything, otherwise there is no suspect. The receptionist says' we rent the car park from a 3rd party, the cctv cameras are broken,. they say its not their problem. we shouldn't leave stuff in the car and are genarally condercending , unhelpfull and devoid of any emotion. our car was broken into in their rented car park and they are giving me the impression that they really don't care.

so i want to speak to some sort of management to get some sort of clarification

we ring the hotel manager and she says' well its your responsibility'. We were never informed verbally not to keep anything in that car on the voucher u got to put on your dash.'

when we checked in, i was not informed of any of the sort. just that they had secure parking. we were not informed that the security camera's are broken and worst of all, we were not informed that this has happened on a monthly basis. Now i accept we should not leave our car with bags unattended even in a mini van with blacked out windows however i do feel we were not informed of the severity of this area. or that car parks have been targeted in this area on a regular basis.
We never met the hotel manager and the police never came - just gave us a crime report number on the phone.

my travel insurance documents are in the computer bag that was stolen. so iam trying to find out my policy number and where i stand.

the show must go on!!!i need to do a show
