I first became acquainted and impressed with Bill Sherman during his unsuccessful run for the Washington State legislature last year. I was very happy to hear he is running for King County Prosecutor this year. King County Prosecutor may not seem such an important position for you to vote on, but it is probably the most important race this election. When it comes to matters of prosecuting corporate polluters, prosecuting bad cops, standing up for the rights of children and abused women, and drug policy reform, the Prosecutor sets the policy. In front of the King County Prosecutor right now is the case of DV One who was brutally assaulted by Seattle Police, and while the Police have been let off, he is being prosecuted for assault! This is a joke, one we will see more of if another Republican is elected to fill this position. We have seen many other cases like this over the last several years. One way we can help stop this is to choose a progressive rather than a Republican for this office.

After a commanding win in the August primary election, elected officials and community leaders continue to lend their support to the Sherman campaign.  Senator Patty Murray, Senator Maria Cantwell, Congressmen Jay Inslee and Adam Smith, the Sierra Club and Washington Conservation Voters, Filipino American Political Action Group of Washington, NARAL, and more than 20 state legislators. Bill served as an aide to Al Gore and Interior Secretary Bruce Babbitt during the Clinton Administration, and is firmly committed to protecting our health and environmental safety.

Bill will establish an interagency strike force, joining with Federal, State, and local environmental agencies to respond to environmental crimes with the necessary investigatory and legal expertise. He will work with courts, service providers, and legislators to reform the system to emphasize treatment and dropout prevention. He will also sponsor and expand innovative programs that reach kids before they interact with the criminal justice system - making sure that students suspended from school receive help, and aren't neglected at a crucial time. For those nonviolent offenders who commit offenses because of drug addiction or mental health problems, Bill will expand cost-effective approaches so we break the cycle of crime. From his REpublican opponnent you will see the same policies on crime and punishment as you see from the Bush administration.

Sherman is currently a deputy prosecutor for King County who focuses on domestic violence. Sherman is a solid environmentalist with endorsements from Washington Conservation Voters and the Sierra Club. He is also supported by Allied Arts of Seattle, and is an advocate for improved public education.

Sherman not only needs your vote, he needs your money. The Republicans see this as the most important race this election cycle, a way to make sure they have a chance to keep King County voting for Republicans before next year's race for Governor. So they are dumping tons of money into this race and will start going on the attack in the final weeks leading up to this election. So please donate to Bill Sherman. I just did. Go to https://secure.blueutopia.com/billsherman/contribute/ and give him whatever you can - even $10 can help.

6  MASS LINE FORUMS / The Dialogue / MusicTech Summit on: October 02, 2007, 06:25:29 pm
On November 2-3, 2007 the Recording Academy PNW Chapter will produce the second annual MusicTech Summit at Experience Music Project in Seattle, WA. The Summit will build on last year�s success by expanding to two days of programming and extend the areas of dialogue and discussion. Already confirmed, keynote speaker Terry McBride will discuss his role as CEO of the Nettwerk Music Group, his focus on advances in digital technology, intellectual property rights and the future of music distribution, as well as his work as the manager of artists like Avril Lavigne, Barenaked Ladies, Dido, Stereophonics, Sarah McLachlan, All Saints and Jamiroquai. Since starting the label in his apartment in 1984, McBride has been responsible for over 100 million records sold world wide.

The rest of the two days will consist of informative panels, workshops, and presentations featuring a wide variety of local and national representatives from top music and technology companies, artists and writers. Areas covered will include but not be limited to:

Mobile Devices and Music Consumption
Digital Sales and Distribution
Social Networking
Performance Royalties in the Digital World
The Changing Face of Record Labels
Building Online Fan Communities
Touring and New Technologies
Emerging Revenue Streams for Artists
Evening networking receptions will follow both days programming
