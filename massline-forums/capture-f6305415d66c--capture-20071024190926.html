12  MASS LINE FORUMS / Mass Line Artist Discussion / Re: Back Home Music Video on: June 07, 2007, 12:04:02 pm
In general, I think it's hard to deliver clean vocals on a slow (tempo) track, regardless of musical genre. There's more time for the audience to process the words they're hearing and therefore be more critical of the delivery.

Geo's tone really works on this track because the message "Bring 'em back home" was exhausted before the track even hit the CD, right? "I don't want to have to keep on singin' this song." The contrast to Blink is spot on. In Blink, it's live and energetic like the tone of the country in those early stages of the "War Against Terrorism." At this point, this country is unenergetic toward the war. We've adapted to it happening. We're just tired of it. That's reflected in this song: fatigue.

Overall on Bayani, the Scholars went with a more polished sound and obviously used more (multi-band?) compression, which tames the low lows and the high highs in Geo's voice, which does muffle vocal tones.
