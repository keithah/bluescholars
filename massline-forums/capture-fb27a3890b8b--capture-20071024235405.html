Dodge and Burn's Featured Artist Interview for 179

Angel a.k.a. OneSevenNine is one of the funnest and most down to Earth chics on the Seattle art scene right now. We want to share with you some of her awesome illustrations, newest paintings and her own take on the work itself. What Angel had to say when we requested a bio shows off her great sense of humor, admiration for her family and heritage, and what her current inspirations are. Read on to get her take:
http://www.wearedodgeburn.com/featartist-one-seven-nine.php


Dodge&Burn is a Seattle based Visual Arts and Culture magazine focusing primarily on the "Urban Contemporary" Lifestyle. We will feature artists who are offering a unique perspective whether it be social commentary, myth making, personal expression, mass marketing, fine art or kitsch.
