Dear Community and Supporters,

I would like to thank everyone for their care and support during this time. Ive received literally thousands of calls emails and texts from concerned supporters asking what they can do to help. The idea is to show a community of concerned parents, friends and family members. The idea is to stand up together as brothers and sisters for what we believe in.

The picture will be published in the next issue of seaspot magazine along with all the updated info regarding the case.
