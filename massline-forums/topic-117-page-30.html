Aiight hold up people...

Sam and the other that commented on the lack of the other elements.  ACTUALLY - Graf and Breakin was honored at the very first Hiphop Honors in '04 - it was dope - the Rock Steady Crew was honored and did a whole set (it was dope) as well as Herc and Hollywood were honored for the DJ Element.  They even had some life graf work during the bboy sets. 

Now what I think about this years show...

I think Ice T SUCKED as a host in my opinion.  Couldnt stop sayin how HARD he was and promoting his "rap school" show.  Weak...

The beginning started off VERY weak.  I think the "story of the beastie boys" was lame, and they could have done alot better job at honoring a group of guys that made such an impact.  HOWEVER their performance was dope.  Seein MC Lyte mouthing the words was hella cool...And having the likes of Q-tip (diddy and fabulous I could have taken or leaven) - complete with the 80s fade and Buff glasses...he looked fresh - that was dope to hear Tip do a Beastie song...

I LOVED seeing respected artists do covers of the honorees.  Or seeing the candid shots of them in their seats mouthing the words.  Like RZA mouthing every lyric to Rakims joints.  Showed SO much respect.  Then they showed Rakim do the same to the WU tracks.  Made you realize that these cats are FANS too...

Mike Epps comment about Ice Cubes PG-13 movie was hilarious.  Erykah Badu's outfit was funky and scary but cool. 

I agree Eric, seeing KRS in a CM shirt would have been dope - HOWEVER, I was hella impressed he wore a ZULU shirt and was paying respect to Bam...there have been many rumors flying about bad blood between Zulu and the Temple of Hiphop - and he proved them wrong right there. 

MC Lyte proved that she still has it and she looked so wonderful.  Lil Kim frightened me a bit.  But it was dope to see her rock it again.

K...Chris - I KNOW you were KIDDING about One Be Lo --- but I think they have ALOT of rappers/DJs/artists/etc to go through before honoring him.  This is about honoring artists from back in the day.  I think you were joking, just making sure you were, lol. 

oh...and...I am such a huge Rakim fan - did anyone catch the line in that new song he spit?  About the new album just "not meant to be" with Dre?  That was dope he called him out like that. However I must say - Rakim is such a laid back MC - its hard to watch him live.

WU was a dope finale - however I felt they got totally cut off by Ice T promoting his teeny bopper rap school.  Their performance was good - sucked that a few people were missing.  Was anyone else bothered by Method Man holding the mic too close to his mouth - I couldnt understand him at all.  The others were dope though...

They honored some people that I had been waiting for them to honor the first 2 years - so I am glad they did that.
