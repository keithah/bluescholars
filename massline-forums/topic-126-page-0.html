This Sunday, October 22nd,

The Organization Proudly Presents
2Mex, Boom Bap Project, and Cancer Rising

@ Nectar in Fremont.
412 N 36th St
Seattle, WA 98103

Doors at 8, Show starts at 9
10 bucks

Proceeds from the show beneift The Organization, a new nonprofit committed to changing the politics of Washington State.

The Organization's mission is to use music, art, and culture to organize and mobilize young progressive voters - to empower young voters, and help them make their voices heard.

Want to learn more? www.myspace.com/jointheorg or www.the-organization.org

Come show your support for politically conscious hip hop!
