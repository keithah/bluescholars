Y: The Last Man

Anyone here read the comic Y: The Last Man? I just started checking out the graphic novels from the library and shit's addicting. Can't put the things down, then can't wait for the next one to become available. Th basic premis of the series is that there is some sort of "plague" that kills all the male mamals on the planet, except for one guy and his pet (male) monkey, and how the world's changed, and what it means that there's only one male human left alive that they know about. The writing is filled with humor and pop culture references, and they bring up a lot of really interesting things to think about. Anyway, it's probably my favorite comic I'm reading right now, and I was just currious if anyone else was hip to it. It's written by Brian K Vaughn (who writes for a number of other comics, as well as LOST I guess) on DC's Vertigo line.
