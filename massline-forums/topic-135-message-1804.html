her: hi babe
me: hey..why you out here...what happened..[i notice her crying]

her: babe please dont do anything..

and finally guy walks away and has the nerve to say: why you trippin?'
im still mad so i say: you wann do somethin about it?'
you can tell he was scared 'cause he said: 'did i say i wanted to?" as he got down the stairs

we go into the classroom to get her stuff

person in the background outside the classroom comes in and sits down: you need to calm down he already apologized.

me: ITS NONE OF YOUR BUSINESS! [in my head im thinkin THIS AINT IRAQ BITCH STAY OUT!...IM NOT RACISTS and FAR FROM IGNORANT BUT I CANT BLAME MYSELF FOR  ASSUMING SHE DEFENDED THE OTHER STUDENT 'CAUSE THEY ARE WHITE]

my lady was so worried i'd follow the punk [who i recognize from franklin] and beat his ass! made me promise not to do anythin before she went to class. on my way to the library one asian friend of the punk was tryin to tell me he didnt mean it and the teacher wants to get involved...HAH I DONT CARE!
