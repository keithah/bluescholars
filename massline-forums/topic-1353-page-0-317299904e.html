Stigmas of Governement aid mixed with racism...makes me so angry...
« on: March 16, 2007, 09:32:42 am »
So those that know me - know how much I love my local Safeway...I have shopped there for years - they all know me....I am very loyal.

Well for the first time in 6 years there - I had to make a complaint and I felt so bad - but it had to be done. Here is what happened.

I am in the checkout line. The two checkers working I know and they know me by first name and that I am a regular. In my line there are 2 people in front of me. The man checking out was a hispanic man - who was using government assistance checks to purchase essentials for his family.

It seems he didn't understand that there were certain brands of juice that you were supposed to get with these checks. So the "nice" checker, was explaining it to him and getting ready to walk back there and get what he was supposed to have. Like for me - if I have an egg broken, they go get me more - or if I have a buy one get one free and didnt know it - they go get me another one, etc.

The women in front of me started to look impatient and rude towards the man that was holding up the line. As the cashier went to go get the correct juice - the cashier next to her says "NO -- you are not supposed to do that for him - explain to him what he needs, and he can get it himself"....While I thought this was incredibly rude. I said nothing.

So 2 minutes later he came back - wrong juice - the RUDE cashier says to him (looking over and butting in in a line that isnt even hers), "You can't have the ones in the PLASTIC containers, you have to go back and get the CANS"...so he lowers his head - so obviously embarrassed - and goes back to get the cans. The cashier decided to put his stuff aside and help the next lady. SHe is short and acts like she has been inconvenienced - and leaves fast.

As the man is off getting his juice - there is a very obvious uncomfortable silence -- a silence that says no one has the balls to say anything. The RUDE cashier looks at the one that tried to be nice and says "You can't help THOSE PEOPLE any more, THEY need to learn this on their own..."

I turn around to see who heard it - and the man was standing there with his two cans - his head down and trying not to make eye contact with anyone. I started to tear up in line - feeling SO bad for him. KNOWING that if it was ME in line - I wouldnt have been treated that way.

I watched him walk slowly out of the store - looking so alone. I was so angry - it makes me cry now to write it out.

I have been on WIC and government assistance. It helped RA and I in a time when we found it hard to make ends meet. Its hard to accept help as it is - and there is already a stigma attached to those people who use and need it - let along having to bring it into a store and use it....

SO I went to the customer service counter, where they know me. The woman asked if I forgot to use a coupon or if I was going to rent a video. I said no - I told her I am so happy with them as a family store, but am so sad that I have to make my first complaint ever. I explained what happened and that I wanted it to be brought up the next morning with the manager and also at the next staff meeting. That they could use my name, since they all know me - and that I didn't really care which cashiers I pissed off. My heart broke for that man and what he had to endure because of their ignorance, and the staff needs to be talked to and the person reprimanded.

She told me that this person had to be talked to about the same type of situation earlier that day, and that she would make it her priority to have it addressed the next morning.

The whole situation just made me so sad. And even sadder that out of 30 some people who witnessed it - I was the only one with the balls to say anything...
