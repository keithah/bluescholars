Which Way Seattle? Series: Inequality in Public Education
« on: October 04, 2006, 09:29:20 am »

Thursday, October 12, 2006

7pm at The Ethnic Cultural Theatre (UW)



Which Way Seattle? is an ongoing series of civic
dialogues about local and political affairs that
directly
impact Seattle�s African-American community. This
program will discuss the current inequality in Seattle
public schools and its impact on African-American
Students. Inequity is seen in the lack of resources in
poorer schools, increase in racial segregation,
academic tracking, and lack of experienced
teachers.



The Seattle Public Schools along with other public
schools throughout this country are still strongholds
of inequity for African-American students. Join the
CD Forum and moderator Dr. Donald Felder, Education
Specialist at Casey Family
Programs 
(http://rs6.net/tn.jsp?t=oo7n9ybab.0.bphvaybab.lpqmbwbab.2012&ts=S0207&p=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.casey.org%2FHome)and

retired principal of
the Interagency Academy

panelist,
