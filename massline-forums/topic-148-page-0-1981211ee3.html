Below you will see a schedule of events -- and below that, a listing of
the workshops. Hope to see you there!

- the 2006 Seattle Race Conference Committee


        9:00a - 9:45a   Registration* / networking time

        9:45a - 10:30a  Opening welcome and recognition of Zakiya
Stewart

       10:30a - 12:00p  Dr. Nichols keynote

       12:00p -  1:15p  Lunch/ networking time

        1:15p -  2:45p  Workshop session 1 (see list below)

        2:45p -  3:00p  Break/ networking time

        3:00p -  4:30p  Workshop session 2 (same workshops as session
1)

        4:30p -  4:45p  Break/ networking time

        4:45p -  6:00p  Organizing session** (open with spoken word
written in Hip Hop workshop)

        6:00p -  6:15p  Close out with song


Workshops (each will be offered twice; locations might change)

1. Culture In The Classroom / Portland Model (Rainier Room)

2. For White Educators By White Educators (Olympic Room)

3. Healing from Internalized Oppression (Olympic Room)

4. WASL (Shaw Room)

5. Early Childhood Education (Shaw Room)

6. Hip Hop as Education (Orcas Room)

7. Community-Based Education / Connections to the Criminal Justice
System (Fidalgo Room)

* Registration will take place in the foyer between the Rainier and
Olympic Rooms.

** The organizing session (4:45-6:00) will be a fishbowl panel of
organizers speaking to the whole conference about current efforts and
ideas to address racism in education: ways to get involved.

     o All workshops will be discussion-based
     o Youth from Upward Bound have photos they took that show
racism/ inequalities in the school system to put up around the rooms at
the conference.
