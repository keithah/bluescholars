FRIDAY MARCH 30,2007

Gabriel Teodros at Trinity College International Hip Hop Festival
Hartford, CT

Friday March 30 - Sunday April 1, 2007 :: The 2nd Annual Trinity International Hip Hop Festival. A weekend full of events that will bring 2 nights of performances and 3 days of films, lecture panels and workshops from prominent people in the International Hip Hop community. Featured artists include Les Nubians, Narcy (Euphrates), Eternia, La Bruja, Gabriel Teodros, BocaFloja, Omzo, Chosan, KG, Bennchoumy, Baba Israel, The Foundation Movement, LF & DJ Laylo, Rebel Diaz, Surreal Knowledge. Confirmed panelists include Michael Eric Dyson, Jeff Chang, Vijay Prashad, Juba Kalamka, Fable, Marinieves Alba, DJ Laylo, DJ Asho, Vanessa Diaz, LF. Film Screenings include "Hip-Hop Beyond Beats and Rhymes" and "Cuban HipHop: Desde el Principio". FOR MORE INFORMATION :: http://WWW.TRINITYHIPHOP.ORG
