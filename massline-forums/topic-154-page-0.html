Whats up everyone... as I said I am new to the board, and was linked by a friend who a few months ago introduced me to Common Market.  I enjoy the music a lot, but I have eclectic taste in music, especially rap music.  It seems like the majority of the patrons of this board lean towards the more "conscious" rap styles, and more often than not that excludes southern rap music.  They seem to certainly catch a lot of flack from purists, and in my opinion a lot of that isn't warranted.  I am going to list some of my favorite southern rap albums, and if youre a fan, feel free to do so as well.  A healthy, spirited dissertation concerning the role of the south in hip hop is encouraged as well.  Lets get down to the list.



Project Pat - Ghetty Green

This one is excellent.  Project is as much of a lyricist as Stephen Hawking is an acrobat, but when he does rap, it's funny.  Whether or not it's intentional is irrelevant imo, but as a piece of art it stands based solely upon the fact he is passionate about his topics.  In case you were sleeping on the DJ Paul and Juicy J beats, this album is beyond ill.  Their sample game is on point and they are nice on the keys too.

Recommended songs: 528-Cash, Slangin' Rocks, Up there, Represent it, Choices, Ballers



Tear Da Club Up Thugs - Crazyndalastdayz

Another HCP gem.  This album yet again has... interesting lyrics and very good production.  For those of you who are not familiar with tear da club up, its 3-6 mafia and the artist on their label, Hypnotize Camp Posse.  It's not the most progressive album, but this is back when they would talk about satan every so often.  If you ask me, it's a gem. 

Recommended Songs: Who's the crunkest, Push Em Off, Big Business, Triple Six Clubhouse, Get Buck, Get Wild



Scarface - The Fix

This one is crazy.  Whole album is dope, go cop it.

Recommended Songs: All of them, but check the Nas verse on In Between Us



UGK - Ridin Dirty

Their albums have a real ill, blues feel to them.  Bun B is nice but Pimp C is good at talking about trashy shit.  This makes for a nice balance. 

Recommended Songs: Murder, Good Stuff, Hi-Life, Touched



Fiend - Street Life

This album is dark, dark, dark.  I can listen to this one all day long.  Check it out, you will see.

Recommended songs: Mr Womp Womp, The Rock Show, Trip to London, War 4 Reason



C Murder - Bossaline

Good beats, bad raps.  It's your typical no limit CD but it is done well, with mr Gnarls Barkley himself Cee-Lo showing up on a song when he used to be down with Goodie Mob.  (didnt he rejoin the group?)

Recommended Songs: Ghetto Boy, Ride On Dem Bustas, Still Makin Moves, Murder and Daz, Where We Wanna, Like A Jungle

Check some of these out if you aren't familiar with them, and who knows, you might like some of it.
