"There had been rumors around the town for the last few years that our activities were being monitored by the FBI and homeland security. Why? I can think of no good reason, but we all know why. It's COINTELPRO all over again. The political climate in this country has created a heightened air of suspicion of ANY progressive organization - anti-war groups, social justice orgs, pretty much anybody out there calling America out on its contradictions. There's an eye on every corner. Personally, I take it as a sort of compliment that they're so interested in what we do. Means we must be doing something right, or at least something that they feel threatened by."

umm...the reasons for domestic spying aren't solely political. 9/11/2001 saw a few thousand people killed when two towers were struck by flights hijacked inside of our country. i respect what you do completely but you're being pretty narrow-minded if you're willing to discount the fact that an organization that cites social justice as its goal isn't necessarily sincere. a huge percentage of funding for Islamic terrorists comes from charities who claim to be and often are servants of their communities' welfare (Hamas, por ejemple, gives TONS of money to the needy in Palestine but also finances and organizes suicide bombers who slaughter Israeli civilians). the conservative side of me says that the government's violation of your right to privacy is unacceptable, but the liberal side of me says that minor discomfort for good people is a small price to be paid for the continued safety of our country, and i generally tend to agree with the latter. my big problem with the wiretapping was that the Bushleague bypassed the checks and balances that make America distinguishable from an Old World monarchy or Saddam-style dictatorship. he should've gone to a judge for permission (like dickface should've notified the police after he busted his heat) before ordering the wiretap, but the big brother concept itself doesn't worry me as much as terrorism does.

"So here it is. In July, the homie and long-time activist Freedom Siyam (who helped me get organized into Anakbayan) was hit up by FBI agents and questioned about his political leanings and affiliations. Even after his attorney made clear that he was not to be contacted unless a formal investigation was happening, the feds continued to hit up Free's ex-wife's parents and even made an unannounced visit to his ex-wife at her home in August, while Free was in the Philippines. This was after the feds were told to NOT contact her because she is still recovering from brain cancer. At this point, it was apparent that not only was Free under investigation, but ALL of our organizations - Anakbayan Seattle, Gabriela Network (now Pinay sa Seattle) and Philippine-US Solidarity Organization (PUSO). Another PUSO member was contacted via phone while she was not home, and other members' names (including my wife) were brought up in the most recent visitation and questioning of Free's mother at her South Seattle home."

not sayin Freedom (sick name, is it gov't or a nickname?) is in any way affiliated with any terrorist organizations 'cause i don't know dude and you seem to be pretty committed to peaceful resolutions but the FBI wasn't created for the convenience of those intending to take American lives. if the Federal Bureau of INVESTIGATION tries to contact you it's not because they want to know where they can find the new Blue Scholars album. also, i don't mean to sound apathetic but even brain cancer doesn't excuse you from cooperating with a serious inquiry made (hopefully) in the interest of national security (it does, however, earn you a big up from me and my best wishes for a speedy recovery).

"We've kept quiet about this for the last couple months as we planned an appropriate response and to see whether the harassment would cease, since the FBI declared that no formal investigation had been launched. Instead, the harassment has intensified. No organizers have been physically hurt, threatened or detained as of now. However, this type of harassment is a clear violation of people's right to peacefully educate, organize and mobilize within their own communities without feeling threatened by their own government."
