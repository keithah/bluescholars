Saturday, September 30, 2006
     

A Few Thangs
Current mood: working

Tom Morello (from Rage Against The Machine and Audioslave) and I have formed a band called "Street Sweeper". He's making all the music, I'm writing all the lyrics and doing the vocals. Music is almost done, we'll be completely finished soon. More to be announced later.

Me and Mr. FAB (for those familiar with the Hyphy Movement, 'nuff said) will be doing an album together.

The Coup will be touring again, this time with Mr. Lif, from November 23rd to December 20th, stay tuned for cities and dates.

This is a little ways off, but we have been confirmed for The Roots' Feb/March U.S. Tour. Hit Questlove's myspace page and thank him for me. http://www.myspace.com/questlove

I'm trying to make some more headway on The Instigators album with me, M-1, and Stic from dead prez. These two are a couple of my really good friends, but we're all doing so much that it seems our schedules will never sync up. Hit stic.man up and tell him how much you wanna see this project. http://www.myspace.com/sticman_deadprez

We'll be starting work on Silk E's album this week. If you haven't seen her performing live with us, you can hear her on "BabyLet'sHaveABabyBeforeBushDoSomethin'Crazy" and "Laugh/Love/Fuck" on Pick A Bigger Weapon, or "Sleepwalkin'" on Martin Luther's album. She's like a young Tina Turner. Rumour has it that Epitaph is trying to sign her too. Hit up Epitaph president Andy Kaulkin and tell him to give her a shitload of money. http://www.myspace.com/andykaulkin .
Hit up Silk E and say hi: http://www.myspace.com/silke1

I'm in a zone, I've already started workin on The Coup's new album, it won't be so long between albums this time.

Peace!
Boots

P.S.
The Coup is nominated for "Best Hip-Hop Group Of The Year" with the BARS (Bay Area Rap Scene) Awards.

For those who don't know- This is a VERY big media event here in the Bay Area, which has a national impact because of all the current hype around here, and we need all the publicity we can get. I think it will be broadcast live on TV this year.

The voting is done on the internet, you do not have to be from the Bay Area to vote.
There is only one vote per category allowed per computer.
So if you're going to vote more than once it has to be from a different computer.

Please Vote Now. Soon it will be too late

Go to www.barsawards.com, hit "Vote", then hit "Secondary Awards", and vote for us. They have mispelled our name as "The Coupe", but don't let that stop you.
