My first job was working at a 2 screen movie theater in White Plains, NY.  During the holiday season, the manager stuck me in a  5 x 7 ft room  popping popcorn for like 4 hours straight with no break.  And one day, when I wasn't working, the place was robbed at gunpoint.  The one guy working the front was this 6' 2", like 240lbs football player from my highschool.  The stickup dude taped him up with ducktape on the floor behind the counter (first time I fully realized that bein' big doesn't gurantee your safety).  Then dude wen't upstairs to the manager's office to try and get money out of the safe, but the manager confronted him, wouldn't give him any money, and wrestled the gun away from him.  Some of ya'll might think what he did was brave (and it kinda was), some might think it was stupid cause he might've got shot (which is true too), but the thing that bothered me the most was that the gun went off in the struggle and the bullet went through the wall and into the theater.  Nobody got hit, or even noticed what happened but the situation could have been a lot worse.  Anyway, I didn't find out about any of this until I went in to pick up my paycheck the next day.  Real glad I wan't workin' that night.
