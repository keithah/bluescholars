we went on mad early on saturday (1pm or so) on the yeti stage... it was my first time at the gorge and the beauty of our surroundings just took my breath away... it felt great being backstage like 20 deep... it was like we brought a whole neighborhood, the way it should be.  the set ended up being my favorite show we've ever put on... better then the release party... we had the full band... amos miller on keys, mark oi on guitar, aaron walker-loud on drums, camilo estrada on bass, and wd4d on cuts... and helping out on vocals was jerm from alpha-p (do u), sistahailstorm & ispire from canary sing (warriors rrreeemix), kwame (no label), and rufio (rest o' me dayz)... as well as nin-g the hyphogropher dancing along with hailstorm, rufio, etc... if you missed it, damn ya'll missed a show!!!  ozomatli killed it as well... i'm sad we missed bjork... but the land and the sky definitley outshined everyone (it was so beautiful)  to everyone that came throo... thanks!  we're in phoenix now... hella homesick and i can't wait to see ya'll mothertruckers again...

yeah it was dope - here are some pics we took.  Right before the CM set - the main stage shut down because of the high winds - so we got the overflow...about 3,000 people at the height of the set - very dope indeed. 

When we got there - went to the catering area outside mainstage - Michael Franti was sitting there kicking around the soccer ball - looked up and smiled and said "Whats up!...Hey when are you guys playin today I wanna catch your set..." then as we walked away his band member said "Who was that?" and Franti said "That was Common Market man..."  Very nice to be remembered.  Then we sat with Gift of Gab and had some dinner after the set - very cool guy - remembered us from playing in Tacoma at UPS earlier this year....

So the set was dope - the wind blew so hard - Sabzi had to STOP using vinyl cause the needle kept BLOWING off the record!  There were tons of fans there singing along - and even more new fans made.
