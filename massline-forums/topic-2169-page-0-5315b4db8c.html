The Attitude of the Blue Scholars performance
« on: May 13, 2007, 07:52:45 pm »
Saturday we hit up the Showbox and were thrilled with one of the greatest mixes of old and new music from Blue Scholars. All props to the band and Massline Media members for showing up and really giving us a meaningful and wonderful performance to remember.

Just wanted to say that the attitude and posture of humility that you have consistently shown in your music and performances bodes well for a revival of hip-hop. There are countless individuals that are looking for what you are pouring your heart and soul into and we really appreciate your down to earth style and groove. I hope you never lose touch with that sense of humility.

love,
