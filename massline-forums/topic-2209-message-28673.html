i think your premise is flawed there.  no 'ism' is going to provide a solution except maybe pragmatism.  marx was a great thinker, had many revolutionary and powerful insites, but there were flaws in his annalysis, and a century of trying to apply the theories dogmatically has pointed out many of them.  you have to take the good and throw out the bad, and if something isn't achieving the desired results you have to modify the behavior.  marxism is not of a higher moral caliber than other systems, its moral caliber is only as strong as that of the people supporting it.  but that being said, marxism is not the root of all evil either.  and attempts to disreguard his entire annalysis because some of his conclusions were not well supported is just as misguided as worshipping marx as the god of the proletariate or something.  they key is understanding what marx concluded and what led him to those conclusions, only in that can they be applied where they are relevent and modified where not.

as for justifications of the chinese invasion.  i don't think it was justifiable, military invasions never are.  the united states isn't the only imperialist power out there, and its deffinately not the only power with imperial asperations.  and when a country like china invades a sovereign nature, no matter what its intentions were, it is an act of agressive warfair.  the only means of effecting social change that i think are ever justifiable are education, and organization, and i don't mean to imply pacifism with that, but non-violence (there is a difference).

the point of the link geo posted is not to try to justify chinese actions in any way, but to explain why they occured, and why they were supported by much of the general populace.  and to point out that while life under chinese rule might not be completely free, a return to what preceeded them would represent a regression to mideval serf slavery, and torture, it would be many many fold worse than what we've condemned in afghanistan's taliban regime.  as the parenti said in his last few paragraphs, he's not trying to justify the behavior of china at all, he's pointing out that what many uninformed westerners support, a return to religious rule in tibet by the dali lama, would be disasterous.
