Title: JUNE 9: White Center Block Party ft. Khingz, Nam, Muridian, DJ Daps1 and more
Post by: olaf on June 05, 2007, 04:19:11 pm
(http://farm2.static.flickr.com/1342/532411119_e3e53a1cb8.jpg)

On Saturday, June 9th, The Salvation Army and the City of Seattle are proud to present the White Center Teen Block Party from 1:00 to 5:00 pm at The Salvation Army Community Center in White Center! This event will feature live local hip hop music, barbecue and food, and three-on-three basketball tournament.

Featured artists include Khingz, Nam, Muridian, Truth & Knowledge, T-Kewl and more with DJ DAPS1 on the ones and twos. There will also be an open mic for poets, musicians, and performers to present their craft to the community. Show up early to sign up for the open mic!

This event is totally free and open to the public. Bring your friends and family! Event will go on rain or shine.
If you'd like to volunteer at the event or have any questions, contact Brett Jelinek at 206-767-3150 ext. 104 or email brett.jelinek@gmail.com.
