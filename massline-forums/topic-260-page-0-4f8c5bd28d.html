Title: Video Posting Instructions
Post by: badempire on November 05, 2006, 09:28:22 pm
This video forum is a place for you to post your favorite hip hop videos - of MassLine groups, stuff you shoot live, Classics, new stuff, etc. Preference is to keep it hip hop related. And quality - don't post your crappy 2 second cell phone vids here. Step it up. [added by Meinert]

To post a YouTube video, select everything in the Address Bar after the "=" sign. For example, for this URL:

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GOViVi-6IVo

Select: GOViVi-6IVo

Click on the YouTube button when you get to the post, or reply window and paste the link in between the two brackets: [yt...]GOViVi-6IVo[/yt]

Press the post button and you're done!
