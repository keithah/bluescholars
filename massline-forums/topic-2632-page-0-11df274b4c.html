So whats the deal?

Basically, a few US and British companies, the biggest of which just happened to also be George Bush's largest campaign contributer in 2004, and is currently Guilliani's biggest donner for 2008, have been syphonning off the money US tax payers have given to African countries in the form of debt relief and donations.  Money specifically earmarked for fighting AIDS, and educating children. 

How do they do it? 

First they buy up the debt of an african country, usually at pennies on the dollar.  Something that looks like a smart sale to the previous debt holder given all the talk about forgiving debt and such. 

Second, congress earmarks millions of dollars in aid money for african countries on the condition that the money can only be spent on fighting AIDS and Malaria, or on education and worker training programs.  To ensure that this is how it is used, congress makes it also mandatory that the money be held in US banks, where its uses can be more easilly monitored. 

Third, knowing that the money is held in US Banks; knowing that they have the tacit approval of the President and the Justice Deptartment; and now owning the debt of the countries which was purchased for pennies on the dollar; Bush's buddies sue the governments of the african countries for the full amount of the debt owed to them, and then get a court order to transfer the money out of US government account and into their corporate acounts. 

FUCKED UP SHIT.  and this is the sole business that most of these corporations do.  Millions have already been stolen.  Please take some time to write your representatives and demand that this loop hole gets changed.  Pressure is growing already to do something about it, but they have to know that this is an issue we care about.
