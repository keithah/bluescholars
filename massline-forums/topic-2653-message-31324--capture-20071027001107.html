Myspace message of the day:

From:   
Lars
http://www.myspace.com/studentlone
Date:    Jun 15, 2007 1:14 PM
Flag as Spam or Report Abuse [ ? ]

Subject:   Back Home...

You should stop singing that song because it SUCKS! Don't you realize how demoralizing this song is? As a soldier it is hard enough having to leave our families and travel overseas to fight a war, let alone being stuck in harsh environments and then find out that our own citizens do not support us (as shown is your song). As soldiers, we do this job because we believe it is better to fight somewhere else than have to watch our citizens die in tragic events like 9-11-2001. As crapy as the environment and work is over in Iraq it is still better than seeing our sky scrapers fall and hearing about thousands who have died at the hands of terrorists and that feeling helpless that goes along with seeing these tragic events. Our actions there are not a state of helplessness but one of action. It is nice to have people say things like "way to go", "keep charging", "you are a hero" because of our sacrifices. We are fighting for a cause over in Iraq and Afghanistan and if we stop fighting in Iraq and Afghanistan another 9-11 will occur because we as a nation are hated for our Christian beliefs, our tollerance to others beliefs systems, and our prosperity. If you think we can just drop our guns and walk away from this you are extremely ignorant and very stupid. Pick up a history book sometime and see how that worked for Europe in the late 1930's and the U.S.A. in 1941. Why don't you try writing and singing a song that praises the men and women who have more guts than you and who are willing to go fight a war that you see is unfit? Write a song to those who are willing to give up hot showers (and go days without one), to soldiers who are willing to leave their homes and sleep in a tent, and leave their king size bed to sleep on a cot or the ground. Stop complaining about the pathetic 3.8% of the US budget that goes to supporting and equiping the military and start saying "Thank You" to the troops for fighting the war with the limited supplies they have. Oh, and maybe you should tell the government to give our military more money so that they could do thier job better. This nation did not become the greatest nation because we decided not to get involved with wars or because we didn't want to fight. Quite the opposite. Our abbility to come together as One Nation, Under God, Indivisible with Liberty and Justice for all is what made us the greatest nation.
I hope this letter gives you a little insight into what this military officer thinks. If you continue to write songs like this and choose to offend many of us soldiers just know that we will continue to fight and sacrifice our lives so that you have your freedom of speach and the ability to have an opinion. Take care,
Lars
LT USN
