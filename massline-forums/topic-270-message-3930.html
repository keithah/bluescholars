Whatup y'all? We finally got a "pop culture" forum (thaswassup).

Because this board is gonna deal with movies and TV shows and books, it's always good to keep in mind that NOT EVERYONE has seen/read ______. So here's a couple tips:

1) Do NOT post Spoilers in the subject line! <---hella important ("OH Shit Bane broke Batman's back!" is an example)

2) If you are going to post a potential spoiler, please put ***SPOILER ALERT*** or some shit like that at the beginning of your message. Remember when Kramer told Jerry who won the Mets game after Jerry taped it and avoided everything/everyone before coming home? Yeah you don't wanna feel like that (although this Seinfeld season 1 reference is a spoiler in itself if you haven't seen the DVD set).

I think that covers it. It is also common sense to NOT post anything pornographic.
