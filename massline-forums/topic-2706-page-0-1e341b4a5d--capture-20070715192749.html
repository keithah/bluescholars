Why is paranoia so destructive?

I think it is because we are searching for security in world that has none to offer us. We don't want to be decieved or betrayed.  Or we don't want to lost the things that we value. So, rather then enjoying where we are and enjoying what we have, and enjoying who we are with... we wait.  We wait, look for, and anticipate the betrayal.  We wait the catch that lie.  And then we are destroyed when it comes.

But You and I both know that our destruction came when we began to wait.

Now... your thoughts.
