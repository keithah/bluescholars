PHIL. T  (NOT JOHNSON) LOL




BAYAN-USA, Pinay sa Seattle, AnakBayan Seattle, the Arts Kollective-Organizing Committee and the Philippine U.S. Solidarity Organization host an evening of good music with good people for a good cause.

Fire for the People!
Celebrating the BAYAN-USA Pacific North West Regional Conference and
the congregation of socially conscious and politically active Filipinos,
Filipino Americans and allies in the Philippine liberation movement from all
over the Pacific and the Northwest.

June 30, 2007
Hidmo (20th and Jackson in the Central District)
8pm Socialize / Karaoke - 10pm Performances

Featuring Performances By:
Rogue Pinay
Kiwi (of Native Guns)
Bridget Hermano (of Battalion Entertainment)
Prometheus Brown (Geologic of Blue Scholars)
TrickNTreat DJ's (DJ Phatrick and DJ Treatunice)

Minimum $5 donation or (Liberated) Office Supplies
No one will be turned away for lack of funds.

Proceeds go to the BAYAN-USA Legal Defense Fund, so we can keep bringing more fire for the people.

WHAT IS BAYAN USA, THOUGH, HOMIE???
BAYAN-USA is an alliance of progressive Filipino groups in the U.S. representing organizations of students, scholars, women, workers, and youth. As the only international chapter of Bagong Alyansang Makabayan (BAYAN-Philippines), BAYAN-USA serves as an information bureau for the national democratic movement of the Philippines and as a campaign center for anti-imperialist Filipinos in the U.S.
