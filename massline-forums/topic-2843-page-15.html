Sabzi's slow outros are phenominal.

One of my favorite musical trademarks of the Blue Scholars formula.

The most effective I think it's ever been for me is on "Burnt Offering"...I think it takes almost like a minute and a half for the song to conlude after Geo says his last "give thanks to the most the least that I can do"...and its just such a beautiful song musically, that minute 30 seconds is like a final conclusion.  And yes, the ending of "La Botella" is VERY much like a solo.

Being a filmmaker, Sabzi's outros always remind me of the closing credits to a film. The majority of people, impatient and ignorant to its power will usually leave the theatre as soon as the film ends. But I always sit there, at least for most of the credits. Not necessarily to read everyone who worked on the movie, but to listen to the music and sit in the dark and use that time to reflect on what I had just seen. Very similar experience for me.
