my fish/"proximity to water" tattoo (from the "blue scholars tattoo, filthy!" post) was only the beginning of my blue scholars homage tattoo. I'm happy to report that, after 6 weeks and 3 appointments, it's all done with.

the fish on my ribs with the line from "the inkwell" had so many personal things incoporated into it that it didn't feel as blue scholars-oriented as I'd originally intended. I had to take it up a notch and dedicate something solely to them- they were the whole inspiration behind me even starting this in the first place.

so today, back to slave to the needle I went, and my artist (josh- seek him out of you ever wanna get a tatt) did an amazing job. now, I have two pieces that honor the scholars and everything they do.

I thought I loved seattle before, but they somehow made me love it even more- even by pointing out its faults. they've educated me about things that I otherwise wouldn't have known about and have made me think about local and global society in a whole new light. I'm thankful to them for the educating they do for everyone willing to listen, and for what they've done for the town that I love.

on the off chance that they do start rapping about drugs and hos (personally, I think it'll be a cold day in hell before they do), I'll still at least love them for who they were. in that case, I would still cherish the years that they gave me with music with a message. I guess that then it might also be considered a bit of a memorial piece, because I'd be commemorating something that had died. (but again, I would place all my chips on the boys staying true to their current style and messages.)
