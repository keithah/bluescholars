the oversized editions would command 30 bucks only if they're NM (near mint) condition. because of their size and audience (kiddies and teens), it's hard to really keep them in good condition. i have several oversized comics myself (including a star wars one, son!). HOWEVER, the Superman Vs Spiderman is highly valuable. Looking at the condition from the pic (VG-FN - very good to fine), it might be worth $25, but NM copies are worth around $95. And plus, Marvel/DC crossovers are pretty cool, especially with these two prominent characters.

i'm not even sure if there's protective backing boards and sleeves for these things, unless someone has a good lead.

my opinion is to keep ALL the comics, and maybe get rid of some of the Wizard mags (unless you have issues 1-10 haha). Wizard is basically a comic mag (and now pop culture mag) that has a price guide in the back. the mag's price guide and opinions are one of the most influential in the game (besides Overstreet).
