Common Market and Ab Creole at the Homeskillet Fest last night
« on: July 15, 2007, 10:43:37 am »
I think I can speak for EVERYONE (RA, Gabe, Khingz, WD4D,Madison...) that this is an amazing place with very amazing and blessed people...

This small, village town - has the close night community that is so evident in their everyday life.  And it raised 2 boys - who have started this music festival.  It may not compare in size to the likes of Sasquatch or Bumbershoot - but what it dosent have in flare - it totally makes up for in heart....i know that sounds cheesy - but these cats are amazing...

Being part of so many shows - its so easy to see a second annual festival, in a small town, not do well.  But this community has bound together to make it happen and support eachother.  The music on the bill dosent matter - folk, jazz, hiphop - the villagers that have been here their whole lives, still came through and respected the music. 

But they didn't just come support - they all WERE the support.  These guys had sponsors for everything - local chefs to make food for the artists (BOMB food, all with fresh locally caught fish ofcourse), the locally owned hotels to host the artists, local businesses DONATED their airline miles to help fly artists out here.  Then there were the homies.  The kids that all grew up here together, so still here, some flew UP from other places to be here to help, some new friends stationed here with the service, etc - they all helped.  Pitching the tents, setting up the sound and lights, DRIVING the artists back and forth from the airport, hotels, soundchecks etc.  The feeling of family is INCREDIBLE...

The show was awesome.  On the local golf course - they had a big stage and tent set up (since the weather wasnt great) - a huge mud pit for the audience (that the kids were HELLA hyped about lol), various food venders (Smoked salmon crepes and pulled BBQ pork sandwiches were on either sides of our merch booth, TORTURE!), and a big beer garden.  Hiphop played from about 5PM on - various acts from Juneau and Sitka began, showing so much love and the hometown kids came out for them in numbers!  Then Ab Creole showed their normal amazing energy - the audience loved it. 

It was RA's first time performing as Common Market without Sabzi (we are preparing for this because with BS touring for their album, its gonna happen) WD4D did a FRESH jobn filling in as backup DJ for the night - and Khingz and Gabe provided backup during his almost TWO hour set!  The crowd was showing SO much love - it was amazing....

There was a local cat that missed his flight so he was forced to play AFTER Common Market (which after that amazing ending I felt hella bad about) and so the organizers felt so bad about the last 2 headliners kinda getting their headlining spot taken, as well as a end of show merch opp taken kindof - THEY BOUGHT HELLA MERCH from all of us - and gave it away free to the crowd!!  Just throwing CDs and T-shirts into the crowd.  We were all shocked and had never seen anyone do that before! 

So basically - I personally have made it my vow to support everything these cats do each year,  because their generosity and family work ethic just makes them very blessed. 

I forgot my camera - but took a few pics with my phone. 

This is an hour into CM's set - its dark - I am at the merch booth with a lamp - and you can see the stage and crowd under the tent in the background....



This is a pic of one of the village novelty items.  One of the rappers from the night (Phonetic) works in the fur industry and said the guy he works for INVENTED this item and has made SO MUCH money off them!!  I tried to think of WHO I could buy one for -- when Maddi saw it, she said we should make them part of Massline merch. 



Ahahahah so fresh.

OH and the good guys at Homeskillet Records (Nick and George - thanks again for everything) will be updating their website  with photos and video they took last night.  Its not up yet, but here is the site, check back soon...

http://homeskilletrecords.com/
