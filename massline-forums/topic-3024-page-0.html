Maddi and I went to the American Idol concert ....
« on: August 03, 2007, 12:24:57 pm »
And if you make fun I will beat you heh

Nah Blake called - asked if we were going and I told him i wasnt gonna buy tickets to a show that i would have to sit next to a 16 year old girl crying over him lol.  SO he said - I love you guys and Maddi - I have 2 VIP tickets for you, so just come...

SO - RA was gonna be in KY - so it was Maddi and I that went.  Great seats.  The show was aiight -- most of it was cheese and covers in costumes - but Blakes songs and sets were great, so artistic and fresh compared to the others.  I know I am biased but he was dope.

SO then we went to the "meet and greet" backstage afterwards - probably 100 other people with us.  As security "Filed" us in - the "idols" were walking in down the hall through another door.  Now we have known Blake for years, he plays with maddi and has been keeping in great touch with her and us via text during all the Idol tv show madness and now on tour - so Maddi really sees him like a close Uncle.  So she saw him, screamed his name, and before security could grab her, she jets down the hall to him.  He jumps out and yells "MADDI!" and they embrace in the hall, he throws her over his shoulder and carries her in with the other idols - SO FUNNY.

SO we are meeting the various idols - and one of them - Chris S - (the one that looks like Jack Osborne) saw Maddis Common Market shirt and said "Common Market, I know them, Blake gave me their CD!".  Then the other Chris, Chris R - the one that sounds like JT - he saw maddi and said "Hey I know you ...I have seen you and your families pics from Blake!  I fee like I know you!"  Maddi said she was shocked that the idols knew her, so funny.

SO during his set,  Blake represented the 5th element so well - putting little blurbs in between all of his songs - then did a whole set with just beatboxing.  Here is the beatboxing portion from the almost sold out Tacoma Dome...
