http://www.kuow.org/defaultProgram.asp?ID=13230

A radio program on seattle hiphop.  Check KUOW 94.9 at 10am or the link above.

Quote
Seattle hip hop is getting more attention than it has since the days of Sir Mix-a-Lot. Many local artists are trying to do something difficult: Entertain audiences and make them think at the same time. Some artists aren't satisfied with trying to get their politics across in music. They're spending their days teaching and mentoring youth.

How do local MC's define conscious hip hop? Is it full of hope and free of conflict? Seattle's Hip hop clubs have been criticized by some who say they promote violence. Is violence an element of local hip hop?

Tomorrow on Weekday: Your Take on News

Guests:
Gabriel Teodros is one half of the group Abyssinian Creole. His new solo album Lovework was recently at the top of CMJ's hip hop chart. He grew up in Seattle and has been making music since he was 15 years old.

Silas Blak Washington is one half of the group Silent Lambs Project. His new solo album is Silas Sentinel. He's works with the nonprofit Powerful Voices as an educator and advocate for youth in juvenile detention.

Laura "Piece" Kelley-Jahn is a poet, musician and teacher. She was Seattle's Grand Slam champion from 2004-2005. She's the director of the Think Big Foundation, an after school education program, and she teaches writing and hip hop workshops to youth. She is currently a Seattle Arts commissioner.
