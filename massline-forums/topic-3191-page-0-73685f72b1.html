Wednesday August 29 | 7:30 PM
P.O.S.
Sims of Doomtree
Rudy and the Rhetoric
$11
The Vera Project, Seattles premier strictly all-ages venue is excited to welcome one of the Midwests rising stars in MPLS MC P.O.S. All abbreviations aside, P.O.S. has been on touring non-stop behind his stellar 2nd album Audition, which is hot shit. Those who hate on Sluggo but rock The Brother and E&A might find P.OS. to be a happy medium between @mos bitching and Eyedeas polysyllabic flow. P.O.S. grew some teeth while doing time with Minneapolis punk rap crew Doomtree who are sending along one of their own: Sims, to open the show. The first group is Seattles own Rudy and the Rhetoric. R&R have been blowing up all over Fremont and now are ready to take the 206 by storm. Rapper The Rhetoric, has a powerful Aes Rock borrowed flow, while Rudy creates a perfect mix of light horn driven beats, and the dense Def Jux murk.

If youre saving money for Bumbershoot this weekend: the show is only $11, which shouldnt hurt your pocket too much, even if youre ballin on a Wu-Tang budget. Or if you got work in the morning, dont worry this show will be done by 11:30 so you can make it to the office in time tomorrow morning. Get your backpacks, rock the new Aesop on the bus with the headphones threaded from the pocket through your sleeve, and get here to Vera now. I know its late, but you got time.

Other Upcoming Vera shows
8-15 Gabriel Teodros, The Physics, J. Pinder, Beyond Reality
8-19 J. Pinder, Parker Brothaz, Muridian, GMK
8-29 Massive Monkees

Ok. I just got a lot of info about this show and everything should be cleared up now. Initially we had heard from the Sunday Night Sound Session guys that Gabe was confirmed along with the rest of the people who were on there. Then when we heard here that he couldn't play we talked with those guys to see what the deal was and everyone agreed the show should be moved back until November 30th. Sorry about the confusion everyone, particularly Gabriel. What this does mean is that everyone needs to come out and see JP on the 19th in this show:
It's free.
Wednesday September 19 | 7:00 PM
"HollA at Yo' BoY" The First Ever Hip-Hop Veracity!
J. Pinder
Muridian
Parker Brothaz
GMK
$Free .99
$3 Vegan Dinner Available

The Veracity series of shows has been going on now for over three years and this will be the first one in which people have rapped (unless Math and Physics Club started busting some freestyle rhymes at a show I missed). For the inaugural hip-hop Veracity weve assembled an amazing lineup with all of the best of Seattles many hip-hop crews. Headliner J. Pinder records for Sportn Life Records, the Seattle label who celebrated their five year anniversary a few weeks ago with a blowout celebration which featured an inspired performance by J. Pinder himself. Bumbershoot-goers were remiss if they missed his two solo songs during D. Blacks amazing set in the Sky Church. If J. Pinder is the most up and coming artist in all of Seattle hip-hop then Chev, ½ of the rappers in Muridian is probably #2. Chev has earned a reputation the old fashioned way: by being cool with everyone and having a tight flow. He shared the stage with Common Market at Bumbershoot and surely won over a few of their fans who hadnt heard his name yet. Parker Brothaz have an early 90s Compton sound which no one else in Seattle can call their own. The duo from Federal Way are always entertaining live and certainly will not disappoint. Opening the show is GMK, who is certainly still an excellent rapper despite his earlier time slot. GMK will win all of the haters over within one song when he spits venom from his small stature. You would be hard pressed to find a lineup of rappers who have more hype all at the same time, not to mention that this one is free. If you consider yourself a fan of local hip-hop then your cred will take a hit if you dont make it to this one.
