You achieve a good atmosphere, but there are some areas for improvement I think.  One of the problems I notice off the bat for new dawn is that the drums seemed like the levels were solid, but the drum sounds took away from the fluidity of the beat.  This is some blunted in the bomb shelter type samples, but the drums sound like some 99 D-Dot steeze, the only thing you're missing is the triangle and fake scratch FX.  The ambiance is good, but the song itself could use something to keep the music interesting, because it drags a little

Vibin has good atmosphere, and the kick is a good choice.  I also like the fact that you have the snares switch up.  That's the way you should be thinking when you attack tracks like this, that's a good look.  I think this one suffers a bit from the same thing the last one did, and that's poor drum sounds. 

The ideas are definitely there, but I think more hard work and the skill in execution will develop itself.  I would invest in some new drum sounds too.  You can get some good kits off ebay, or if you want to be sleazy with it im sure you can snag them off limewire/etc.  Don't waste your time with the bangin beats kits though, they are el garbagio.
