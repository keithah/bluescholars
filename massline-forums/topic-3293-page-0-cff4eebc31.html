« on: September 18, 2007, 04:35:25 pm »
Just wanted to give everyone one last reminder about the hip-hop Veracity show tomorrow night 9-19 at Vera.

Let me break it down: I'm not plugging this show because Vera will make any money off of it (we lose money), or I will get anything out of it. I'm plugging this show because I truly think that all of these cats are great rappers and deserve to be heard by everyone.

I think that all of these groups are at the same stage in their careers (you know the takeover scene from "The Godfather?" That's these groups in six months. Except I hope none of them murder anyone) and that they all have different fan bases who would like the other groups if they heard them. Just come bringing love for everyone.

I really hope that we can get some people out to support local hip-hop. It doesn't take much. It's free, it's going to be a short show - ten minute breaks or less between groups - and it will be done before your bedtime. It's perfect for any fan of local hip-hop who is seeking out new groups. Let me just say - If I've convinced anyone on here to come to the show: come and find me tomorrow - I'll be show managing, or you can ask someone, I'll know a lot of people there - and I will personally thank you for supporting local hip-hop.

And there'll be lentils for dinner.

Hey there all,
Just a reminder to everyone about the exciting show on Wednesday 9.19 | 7:00 PM. This is the first EVER hip-hop Veracity show - ever. Not to mention that it has the greatest lineup of any hip-hop show in Seattle in 2007. J. Pinder we all know is blowing up, Muridian is on the come up and you know this. Not to mention the opening two groups: Parker Brothaz are the realest group out of the two-oh and I know you heard opener GMK on Street Sounds last night. He came through and rip it up. B-Mello was definitely feelin' his music and even said he was going to host GMK's mixtape. You can call me a liar if you think this isnt the next wave of Seattle-Hop, but you're the one who'll be eating your words when you're going to see these guys next year at the Capitol Hill Block Party, at Bumbershoot, everywhere in one summer. Not to mention on top of all of this: the wonderful Kari is going to be making up some delicious vegan eats which will be selling for only $3. All four of these groups are doing it up big time. It's free. Free. This is the best hip-hop show of the year. Be Here. Hit me up if you have any more questions, but I know you'll be here. One more time: great hip-hop, $3 vegan dinners from Kari (I heard she makes a mean vegan meal, too) - what more can I say? Guru play the beat!

Can you believe this? For Free. I'm scared for the competitors.
Wednesday September 19 | 7:00 PM
"HollA at Yo' BoY" The First Ever Hip-Hop Veracity!
J. Pinder
Parker Brothaz
Muridian
GMK
