KRS-One Marley Marl Hip Hop lives

1st I have to say this is the 1st LP I've checked out by KRS since his self titled lp in 94/95.  Not that I thought the blast master was wack, but after that LP I just wasn't feeling his new ish.

So, I popped this in the deck and at the first listen it was okay.  I was expecting more of that Marley Marl signature sound, but it was dope nonetheless.  Now that I've had time to listen to it a bit more... this ish is dope.  KRS is KRS and to some it may be too preachy, but it's good sh!t and worth a listen.  (plus if you grab it up at Circuit City it has 3 extra songs) 

Was it worth 20 years in the making?  I'm not sure about that, but its still a dope record.  It would be a REAL dope record if it had Shan, Kane and other Juice Crew cats on the LP, but oh well.
