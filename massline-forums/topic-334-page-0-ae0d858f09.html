First off, I implore all to read this LA Times article I am refering to:
http://www.commondreams.org/headlines07/0108-03.htm

Quote
guys like bill gates realize that the people on top are on top because they're assholes, and a big change of heart is quite out of the question.

Some of them may be assholes, others might not be. It's not about individuals and their personalities. It's the legal obligation of CEOs to increase share holder's profits to the best of their ability. This obviously leads to all kinds of corruption and human and natural destruction. But I don't want to say that it's because CEOs are assholes (though they may well be); they do what they do because if they don't, someone else will.



Quote
what good is undone by this?

bill gates has given more than he has taken, and that's all you can ask from anyone. i won't defend the others, though.

Please refer to the article above, and tell me if you still stand by this? But regardless, if foundations, and investors in general, invested primarily in socially responsible businesses/companies/corporations/organizations etc, not only would it shift the economy into a more socially responsible one (though still capitalistic), it would also decrease the need for such philanthropy to begin with. Socially/environmentally companies likely will not turn as much a profit as the Exxons and Walmats out there, so the foundations would in turn have less philanthropic capital to give, but I believe that strengthening companies that treat their workers well and promote a sustainable environment should be the primary concern.

Last year when I was working for a local non-profit, my supervisor told a group of us in a meeting that without a proper perspective on the role of non-profits in the economy, you will basically become a parasite making your wealth because people are poor withouth really changing the reasons why people are poor. The same goes for any philanthropic or charitable cause, including the huge foundations.

I'd rather these foundations didn't exist at all rather than investing in companies that undermine the very causes they claim to support.
