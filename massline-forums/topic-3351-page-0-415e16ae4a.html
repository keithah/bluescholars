Digging through my MySpace inbox about 30 or so pages back, I found this funny message I sent to the Blue Scholars around 2005. Looking back, I realize how ridiculously young and naive I might have sounded, but here goes:

Quote
From: Jawn
Date: Jul 28, 2005 6:22 PM

My name's John, and I hope to learn to DJ (still teaching myself what I can, though I can't always afford the means). My friend Emily Cunningham told me about you guys and what you do, and I'd like to help out. Hit me back.


One of you replied (Geo or Saba?), too:

Quote
thanks for the shout. good luck w/djing it can be a very rewarding activity. how would you be able to help out? ez.

Too bad I deleted my response. I don't even remember how I wanted to "help" exactly. Someone from the Seattle League of P.O.'d Voters described you guys as "raptivists", so I probably thought I could help you on that tip. Or maybe I just wanted to spin records with Sabzi.

Oh boy, enjoy it anyway.
