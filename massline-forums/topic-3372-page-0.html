MusicTech Summit
« on: October 02, 2007, 06:25:29 pm »
On November 2-3, 2007 the Recording Academy PNW Chapter will produce the second annual MusicTech Summit at Experience Music Project in Seattle, WA. The Summit will build on last year�s success by expanding to two days of programming and extend the areas of dialogue and discussion. Already confirmed, keynote speaker Terry McBride will discuss his role as CEO of the Nettwerk Music Group, his focus on advances in digital technology, intellectual property rights and the future of music distribution, as well as his work as the manager of artists like Avril Lavigne, Barenaked Ladies, Dido, Stereophonics, Sarah McLachlan, All Saints and Jamiroquai. Since starting the label in his apartment in 1984, McBride has been responsible for over 100 million records sold world wide.

The rest of the two days will consist of informative panels, workshops, and presentations featuring a wide variety of local and national representatives from top music and technology companies, artists and writers. Areas covered will include but not be limited to:

Mobile Devices and Music Consumption
Digital Sales and Distribution
Social Networking
Performance Royalties in the Digital World
The Changing Face of Record Labels
Building Online Fan Communities
Touring and New Technologies
Emerging Revenue Streams for Artists
Evening networking receptions will follow both days programming
