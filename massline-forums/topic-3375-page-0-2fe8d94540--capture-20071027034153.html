When I first heard about it, I thought it was bull.  Sure enough when I got off work yesterday and turned on NPR they were talking about how this move is a HUGE statement to the current music industry and how its changing. 

BTW on the site you can decide how much you want to pay for the DL and to "official" album wont be released until December.  And this is the best part, its 40 pounds... THAT'S ABOUT $80!  It's almost like they WANT people to dl the LP for free.  Some statement.

(see below)

It will be called In Rainbows and the way it will be released is quite interesting. First off, it will only be available on www.inrainbows.com initially. Secondly, it appears that they are releasing this album without a signed deal with a record company. Of course, many of us have been saying for years that they don't need EMI or any label.

If you go to the site now, you'll notice that there are two options for pre-ordering: Discbox and Download.
