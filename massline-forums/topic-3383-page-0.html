^^^Point taken.  Didn't like 14 Shots?  Well at the time the LP came out it along with a lot of other albums it got lost in the shuffle.  Example: Da King & I's "Contemporary Jeep Music"... DOPE record, but it came out when there was all this really dope stuff dropping, so people either never heard it or just thought it sounded like the other ish that was coming.  I know I wanted to, but never got 14 Shots simply because I wanted to get my copy of "93' Till" or "Stunts, Blunts & Hip Hop" first.  Now looking back and revisiting the album, the ish is dope.  That album along with several other LPs from that time period I've been digging up recently, so I can finally see what I missed and honestly judge them.  There are several albums that when they came out got bad reviews, but now when I listen to them I think they're really dope and wonder why they got dissed so much.   (PMD- Shade Business, Milk- Neva Dated, Das Efx - Straight Up Sewaside, Lil 1/2 Dead's two LPs and... there's more to list, but I just can't think of them at the moment)

I know as for PMD's stuff a lot of the hate he received was due to the Source and other publications siding with Def Squad after the EPMD break up.  Lil 1/2 Dead's hate was probably due to the over saturation of G-Funk and everybody getting sick of all the Chronic/Doggystyle spin-offs. 

On a side note 2 DOPE albums that got wack reviews in "The Source" and were a sign of what was to come with that publication.

Smif N Wessun's -Dah Shinin'
Channel Live's - Station Identification
