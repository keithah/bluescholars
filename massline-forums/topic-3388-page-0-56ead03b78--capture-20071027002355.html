======================
so the unthinkable happens in beautiful Bristish Columbia. Our tour mini van gets broken into!
We were staying in the Ramada inn - granville st , down town Vancouver city, Canada. We checked in around 7pm sat night and left the car in the hotels underground parking which was guarded by a secure steel fence. security cameras, 24 hour patrols with dogs and warden etc... it really felt secure and the area where the hotel is , is ok- east hastings is far far worse.
anyway. we receive a call from reception saying someone has broken into our car around 9pm - just 30 minutes b4 soundcheck!!! iam like WTF. run downstairs and go to the inside carpark and sure enough, our chrysler voyager(mini van) back window has been completely smashed through. so im sweating working out whats taken and whats not.
gone -
a bag of merch(like 50 records, cds, tee shirts),; another bag full of jay (tour driver) clothes; ab rudes bag of shoes and my computer bag are stolen, luckily my computer aint inside as i took it to the room. However there was my mpc 500, g tech pro hard drive , senheiser hd 25 headphones, 4 x ortofon needles S, serato interface unit , cables and sd memory cards, memory stick all inside there.
shitttttttttttttttttttttttttttttttt

so im like - well ' its a smash n grab, they probbly took the stash , ran somewhere and may of dumped some of it since it may be worthless to them (ie the merch, 2nd had clothes etc...)!!' the bag of merch it self weighs like 80 pounds (30 kg) and sure enough after some running around inside the carpark , the thief has abandoned some of his stash on the starewell. he went thro all his stash and left wat he thought he couldnt get rid of
i get all the merch back and Jay the super driver gets most of his clothes back minus all his socks, under pants, backpack and some various articles of clothing plus abs 3 brand new pair of kiks


fuccccccccckkkkkkkkkkk


next to where i find some of our stolen gear is a pile of puke with a distinct piss smell and some hyperdurmic needles. Nice!!! For those who have never been to Van, there are seemingly a larger number of homeless street people than normal. whole communities sleeping in tents and shacks on the street similar to skid row in downtown LA. However we were staying perhaps 2 miles away from that area.

I know , i know wat u gonna say.
' u should take all ya shit out the car every night'

however, the hotel is reputable, the area looks ok and its an underground gated car park with no access unless either the hotel buzz open the steal gate or u somehow a thief slips into the carpark after a car entering.

i call the police. they wont come unless there is a suspect because this happens so often in vancouver.
they say we need to get to look at the cctv footage before they can do anything, otherwise there is no suspect. The receptionist says' we rent the car park from a 3rd party, the cctv cameras are broken,. they say its not their problem. we shouldn't leave stuff in the car and are genarally condercending , unhelpfull and devoid of any emotion. our car was broken into in their rented car park and they are giving me the impression that they really don't care.

so i want to speak to some sort of management to get some sort of clarification

we ring the hotel manager and she says' well its your responsibility'. We were never informed verbally not to keep anything in that car on the voucher u got to put on your dash.'

when we checked in, i was not informed of any of the sort. just that they had secure parking. we were not informed that the security camera's are broken and worst of all, we were not informed that this has happened on a monthly basis. We were not informed drug addicts and wine oes use it as a den of iniquity
Now i accept we should not leave our car with bags unattended even in a mini van with blacked out windows however i do feel we were not informed of the severity of this area. or that car parks have been targeted in this area on a regular basis.
We never met the hotel manager and the police never came - just gave us a crime report number on the phone.

stolen stuff
3 x circa new kiks
various clothes, tee shirts, pants, jackets, socks, north face back pack
g tech 500 gb pro hard drive -$600
akai mpc 500 - $700
senheiser hd 25 headphones - $150
various usb leads, sd cards + mlti compartmental computer bag
serato interface box
3 x ortofon S scratch needles with styli and carrying box - $400

so thats easily like $2000 if not more like $2500 plus on top of that there is excess for windows on mini vans of like $150(ie i pay the first 150 damage!)

the car insurance says i need to return tthe car to seattle to change the vechicle. they have no aggreements with 3rd parties in canada. so i either drve around in rainy cold vancouver for 4 days with no back window and just a plastic sheet covering it or drive 150 minutes south, cross the border and change up the car. problemo - my canadain visa is single entry. which means no return. we are staying in this pimped out 8 bedroom house clinging to the side of the very western peninsula of canada mainland like a 1 hour ferry outside van north.

my travel insurance documents are in the computer bag that was stolen. so iam trying to find out my policy number and where i stand.

the show must go on!!!i need to do a show

i did not feel like doing a show or going out. I want to stick my head in the goround and hope all of this goes away!!!!
that takes a lot of energy to go on, rip it up, smile , jump up and down when inn the back of your head all u can think about is some damn crak head had just jacked ya shit. i didnt have phones or needles to do the show - big up sage for holding me down. big up to the crowd in van cos u were dope and the guy who gave us 125$. u are fam. !!!!
thanks to O dog who drove me up and down east hastings and showed me where all the druggues, crack heads, hoes and unsavoury characters reside. there is this little park on east hastings where we walked around and it felt like we were in a scene of' night of the living dead' . so many jacked up people swaying to and fro with expressionless faces - very zombie esque and its me and o dog walking around . we must of stuck out like a saw thumbs...
this one elderly 'lady' with a terrible hunch back and gravity defying 45 degree lean to the left came up to us and said' hey fellas, im clean for 149 days, ill suck ya dick for some change'. she must of been like 60 years old!!!!

holy moley!

anyway, now after some sleep , the clowd has lifted somewhat. this happened to me on tour before. like 10 years ago in marseille - south of france when our van was broken into and my record box was stolen, pants, socks, shoes and all our jazz fudge tour shirts.... that time, i got back my records cos he dumped them down the road but not the clothes. funnily enougth the next day i saw a lot of people in marseille wearingmy tour tee shirt!!!!!
of course it could of been worse. what happened to mr Lif last year with his bus overturning and losing all his stuff. what happened to J5 in their bus. mark bolan died driving so yeah. Thank God that we are all safe, un harmed and in the grand scheme of things ok. we just lost material things. but even so. i aint jay z getting 500 thousand a show who can just rite off 2000$ worth of gear like that. thats takes a lot of time and hard work to earn.

is the hotel responsible since its their carpark?
well here's where it gets interesting. the recepionist lady, the manager all denied any responsibility. the car park is owned and operated by a third party. when i arrived i was told i can park my car in a 'secure underground carpark that is patrolled 24 hours a day' .
i was never ever told its not their carpark

they give me a coupon to put on the car dash. on this there is the standard disclaimer of the car owner is responsible for all contents of the vechicle and that they are not responsible for any theft or damage.
on the gate of the car park are signs that the carpark is guarded 24 hours a day by k-9 teams. there are ccctv cameras inside and once again - this sliding steel fence which locks the underground car park. by all means it seemed secure and it was patrolled.

i was not informed that the cctv cameras are broken or that in fact the car park is not patrolled

in the stairwell where i found my gear that the thief dumped were a handfull of syringes, some with blood in them in. also loads of cans of bear! so obviously some junkies have got into the car park and are jacking up.

now, it transpires that the same eveing over a span of 12 hours , 3 cars are broken into in the car park. the other 2 cars were valet parked by a ramada inn employee. none of them were informed that the car park is basically a hot bed of crime and drug abuse. even after our car was done over, a driver who came in later was not informed of that. surely if u were running a respectable establishment and offering car parking even from a 3rd party host. u would feel it important to inform customers who want to park that their car park is not safe right now. otherwise if u know already 2 cars have been done in the span of several hours, u are basically leading lambs to the slaughter. since the police have not come and that u as a hotel employee has done nothing to secure the hotel in any additional way.
was it the same guy who struck 3 times?
the hotel manager who refused to come and see us saud the carpark hd been hit regularly. monthly. if thats the case, why wernt the cctv cameras working. u coud capture the culprit. this was not an isolated attack. it was not a snatch and grab in a street.

the owner of the car park also refused to come and see us and disclaims all responsibility.

desperatly searching for headphones and needles but its canadian thanksgiving ......ahahahahaha
need to re buy everything in seattle next wednesday. even tho the canadian dollar is for the first time since the dawn of time, worth more than the US dollar, everything in canada is 20/30% more expensive. Wen the US $ was stronger it meant that i could get bargains here . since i am already down 2000$, i aint got the luxury of giving another couple hunderd in over priced goods...

helllppppp!
