Have we done this already? We need a general junk food thread.

I won't lie, as a kid I ate hella sweets. I mean candy bars, M&Ms, cookies, doughnuts, brownies, you name it. My parents needed to hide that shit to keep it out of my hands. I only started watching my weight midway through junior high, and thankfully managed to drop it all before sophomore year. Now I hardly eat any sweet stuff besides fruits, but I still feel that craving every now and then.

Anyone ever try the doughnuts that Top Food & Drug sells? Man, I could eat a whole box full and then some. Oh, and remember those Little Debbie frosted fudge cakes?? Endless mouth parties! If anyone wants to try real chocolate too, go down to IKEA and check out their European brands. Damn, do those Swedes know their chocolate.
