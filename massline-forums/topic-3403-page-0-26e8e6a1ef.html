Title: Hint: The answer is "zero" - Get on the Washington Bus
Post by: The Washington Bus on October 08, 2007, 01:31:17 pm
Question: How many people of color are on the Tacoma City Council, Tacoma School Board, and Pierce County Council combined?
Is it:
a) Zero
b) One
c) Two
d) Four

Answer: a) Zero. Zilch. Nada. For real. Wackness.

In a city that is 31% non-white, having only white folks in elected posititions is ridiculous, and with your help, the Washington Bus is going to do something about it. This Saturday the Bus (literally, we have a bus) is driving down to T-Town loaded with young people who want to make sure Tacoma has better representation.

We're heading down to canvass in support of Marilyn Strickland, who is running for Tacoma City Council. Marilyn's done a lot of great work with youth mentoring and affordable housing, and currently works for the Tacoma Public Library. She's also a woman of color, and we believe it's vitally important that she be elected.

The Bus departs Triangle Park on Beacon Hill (Beacon Ave and Stevens St) at 10 on Saturday, and returns by 6 that evening. In between, you'll get breakfast, lunch and dinner, prizes, and super secret special guests. It's all free, and you'll be changing Tacoma for the better.

To RSVP or if you have any questions, email washingtonbus@gmail.com, or go to www.washingtonbus.org.
