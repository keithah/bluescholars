FBI monitoring and harassment of Seattle Filipino community organizers
« on: October 06, 2006, 12:03:55 pm »
Please read this. And if you know me, then you know I don't say that often.

First off, I want to say thank you to everyone who has supported me, my work with Blue Scholars and the new Mass Line squad. By supporting the musical element of my life, you have indirectly supported the community work that I do, particularly with Filipino youth in the 206 and the campaigns and educational work we do to highlight the inequities in the Philippines and how it's tied to our communities here. I've been a member of Anakbayan Seattle since 2002 and we are part of Bayan-USA, which is a national alliance of progressive Filipino orgs in the US that supports the National Democratic movement of the Philippines.

There had been rumors around the town for the last few years that our activities were being monitored by the FBI and homeland security. Why? I can think of no good reason, but we all know why. It's COINTELPRO all over again. The political climate in this country has created a heightened air of suspicion of ANY progressive organization - anti-war groups, social justice orgs, pretty much anybody out there calling America out on its contradictions. There's an eye on every corner. Personally, I take it as a sort of compliment that they're so interested in what we do. Means we must be doing something right, or at least something that they feel threatened by.

So here it is. In July, the homie and long-time activist Freedom Siyam (who helped me get organized into Anakbayan) was hit up by FBI agents and questioned about his political leanings and affiliations. Even after his attorney made clear that he was not to be contacted unless a formal investigation was happeneing, the feds continued to hit up Free's ex-wife's parents and even made an unannounced visit to his ex-wife at her home in August, while Free was in the Philippines. This was after the feds were told to NOT contact her because she is still recovering from brain cancer. At this point, it was apparent that not only was Free under investigation, but ALL of our organizations - Anakbayan Seattle, Gabriela Network (now Pinay sa Seattle) and Philippine-US Solidarity Organization (PUSO). Another PUSO member was contacted via phone while she was not home, and other members' names (including my wife) were brought up in the most recent visitation and questioning of Free's mother at her South Seattle home.

We've kept quiet about this for the last couple months as we planned an appropriate response and to see whether the harassment would cease, since the FBI declared that no formal investigation had been launched. Instead, the harassment has intensified. No organizers have been physically hurt, threatened or detained as of now. However, this type of harassment is a clear violation of people's right to peacefully educate, organize and mobilize within their own communities without feeling threatened by their own government.

There are too many eerie parallels between the increasingly hostile political climate for us here and the wave of Human Rights Violations against our counterparts in the Philippines, which ranks second behind Iraq in the highest number of journalists killed. We know that since 9/11, the Philippines has become an even more strategic area of interest to the US as it declared our homeland the "second front in the War on Terror." The US military has mobilized forces in the Philippines for war exercises against the wishes of the Filipino people. And that's just the tip of the tip of the iceberg, yall. And all we're doing is getting folks hip to the BS they don't see.

I don't have a rally cry to end with. I don't know what's going to happen next. All I know is that this is the opening salvo, and that it's time to start the dialogue. I guess I just want to know who's gonna have our back when they kick down the door. "Free Freedom" would make an ill-ass t-shirt, but I'm really not trying to actually have to wear one. I'm just tryin to keep my head up in all this madness and I thought I'd let yall know. So go 'head and let others know, too.

We in this together son, your beef is mine.

------
