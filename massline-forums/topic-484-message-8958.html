-----------------------------------------------------
Technically, I will always be muslim... just because the word muslim means to submit to God.

To boil it down the main reason I quit practicing had to do with my relationship with God.  When I was muslim I found that there was more of a focus on the practice of the religion than with my realtionship with God.  Due to that I was finding my spirituality was hollow.  I loved the people, I loved the culture, I loved salaat, the call to prayer, I loved going to the misjid and sitting with my brothers... but I didn't feel any connection with myself and God.  There was more of a focus on reciting a prayer correctly than with me being able to actually talk to God, (so to speak.)

Now this is just my opinion and just reflects my experiences with Islam.  I'm pretty sure you will find people that feel just the opposite and have a wonderful relationship with God through Islam.  But...
