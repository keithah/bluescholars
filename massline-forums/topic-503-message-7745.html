The Best Kept Secret in the Central District:  HIDMO ERITREAN CUISINE
named "Best Venue for Live African Music" in Seattle Weekly's Best of Seattle 2006. 

HIDMO Eritrean Cuisine
2000 S. Jackson St.
206-329-1534 or 206-303-8536

HIDMO is a great Eritrean/East African restaurant offering delicious food in huge portions at reasonable prices and a full bar - OPEN LATE EVERY DAY. 

Stop by anytime for a meal, a drink or come join us every Friday and Sunday for live African music!  Fun music in a "house-party" atmosphere where the performers let their hair down and try out new things.

Fridays 9:30pm to closing
Sunday 8-10 pm
FREE (donations welcome)
