Community Brunch at the Aaron Dixon Center this Saturday.
« on: December 06, 2006, 07:07:02 pm »
[From Julie C of 206 Zulu]

Peace y'all,
 
Come through to the Community Brunch at the Aaron Dixon Center @ 2111 E Union Street for some food for thought this Saturday. Shouts to dRED.i, BBP 206, Seattle Hip Hop Summit Youth Council for hosting the event!
 
This Saturday, we'll have Davey D joining us as special guest speaker to lead a discussion on how to organize and utilize existing independent media outlets as a tool of community empowerment.
 
Dr. Mako Fitts, assistant sociology professor and Hip Hop Congress faculty advisor at Seattle University will also be speaking about her recent experiences with neighborhood associations and key power players while researching and documenting  the gentrification of the Central District.
 
On that note, I'd also like to take the time in this email to informally introduce everybody to Willie "J.R." Fleming.
 
J.R., Chairman for Hip Hop Congress Community Chapter in Chicago, is a national leader in the fight against gentrification. The Cabrini Green resident is a researcher, organizer, documentarian and website designer for the Coalition to Protect Public Housing. He spoke on community organization and empowerment at this year's National Right to Housing Forum, in Washington DC, and is collaborating with several organizations to create a national network of organizations which advocate for the people's rights in housing.

Earlier this year, he presented testimony before the U.N. Office of the High Commission on Human Rights demanding that "urban cleansing" and the displacement of thousands of black and brown people across the nation is a human rights violation that must be addressed. Fleming also led the march on Right to Return with public housing residents and leaders in New Orleans during the Katrina One Year Memorial. He filmed and edited a gospelmentary titled "Voice of the Voiceless" along with several other short videos on housing that can be found at www.housingisahumanright.com. Before joining the Coalition to Protect Public Housing, Fleming was the director of a gang intervention music program for young men in Cabrini Green.
 
J.R wants to link with the individuals and organizations who are moving or are ready to move on the issue of gentrification in Seattle, and is the process of organizing a team of folks to come out, and link our local struggle with national efforts to combat urban cleansing. His email is iamhiphopchicago@gmail.com
 
Let's keep it moving!
 
-Jules C
