the streets code..
« on: December 06, 2006, 11:52:05 pm »
so me and mom were listening to g-code by geto boys in the car today when the discussion that unearthed this information happened....

my mom (who comes from a long line of quakers) doesn't condone snitching except in cases where the prospective informant/testifier feels that society will benefit from the information they would provide, regardless of the law. for example, when my sister's school gave her and other students a choice between revealing names or being suspended after a bumpin' party, my mom told her she'd pull her out of school entirely before she'd see her betray her friends under such petty circumstances.

my own views are a little bit more extreme. i feel that if another party can solve a problem more effectively/fairly than the institution requesting my testimony, i should withhold it. for example, if a dude raped mike tyson's 10 year old granddaughter, i wouldn't contribute to the case against him because i would know that he was going to get his and it would be more fitting than the sentence that the constitutionally restricted justice system could provide him with.

how do you feel about taking the stand and in what (if any) situations do you think it's a good idea?
