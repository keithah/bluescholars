I believe that there are people in the United States who are ready to kill and die for their beliefs, but realize that that is not an effective from of struggle in our society. The conditions are different in the US than from most countries that have active revolutionary armies, and because of the different conditions, different strategies are needed. At this point in history in the US, a teacher or artist who changes heart and minds with strategic words is more effective at bringing the revolution than an armed millitia fighting armed battles against their enemy (whether it be the government, corporations, etc) who would get slaughtered by military and law enforcement. If armed struggle is strategic for changing the world, then it's revolutionary. But just because you hold a gun and are ready to die for your convitions doesn't mean you're a revolutionary. It's all about context and strategy. What is going to be most effective at achieving your short term and long term goals with an understanding of the concrete conditions before you.
