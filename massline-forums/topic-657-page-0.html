THE END OF ALL AGES SHOWS?
« on: December 20, 2006, 05:58:57 pm »
there is a new bill proposing changes to the State of Washington Liquor laws. There are many horrible ideas in it, the worst being the elimination of all ages shows at venues with alcohol. This means no more all ages shows at the Showbox, Chop Suey, Neumo's, Hell's Kitchen, etc, etc,.

This must be stopped. Mayor Greg Nickels can greatly influence this by opposing it. Make sure he does by writing him at http://www.seattle.gov/mayor/citizen_response.htm and jam The Seattle Music Office at es.keblas@seattle.gov

You also need to write the liquor board - wslcb@liq.wa.gov   

and write your State representatives http://apps.leg.wa.gov/memberemail/Default.aspx - this is VERY important.
more info on the bill will be posted at http://seattlenma.org/news/index.php

this is serious!
