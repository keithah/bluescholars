I'm glad you didn't go see Pan's Labyrinth, at least not with Maddi.  The movie is very good, my wife loved it.  There's a good mix of historical setting (in Spain, just after WWII under Franco, with resistance against him just starting), there's good fantasy with good effects (though I wish there was a little more), with a well written story and well shot picture.  Unfortunately this fairy tale for grown ups is really just that.  There's no sex in the movie, but there are some intense graphic scenes of violence that children really don't need to see.  So, I recommend the movie, just don't think the fantasy elements mean that its okay for the kids.
