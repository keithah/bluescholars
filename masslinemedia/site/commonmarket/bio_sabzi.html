SABZI

Sabzi is brilliance on a track. His skillful manipulation of sounds and samples make him among the most sought-after producers in the Northwest. As a classically trained pianist, Sabzi commands a mastery of the keys that brings melody back to the music. Sabzi's beats are immediately identifiable, ensuring that his fresh imprint on Hiphop will be around for years to come.

As one half of the Blue Scholars Sabzi has garnered a wealth of critical acclaim for his production, including Seattle Weekly’s Album of the Year. On this project he seems poised to take it to the next level.
