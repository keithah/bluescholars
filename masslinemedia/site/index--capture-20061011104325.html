2006-10-10 | Tuesday

It has arrived! Yes, yes y'all. Today marks the national release date of the Common Market Self Titled album, MassLine's first official release. You can either pick it up at finer record stores nationwide or at the Common Market website. So go Refresh your browsers or Push through the nearest record store's Doors until Every Last One of us has a copy of the album.

Yall heard about the Minutemen Protest at Columbia University that left some students beaten and bruised? Damn, didn't classes just get started?

One more thing - the rumors are true. MassLine will be going on a west-of-the-Rockies tour this Fall. Details forthcoming.

2006-10-07 | Saturday

watt do. looks like the forums are crackin! big up to everybody who has registered and contributed to the dialogue. in time we hope that these forums will truly develop to be another extension of the people's media. a place where you can hear opinions and find news you may have missed, or are deliberately hidden from you.

speaking of unheard perspectives, be sure to listen to KEXP 90.3fm this Sunday, October 8 for another special edition of STREET SOUNDS hosted by the MassLine representatives. this weekend's 3-hour block (6PM-9PM PST) is unique because we have decided to dedicate it to the police. our unsung boys in blue. it's a tough job to be out there keeping watch over the block, dealing with all the unpublicized criticism they get, and trying to manage the privilege of using whatever force they feel is necessary to stop what they think may turn into a crime; be it robbery, murder, slowing traffic at a high school football game, or just simply being brown. your favorite cuts from NWA, Jay Dee, and others will provide the musical background for the commentary of live guests and snippets of citizen's stories that will explore the realities of the police in urban communities.

hmm, what else... oh, if you know who zia is, and you happen to see him, wish him a happy birthday, which happened yesterday. may he live long and make many more videos for MassLine.

two.

2006-10-04 | Thursday

People are burning in the Middle East; FBI investigations are driving our neighborhood residents to the edges of paranoia; friends are getting thrown into the Tacoma detention center; the police continue to try and remind us who's in charge.  While remaining conscious of these realities, we will not dwell on the unpleasant things of life.  Life is good.  I'm reminded of that when I step out of the house in the afternoon to see all the beautiful brown, yellow, black and white faces of the Mercer Middle School students as they flood the Columbia & 15th intersection after school.  Stopping traffic.  Their boisterous voices overpowering all the noises of the city for a brief moment in what seems like a foreshadowing of things to come.  Their presence a small and temporary manifestation of their power as a unified mass.  The potential of our people is high and far beyond our comprehension.  Life is good.

This unusually warm and sunny October provides an excellent background to all the new things developing here in the MassLine camp.  If you're reading this, obviously, you see the new site is up.  Enjoy it!  Visit the forums and throw in your two cents.  Discuss all things music and Seattle and otherwise.  Look out for Common Market performing this month, and maybe catch Blue Scholars rockin' an impromptu show where you never expected to see them. =)  Keep an eye out for the new Limited Artist Edition Handstyle shirt, this month designed by peekaboo! and available mid to late October.  This month's shirts will be printed on the increasingly popular ProClub brand, sure to entice the cloth palette of all white/black tee enthusiasts.  

For those folks who've been asking about Gabriel Teodros' solo joint coming out on MassLine, hold tight.  The artwork is finished and the release date is soon come.  Heads aren't ready.

In the meantime, we encourage you to dig up some old records to listen to, go to a Seattle hip hop show, read a book, hug your mom; whatever it may be that reminds you what's good.  Because life is.
one.
saba

 

 

2006-10-04 | Wednesday

Greetings, fam. Welcome to the brand-new masslinemedia.com, currently in its Beta version as we tweak the design and add new links and info. Big things are going down for this humble little record label, beginning with Common Market dropping the re-release of their debut self-titled album nationwide on October 10. The two Blue Scholars albums, Blue Scholars LP (2005) and The Long March EP (2005), are available in select record stores nationwide and online at Bluescholars.com, as well as a new line of Limited Artist Edition T-shirts. Gabriel Teodros' upcoming masterpiece Lovework is slated to drop in January 2007.

THE FORUMS are now open for registration and ready for discussion, including updates on the homie DV One's situation with the SPD. Stay tuned for an upcoming music blog from the homie DJ daps1 and the launching of the MassLine store with a bunch of goodies available here exclusively.

Lastly, the MassLine crew (Gabriel Teodros, Geologic, RA Scion & Sabzi) will be filling in for B-Mello and the Street Sounds crew on KEXP 90.3 this Sunday (10/8) again from 6-9pm.

Northwest stand up!
