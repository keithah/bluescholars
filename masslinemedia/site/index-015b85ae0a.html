2006-11-07 | Tuesday

 


Go pick up a copy of this month's URB Magazine (Perry Farrell/Immortal Technique cover) and peep Common Market's feature and 4.5 star review!

Geo makes beats now. He also draws filthy, filthy pictures. Thanks to the sharp minds of your boys that are Abyssinian Creole, the "new shit" is to replace all statements of "Thas Wassup" with "Filipino Warrior" as inspired by the Geologic drawing that's been hanging in the office for a little over a week now.

To clarify, a phone conversation that went like this:

"ah, thas wassup. but LOST? is that on tonight?"

2006-11-01 | Wednesday

a beautiful, sunny wednesday morning.

big things goin on tonight people. catch your boy Macklemore, one quarter of the elusive group, "Good Medicine," opening for Devin The Dude tonight at the chop. if you go to the show tonight, clap for them, cheer for them, buy their merch, yell nonsensical things at them... but whatever you do, don't throw any of them in the briar patch. that'd just be too much. on the other hand, if you're not feelin the whole hip hop thing, Anthony B, reggae recording artist extraordinaire, will be doin his thing at Neumo's tonight.

hm, Neumo's or Chop Suey? this is the the tough question of the year that so many people just have such a hard time answering.

if you don't catch me at either of the shows above, i'll be eating naan&paneer and watching LOST at the MassLine office with some of my associates.

hollurr. saba

 

2006-10-31 | Tuesday

boo! nah, not "trick-or-treat, smell my feet" boo -- i mean BOO on the governor of oaxaca (pronounced "wa-HA-ka") for ordering the murder of and sustained violence against underpaid teachers in mexico. the educators took to the streets nearly five months ago calling for better wages; the governor soon demanded that the military remove the group heading-up the protest; the peaceful demonstration recently turned into a mass movement (the "people's popular assembly") to oust the governor; yeah, head-crack, head-crack. yesterday around town i saw a few folks "demonstrating," but aside from that i'm rather surprised at how little reaction there is to such an attrition. teachers are truly the salt of the earth, fam -- if nothing else i ask you keep them in your thoughts and prayers as they offer up the ultimate sacrifice in the path of this cause.

peace to you and yours...

R

2006-10-16 | Monday

Peace to the good folks who witnessed Common Market hold it down at The Paramount last friday. If you missed it, you can catch CM at the Showbox this Thursday October 19th headlining the Seattle Art Museum benefit with souls sista Choklate (who performs with Blue Scholars on November 22 @ The Showbox).

2006-10-12 | Thursday

Warm militant greetings with a smile. Geo here with an update.

More and more folks are hitting us up on some "When's Gabe's Lovework album going to drop?" Easy, star. The album artwork is done - a masterpiece from Overstand artist Darvin Vida and designer Wilmer Galindo (who designed the Blue Scholars The Long March EP and Common Market self-titled album art). The production on the record is incredible, with 206 vet Amos Miller handling the bulk of the production, with additional cuts produced by Kitone, Moka Only and ya boy Sabzi!

But instead of sitting and waiting for Lovework, get your GT fix with his previous musical body of work. If you haven't yet, go get a copy of Abyssinian Creole's 2005 album Sexy Beast, featuring Gabriel Teodros with counterpart Khingz Makoma (aka Khalil Crisis). Gabriel is also posting up exclusive and unreleased tracks on his myspace page every week. Free downloads for the people!

Also, pick up this week's copy of The Stranger and check out page 13 for an article on the current FBI harassment and monitoring of the Seattle Filipino activist community that I work with, including the homie/teacher/organizer Freedom Allah Siyam and my family. The support has been beautiful, the struggle continues, the music won't stop.
