get your gear, get laced up

2007-03-20 | Tuesday

Greetings, humans! 

Massline emcee extraordinaire Gabriel Teodros and I performed at Westlake Center in downtown Seattle, yesterday March 19, amidst a mixture of youth, veterans, media and community organizers to protest the illegal war in Iraq. It was good to see a lot of youth close to the stage, with one guy in particular yelling "that's bullshit!" every 20 minutes. I think that should be the new phrase…so feel free to say it while other people are talking about imperialism, racism, classism, Hurricane Katrina, Snickers commercials, all-over print hoodies, 835+ murders in the Philippines, 41 cent postal stamps, 50 Cent, Bryant and Greg Gumbel, Bill Buckner, blood diamonds and hidden bank fees, amongst other things.

 

Anyway, Gabe and I were interviewed by the Seattle Times but didn't make the article, nor did we make it on TV (to my knowledge). Wassup with that? We're looking for our big break…it's also so I can afford a watch to keep track of how many minutes of fame I've already used up. Big ups to the Troops Home Now Coalition for holding down Westlake and for having inspiring high school youth up there offer their thoughts with enthusiasm.

 

I, like you (yes you) should be enthused about this Friday's Stop the Killings show at the newly built Vera Project in Seattle Center. Your boy Prometheus Brown will headline, with Kiwi of the Native Guns as one of the many guest performers. The show is going to offer a whole lot more, so come through and show your support…it's needed now more than ever. Check out the Massline forums for the info!

 

Peace
