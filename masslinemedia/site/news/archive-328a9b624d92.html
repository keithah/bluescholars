2007-11-20 | Tuesday

"Blue Scholars' MTVU video takes a ride through Seattle"

Check out the Blue Scholars' latest video at MTVU and read up on it in the Seattle Times! Article reposted below:


Seattle icons such as the Space Needle and Pike Place Market are as familiar to national TV viewers as David Letterman and Oprah. Leave it to the against-the-mainstream Blue Scholars to take viewers on a journey through the underside of Seattle. "Joe Metro," a video that made its debut on MTVU on Monday, is a trip through Rainier Beach, Columbia City and the University District.

The gritty video follows lyricist/rapper Geologic's No. 48 bus ride — with DJ/producer Sabzi making a cameo as the bus driver.

Fitting that the video should debut on the college-oriented MTVU (online at www.mtvu.com), as Geo is one of Seattle's brightest street professors. Here he takes viewers and listeners to school on modern socioeconomics, sketching the minority experience with lines like, "Proletariats and wayward sons / old Filipino men / speaking in their native tongues."

Zia Mohajerjasbi — Sabzi's brother — directed the illustration of Geo's ponderings on people of color, rolling en masse.

Next up for Blue Scholars: a five-night run at Neumo's, dubbed "The Program," Dec. 18-22.

For more details: www.bluescholars.com

2007-11-06 | Tuesday

i'm in San Francisco getting ready for these shows with Nefasha Ayer... check it out! -- gtzi http://www.myspace.com/nefashaayer

The Red Poppy Art House Presents:

NEFASHA AYER: Excerpts from the Space of In-between
Two nights! Friday, November 9th and Saturday, November 10th

Drawing inspiration from jazz, blues and traditional Ethiopian melodies and rhythms, Nefasha Ayer (loosely translated from Amharic as “the wind that travels”) is an in-progress body of music exploring the most essential qualities shared among individuals worldwide whose identity no longer fits within the boundaries of one country, culture or tradition. Through its tones and colors, poetic texts and trans-cultural melodic scores, Nefasha Ayer joins the continents of Africa, South Asia and America to produce a uniquely San Franciscan music, born from present-day realities of life at the crossroads.

This weekend’s shows feature in-progress selections from Nefasha Ayer, with Ethiopian-born vocalist and Art House Director Meklit Hadero, Art House Founder/Director and guitarist Todd T. Brown, Carnatic saxophonist Prasant Radhakrishnan (of VidyA), award-winning poet Michael Warr, Ethiopian MC Gabriel Teodros (of Abyssinian Creole), bassist/flautist Eliyahu Sills (of Qadim), and percussionist Mohini Rustagi (of Ambika). On Friday night, we'll be joined by bassist Miles Jay (formerly of Yousou N'Dour).

In April, the Red Poppy Art House received a grant from the San Francisco Foundation and the Fund for Artists commissioning this work.


For more information: www.redpoppyarthouse.org/concerts

$15 suggested donation. Friday and Saturday: Doors open at 7:30 pm. Show at 8:00 pm.

Red Poppy Art House
2698 Folsom St @ 23rd
San Francisco, CA 94110
