2007-01-20 | Saturday

chunk a deuce, pahtnah!  take a lint roller to that musty-ass members only jacket and get suited up for tonight's medicinal goodness at the chop.  fuck what the Times say - with all the talent this bill has to offer i'm bettin' the line backs up to AT LEAST piecora's.   and when the guns is in the house, oh my god...

anybody catch ya bwoy sabzi last night on the 11 o'clock news?  evidently he's been part-timing at huling bros.  nah -- for real though, he was doin' the damn thing for the sake of the kids (if nothing more) at EMP, schoolin' young bucks on the ways of the come-up.  good on ya, cousin.

i'm becoming awfully suspicious of VH1's white rapper show.  not for any reason steeped in half-hearted socio-political nonsense, but because ego trip's got too much hood cred to really let this thing come off like another season of the real world.  i'm bettin' a cup of joe bar's finest that the one crowned king of caucasians is in for a big (book of) surprise when they find out THERE IS NO MONEY.  showin' these scrappers what it's like to be hustled, humiliated and commodified  would be a lesson worth more than millions.  now that's KILLER!!

see y'all in the sewer -- i mean the suey.

RA



2007-01-18 | Thursday

?good watts.

it's slightly warmer. the two foot icicles hanging from my house have now liquidated themselves and returned to the earth from whence they came.

ahem.

this weekend is gonna be full of activity.

Grammy Career Day 2007 tomorrow at the EMP should prove to be an interesting event. i'll be there facilitating a "How I Make Beats" workshop around lunchtime with a projector hooked up to the computer.

later that day, if you happen to be in Corvallis, OR in the evening, you'll be able to catch the Native Guns and Blue Scholars doin a little somethin somethin at Oregon State University.

saturday, i'll be at my uncle's house in Renton, WA kickin it with fam in honor of my cousin visiting from Beijing. that's not really a public event, so i'm not sure why i'm bolding the pronouns, but don't trip cuz at that same time, your boys Good Medicine will be rocking the stage with Native Guns and a plethora of local talent at Chop Suey. GO TO THAT.

for those who decided to watch American Idol (Seattle auditions) last night, you might have noticed that, as usual, the mainstream media has an affinity for bad-mouthing our beloved Town. "Seattle ain't sh**!" was the prevailing theme of the show. i personally take offense to this, although i'm not particularly surprised. oh well.



some of you may have recognized former Seattle SCIONtific Records Beat Box Competition champion BShorty (alias Blake Lewis) holdin it down claimin "Emerald City!" throughout his audition. good luck brosef. do yo' thing.

-sabarillo-



2007-01-17 | Wednesday

as shalom ashanti, company.  i'm hoping this reprieve from frozen precipitation brings warmth to your heart as well as your block...

speakin' of heat - big shouts out to the local beatmakers who keep blessin' me with the goodness.  thanks to flowside, MTK, infi-note, khazma, Cin7, seance and a full clip of others.  nearly 6 weeks after takin' one helluva loss in san fran, i've re-upped somethin' marvelous.  the pen is hot, y'all - keep both eyes open for compilations and mixtapes set to drop in the very near future...

and speakin' of the future - as i understand it, in 2009 women will inexplicably become completely infertile.  word up - not a single child will be born for 18 years causing the world (but particularly england) to be stricken with fear, distress and depression.  if you think that's disturbing, you should see how first-world immigration policies evolve.  if you wanna see more - get to a theater near you and peep children of men - it's that shit!

and speaking of shit - check for tonight's episode of american idol - it's the town's night to clown.  i'm watchin' for the sake of my dude b-shorty - rumor has it his bags are packed for hollywood.  take one for the team, fam...

be good.

RA



2007-01-16 | Tuesday

This Saturday ya'll... Native Guns are back in Seattle for the first time since the MassLine launch party last summer, along with only the 2nd Good Medicine performance ever!  And did I hear you say something about Yirim Seck?  check his crew Pyrate Radio's video here... and get those tickets fast at Ticketweb.com 

Lovework has been getting more amazing reviews left & right... along with being in URB Magazine this month, check out what Shotgun Reviews had to say, they'll have an interview up soon as well.  The national release date is set for February 27th, but you get your first chance pick up the album at the Chop Suey on February 24th. 

Khingz Makoma of Abyssinian Creole has been mad busy lately as well, after just releasing the amazing Hillionaire Boys Club MxTape only 2 months ago, he just finished recording for Khingz County, a new mx-tape featuring the South End's finest lyricist rockin beats from the late-great J Dilla.  Lookout for both projects at an Ab Creole show near you... your mom loves em.

Other then that, we've just been enjoying good food at the Hidmo, driving carefully over this ice, and i've been completely inspired by the future of hiphop... peace to Canary Sing, Muridian, Language Artz and El Dia.  Ya'll make me proud to represent Seattle.

and some of that art ya'll have been posting in the forums is dooope.

peace fam
-- gtzi
