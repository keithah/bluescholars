2006-12-12 | Tuesday

so here we are in Albuquerque.  the "Mexico" part of "New Mexico" is somewhat misleading to me in terms of weather right now.

it is cold as S$*#!

or maybe i'm just a foo for thinkin that Mexico is supposed to be warm.  yea, that's probably it.

after visiting the Baha'i Center and catching up with reknown violinist Farzhad (we rocked a show together at the Premier a while back) RA and I decided to kill some time yesterday evening on our day off by seeing Apocalypto. 

still dunno what to think.  it wasn't long ago that "Yucatec Maya" was a forbidden language like most indigenous tounges of the North American continent during the age of boarding schools and re-education programs.  now it's featured in the newest Mel Gibson film and is possibly about to be the new hawtschitt like Aramaic was when the Passion of Christ dropped.

either way, i thought the film was a good watch.  RA got a little bored.  this afternoon before soundcheck, we're gonna go check out Babel.

if you happen to be in the 505 this evening, come check out the 2K7 tour at the Sunshine Theater on Central Ave.  holla holla holla.

saba

2006-12-10 | Sunday



aspen is an interesting place.

i don't think i've ever been to a place with such a high concentration of wealth.  we met more australians and south afrikaaners at the club than any other citizens, and i heard more european languages spoken in the street than english;  all here in the heart of colorado! 

some social investigation informed us that the cost of living is quite high ($1600/mo. for a studio apartment is considered "a steal"), everything--with the exception of the club--seems to close at about 7pm, most of the workers that labor in aspen commute from the "worker town" about an hour out of the city (just like the good old feudal days!), and the average ticket price of the club we played at last night is something like $300 bucks a show. 

when we said "hey! that's expensive!"  emily, our host for the evening, said "no, expensive is when we do special events and charge $1200 a head to come see the show; we'll still sell out tho."  we're talkin about a regular old club, folks.  like chop suey, but with stools and woodgrain.

nevertheless, the folks in aspen were quite live.  they showed lots of love to us and all the other players on the tour.  we look forward to coming back.

also got a chance to catch up with the folks that do Akomplice clothing.  i did not know these doods were from colorado.  they had a hard time blending in to all the ski bunnies in the club what with their extra clean all over hoodies and gold foil shirts.  big up fellas, keep doin your thing and continue to serve up the hottschitt.

we'll be in boulder tonight, coincidentally with your boy Macklemore who's got a show across the street from ours.  before the show, we'll likely just sip some Beast and talk about how we can't wait to get back to Seattle.



2006-12-09 | Saturday

what it gwan?

Tonight at Youngstown Cultural Arts Center, TSB presents... "So Fresh, So Clean" featuring The C.R. Avery Band, Abyssinian Creole, YouthSpeaks, the Service Band, Roberto Carlos Ascalon, Jeromeskee and the ArtsCorps All-City Breakers... 7:30 pm, and thats an all-ages show ya'll

and tomorrow, you don't want to miss the Seattle screening of the documentary Betrayal Of Democracy: Ethiopia happening at the Langston Hughes Performing Arts Center

This film gives an insider's view of politics in a Third World country where a system of tyranny, corruption and oppression is being held in place by a complex assortment of players, ranging from Ethiopians themselves to those in the international community, all who influence the internal affairs of the second most populous country in Africa, not always with the best interests of the Ethiopian people in mind.


In an atmosphere where information is tightly controlled, now you can discover what is actually going on behind the scenes as the people of Ethiopia speak out from the dark places of oppression, bringing the lens of truth to the public about the rampant human rights abuses that go on in the secret places of this regime while captivating the world with an illusion of democracy.


There will be 3 showings, 11:30 am, 1:30 am, and 3:00 pm and tickets are $15.  More information can be found at http://www.anuakjustice.org
