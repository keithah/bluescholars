2007-06-28 | Thursday

NY is still hot. We came and brought the rain. In the form of a massive thunderstorm. Show review and pictures coming soon, but first... today we stand in solidarity with our folks back in Seattle locked in a struggle with the SPD.

Our communities have been hollering for years about police brutality, from higher profile cases like Robert Thomas Sr.'s murder and the WTO protests to the many unreported cases of youth of color being harassed and attacked by the "anti-gang" unit. Recently they've attacked people in the local hip-hop community (DV-One, Rajnii Eddins, Fatal Lucciano and others) and now cases such as George Patterson's drug arrest and Maikoiyo Alley-Barnes' (pictured above) brutal beating are putting the SPD on an even hotter seat. Now think of all the cases that don't make the headlines. It's nothing new to us, but it has become apparent to the general public that THE POLICE NEED TO BE HELD ACCOUNTABLE for their actions. If you feel the same way, stand up TODAY!
