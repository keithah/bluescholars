2007-04-27 | Friday

 

Big up to the comrade Bambu (Native Guns) on the release of his new album I Scream Bars for the Children. This Sunday (4/29), Sabzi and I head down to Los Angeles to celebrate the album release with a Block Party in Historic Filipinotown featuring Ise Lyfe, The Attik, Eye A Sage, The Committee and special guests. And of course, Bam himself with DJ Phatrick.

If you can't make it to the show, you can order the album online at www.beatrock.com. The album is hot and the brotha reps hard for Pinoys and Pinays everwhere. Plus, I got a verse on that joint. Cop it!

See you in LA!

2007-04-26 | Thursday

Hello world. Your comrade Geo is back on the blog. Finally back home from three school shows in three days on the East Coast. From Peddie High to Columbia University to Lehigh and back to NYC, it's good to see young students of color holding it down on their respective campuses. Also helps to have down-ass teachers (right. Mr. Hiebert?). Peace to Ryan (Heiroku) at Columbia who we learned was one of the students who took part in last October's Minutemen Project protest (posted in the Forums).

Also, we got a chance to chill with Noah at WFMU to talk about the upcoming album (June 12!), Seattle hip-hop and even bust a quick semi-freestyle. You can check the audio at wfmu.org (scroll down to April 18, 2007). Keep listener-supported radio alive!

Lastly, big up to Ralph from Kicksclusive for hookin us up with some of the latest Kilo Goods and Rocksmith gear and making Sabzi a very happy man (I'm good with rockin' all-over neon and pastel colors - thanks tho homies).

I love the fresh Northwest air. Almost makes the constant cloud cover worth it.

Easy
Geo



2007-04-23 | Monday

peace, kinfolk.  one week after the tragic event to be known henceforth as the "VT Massacre," i trust your hearts and minds have taken some time to heal.   and in the process of grieving i hope your reflections have led you to the understanding that no act of violence is ever senseless, but systemic.   may we begin to think critically about the individual role we play in this increasingly aggressive society, and work diligently to spread among our compatriots a sense of honor, love and trust in one another; we truly are the keepers of our brothers.

2007-04-13 | Friday

"No Label (Esma Remix)" the first single from Gabriel Teodros' solo masterpiece, Lovework, will be made into a music video this weekend.

Saturday, April 14th, 2007, MassLine invites you to participate in the production of this video at Beacon Hill Elementary School, beginning at 1:00pm. with a song that serves to represent the community that our homie GT came up in, it is only appropriate that the community is there. come out, show your face and support!

make sure you arrive on time!

when you come through, be sure to have the video release form signed and ready to give to the crew. this is absolutely necessary, as the video will be broadcast on national television when it is complete.
