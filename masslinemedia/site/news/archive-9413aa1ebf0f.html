2007-10-04 | Thursday

Family,

I'm proud to announce that the first ever Massline short film MANOJ, directed by Zia Mohajerjasbi and written by Hari Kondabolu, will be playing TWICE in the next couple of days in film festivals in Seattle.

When, you ask? I'll tell you:

TODAY, Oct. 4th, 7pm in the 4th Annual Independent South Asian Film Festival. Broadway Performance Hall, 1625 Broadway Ave. Seattle, WA, 98122. TICKETS FREE!!!

It will precede the film, Anuranan.
http://isaff.tasveer.org/2007/program.php?id=2#ev_16


SUNDAY, Oct. 7th, 9:15 pm in the NWFF's 10th Annual Local Sightings Film Festival. Northwest Film Forum, 1515 12th Ave, Seattle WA 98122. Tickets $8

It will be played in the "Points of View" section.
http://www.nwfilmforum.org/localsightings/schedule.php

Much love,

The aforementioned Hari Kondabolu
