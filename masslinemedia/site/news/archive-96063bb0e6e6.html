2007-06-10 | Sunday


Geo and new Blue Scholar fan Alexander chillin at South Seattle Community College last night. Photo by CGC.


Last night, Blue Scholars, along with yours truly delivering the cuts, performed at LELO's annual Domingo, Viernes and Jefferson Awards Dinner at South Seattle Community College. Lt. Ehren Watada, also on hand to receive honors, introduced Blue Scholars to the packed house. The "Back Home" video was shown right before the guys went through a set of all new material from the Bayani album, in stores everywhere on June 12. Shout out to LELO, Ehren, the Watada family, all working class folks and their families, and your mom's mom!

Blue Scholars, just fresh off the Massline west coast tour, stay busy today with another performance at the Seattle Center. Come check them out and other quality acts (hip hop and non) at the 2007 Pagdiriwang Philippine Festival's Diwa Na Kalayaan showcase, starting around 1pm. Big up to all the organizers and Battalion Entertainment.

See you at the in-stores!
Peace
