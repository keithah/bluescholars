2007-10-04 | Thursday



Wassupers? Today, DJ Daps1 and Geologic would like to take some blog space and perform some classic hip hop poses. There are dozens more, we're sure, but we hope that these poses can help out you, fellow hip hopper, in your quest to find the right pose for your album cover/myspace. Above is your basic hip hop duo and "let em know you're here, world" pose.



Classic crouch-down pose reminds me of Gangstarr's "Hard to Earn" LP cover. And no, we don't do this well cuz we're "asian". Lollercopter.



Every hip hop artist has to have that "hey look at my back" shot.



"Tougher than Leather" pose. See how Geo's lookin one way and i'm lookin at the camera? That's not even rehearsed, son!



The East Coast "Reachin' Out" shot. Reachin out to who(m) though?



The West Coast swag pose. The snarl is extra.



The 1981 high five pose. Usually there's 30 other people around you for this shot. Others can perform the crouch-down pose to make this shot a filthy one.



The "conscious rapper" pose, complete with pointing at the dome and rubbing of the third eye.



And finally, the "Doin' in the park, doin' it after dark" pose at Rockcreek Park, WA DC (yes, that very same Rockcreek Park). That's Kiwi in the center. And we managed to wear the same hats in the shot (good job).

Good luck! And don't forget to post a "NEW PICS!" bulletin on myspace when you do so.

Peace
daps1



2007-10-04 | Thursday

a while ago we started talking about what to do for the New Year. after debating about where to play a show and who to put on it, we concluded that there was just way too much goin on in the town to limit an end-of-the-year celebration to just a couple of acts on the night of Dec 31st. besides, i'm sick of working on New Year's Eve!



soooo, we decided to do a series of shows at a good intimate venue and put the whole Town on and call it The Program. it goes as follows:
