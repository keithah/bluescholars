2007-08-11 | Saturday

tuesday, we had the opportunity to hit San Diego on many fronts. our folks Kid John and Zar from pnoyapparel hosted us and set up a few events for Blue Scholars to rock at.





our first stop was AMFM Skate Shop. located on Garnet Avenue, AMFM is San Diego's premier boutique skate shop and carrier of fine goods from Stussy, Orisue, and other brands coveted by those who consider themselves the bastions of coolness.



there, we had a chance to meet Cathy and Jay (or was it Ray?) who held the shop down in the daytime.

the homie Will, aka DJ Ill Will came thru and set up a little sound system for the instore. as soon as the speakers and turntables got set up, the googly-eyed and drooling bedroom-DJ teenagers crowded around and stared at the decks and mixers; very much like how a kid stares at purple drink, or how an ethnic stereotype stares at their food of choice.



oh right, we're in california. skratch DJ mecca. so we got together and cut it up like it was 1999. literally. peace to Jay/Ray and young Ray (this other guy was actually named Ray) for holdin it down. keep doin your thing maynes.

then we did a show.



then we posed for a picture.



check out Bee wearing the new Artist Edition shirt scheduled for release in the Fall. and no, that's not a Persian Max Headroom, that's me.

i was informed that The Armory, a legendary hip hop store in San Diego, used to be just down the street. then i was informed to stop calling San Diego, "San Diego" and call it "Day-go" instead or else i'd be kicked in the kneecap repeatedly.

talks of The Armory then reminded me of this little gem,


a flick of Valentino's Mexican Restaurant mascot that was taken awhile back when we were in Day-go before. haha, that's supposed to be a cactus.

after AMFM, we went with Zar to get Mexican food and headed out to Kava Lounge for night time show. there, i found out that Mike, co-owner of AMFM, in addition to managing a skate team, also rapped. what's more, is he rapped with a band. they held it down.



Kava Lounge kinda reminded me of LoFi down by the freeway in Seattle. except that Kava Lounge had a stage. LoFi doesn't have a stage. Kava did. that was mainly why i think i liked Kava Lounge better than LoFi. you know, the stage thing. there were like 300 people there, some local opening acts, and an Orisue clothing giveaway that never ended. good work pnoyappco!

after the show, we all hi-fived each other for a job well done, kinda like the Hi-Fivin' White Guys from Almost Live except without the white guys. then we went to eat at Denny's.



Denny's was ok. not great. i think the best part was when Geo suggested that we play in the Food Olympics right as our check came.

invented by frat boys in the mid-seventies, the Food Olympics began as a series of games incorporated into the hazing rituals employed during rush. it eventually became a cultural pastime among stoners and suburbanites and went on to find a home in the routines of tokenized comedians of color who make fun of white people.



the game chosen for this night involved the creation of a dish from anything edible on the table. Bee, not being a fan of pork, grabbed all the sausage and bacon, combined it with hashbrowns and eggs, and slid it between two pancakes to make some kind of rancid homemade Denny's McGriddle. a bunch of people said "ew."

then this poor bastard ate it.


more people said ew, and he didn't have to pay.
