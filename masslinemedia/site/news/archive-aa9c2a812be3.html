2007-07-13 | Friday

DJ Q-Bert was and still is a source of pride for any young Filipino hip-hop head growing up in the 90's. My boys Eddie, Hoven, Fed, Randy and a bunch of folkers in B-Town traded VHS tapes of the DMC battles, which inspired me, like many young Pinoys at the time, to want to become a DJ. Before I ever touched a mic, I called myself DJ Geologic, putting out mixtapes made with two belt-drive turntables and a 2.5-foot wide mixer loaned to me by the homie Danhil. After a few classic mixtapes such as Mission: Illogical and Ill-dependence Day (1996) (anybody in Bremerton still have those joints?) I started dropping verses over instrumentals on these tapes. That's where it all started. The DJ becoming the MC, just like the evolution of hip-hop itself.

So imagine my excitement as a grown man to be able to see Q-Bert come through town tonight at Neumo's to (hopefully) remind us why he's the baddest turntable technician of all time. Even better? The 206's own up-and-coming crew The Physics (Thig Natural and Justo) will be opening up. And Massive Monkees? And Big World Breaks? Stop playin! I'll be in the crowd pretending it's 1995 again.
