2007-06-05 | Tuesday

pictured: Evan Flory-Barns, Ahamefule Oluo, D'Vonne Lewis, Josh Rawlings

DON'T MISS THE NEW "MASSLINE PRESENTS" EVENT!!!

Beautiful things are happening in Seattle and it is time for a celebration of what our great city has to offer. For 2 nights at the Langston Hughes Center for Performing Arts we are pulling out all of the stops and presenting The New Seattle Orchestra, a 20+ piece orchestra consisting of some of the most innovative performers in Seattle including Paul Rucker, Izaak Mills, Owour Arunga, Dylan Rieck, D'Vonne Lewis, Jacques Willis, Eric Peters, Beth Fleenor and many others performing the works of Evan Flory-Barnes and Ahamefule J. Oluo with special guests of RA Scion of Common Market and Gabriel Teodros who will actually be performing with the orchestra. Comedian Hari Kondabolu and Sabzi (Blue Scholars, Common Market) will be hosting this event. This will be a night to remember!


The New Seattle Orchestra
Featuring: The Modern Orchestral Works of Evan Flory-Barnes and Ahamefule J. Oluo w/special guests Gabriel Teodros and RA Scion of Common Market.

Langston Hughes Performing Arts Center
104 17th Ave S. Seattle, WA 98144

FRIDAY / June 15th &
SATURDAY /June 16th
8pm All Ages
$15 presale / $18 door

2007-06-02 | Saturday

Peace. It's June already? Daaamn. 

5300+ miles on the odometer. It's day 18 of the tour and we finally found an internet spot at the Bend, Oregon public library. Saba just hipped yall with the sessions we had with some dope bay area youth. Thanks for riding with us!

 

(Thanks to daps1 for the updates in our absence! LOST Flash Forwards - ahhh shit!)  

 

The first ever MASSLINE Tour is about to come to an end. So that means it's shoutout time. For the fam who we left to go and rep for, who broke bread with us on the last night before departure, who shouldered our tasks back home and kept us updated and connected - we couldn't do this without you. 

 

Shoutout to all the NW-erners in each and every last city we hit - keep carrying the torch for the region wherever you're at! And all the folks who put us up in their cribs, cooked us meals, guided us through traffic, came out to the shows, gave us free coffee (peace to Alma in SD!), gave us free other unmentionable stuff, hit up the in-stores, venue staff, all the folks I've reconnected with after a hot minute - THANK YOU. Maraming salamat sa inyong lahat with rev love and fire for the people. 

 

Stay tuned for video and audio of the trip. Oh, and our new music video for "Back Home" filmed by Zia Mohajerjasbi!
