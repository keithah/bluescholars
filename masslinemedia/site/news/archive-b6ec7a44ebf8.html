2007-01-29 | Monday

what a beautiful weekend, in so many ways.  on saturday, masses of northwest folkers joined a nation-wide demonstration in protest of US involvement in iraq.   the message to military recruiters was delivered loud and clear as the congregation assembled at the station's front door, like what?!?  i send a special thank you to all in attendance for speaking in my absence - i love your work.  aiyo daps, post some pics, sun.

after the rally, a good grip of the company rolled south to attend church at hell's kitchen.  news cameras from MTV, al jazeera and the Japanes News Channel were intently focused on the evening's key note speaker, lt. ehren watada.  he delivered a stirring speech, insisting that, if convicted and sentenced, his time served will have been a worthy sacrifice.  that's a good look, my friend.  another special thank you to the staff at hell's kitchen and to the entire 253 for the support.  aiyo daps, post some pics, sun.

this weekend also marked the grand opening of silver platters in lower queen anne.  for those of you who not familiar, the locally-owned mom-and-pop record shop moved into the house that tower built.  i see the move as a remarkable success for independent music stores, in general.  i highly encourage you all to purchase your copy of lovework at a silver platters near you.

lastly, i capped off the weekend at a send-off party for the homie b-shorty (soon to be known to the entire fox-watching population as "blake lewis").  until watching him perform for a packed house at nectar, i had forgotten just how brilliant this kid is.  we wish you much luck, much love, and the strength of a thousand soldiers, cousin -- you're gonna need it.

be good, family...

RA



2007-01-25 | Thursday

* The military cost of 2.5 days of in Iraq would have provided college tuition for 463,000 low income students

* The total war cost of $360 Billion would have paid for 17 Million full-ride scholarships to college

This aint no theory or politics, just raw facts. And the fact is it's about to be FOUR YEARS since the illegal invasion and occupation of Iraq and almost FIVE YEARS since military occupation of Afghanistan and the Philippines, all under the guise of "war on terrorism." With over 3,000 US casualites (and 650,000 Iraqi civilian casualties), Bush, Jr. is pledging 20,000 more troops to this madness. We need more folks to stand up like Lieutenant Ehren Watada, who faces a February 5 court-martial for refusing to deploy.

We salute Lt. Watada for calling the war's legality and morality into question and refusing to follow unjust orders. As his court martial approaches, the urgency to bring the troops back home increases daily. Saturday, January 27 marks a nationwide day of protest with a massive march on Washington. In solidarity, Seattle will be holding a rally and march at 1pm in the Central District. Lt. Watada and other guest speakers will be speaking at the Seattle rally.

Later that evening, the entire Massline Fam (Blue Scholars, Common Market, Gabriel Teodros w/ Khingz Makoma and DJ daps1) will be rocking two shows (5pm - all-ages, 9pm - 21+) in Tacoma (Hell's Kitchen) in support of Lt. Watada's speaking campaign against the war. Special guest speakers, including Lt. Watada himself and the Bayan-USAhomie Rachel Cendana will be at the show as well. See you there! 2007-01-23 | Tuesday

big fat news for my big fat MassLine fam.

MassLine is sponsoring its first ever comedy show produced by our good friend Hari Kondabolu. this Friday, Jan. 26th at the Langston Hughes Performing Arts Center will be a night of social commentary mixed with absolute hilarity starring Ahamefule Oluo, Dan Moore, and Hari Kondabolu. the night will be hosted by yours truly and your boy Khalil, better known to some as Khingz of Abyssinian Creole.

this is an event that's a part of a week-long comedy festival , aka the Week Of Fun, put together by a group of local Seattle comics.

this event is especially special especially because it will also be the premier showing of the first MassLine ShortFilm "Manoj." written by Hari Kondabolu and directed by Zia Mohajerjasbi, "Manoj" is a 15 minute pseudo-documentary exploring the intricate details of the life and career of a pseudo-famous South Asian stand-up comedian.

you can buy your tickets at BROWNPAPERTICKETS and we suggest you do that NOW. only 7 bucks!
