2007-03-05 | Monday

STK Tour Journal #1

We're 4 hours into our road trip and are making a short stop in Portland. I'm feeling excited about heading down to the bay for show #1 and seeing hella good folks, kasamas and allies. Our rental has a filthy GPS system so we're lookin fwd to messin with that a little bit. My ears have already been treated to the following:

*Massline mixtape
*New beats for the Common Market LP
*Unmastered Blue Scholars' Bayani LP

Thoughts? Upon first impression, the mixtape is like the 2007 version of the Do The Math Compilation, in where hella emcees are just doing their thing over consistent, bangin beats. It's already a historic collab of ML artists and local cats, some or most who inhabit the ML forum. Big up to everyone involved in this project!

The Common Market beats are bangin'! Big up Sabzi!

The Bayani album is gonna surprise everyone. It's a soup of all type of different ingredients, and it sure tastes hella good. Check out the info to your right about the Bayani release party.

Looks like we gotta bounce. Big up to Claire (and her cat, Faith) for the hospitality!

Excelcior!
daps

2007-03-01 | Thursday

this is why i'm hot

the effects of global warming on our atmosphere are messin with a brovah's head right now. i get on myspace and i see post after post from people who live about 15 minutes from me saying things like "it's snowing outside!" or "we have 7 inches of snow!"

then i look out the window and it's just sunny beacon hill to me. oh hey there's the garbage truck... DAMN! i forgot to take the trash out again.

but back to the subject at hand. for today, i'd just like to take a moment of silence to remember


Lohachara.



Lohachara is an island off the coast of India--er, was. the sea level has risen so much that a small island that used to be home to 10,000 indigenous people is now submerged.

2007-02-28 | Wednesday

respect my fresh

khalil and i hit the block, flipped the work, and made it do watt it do yesterday.

for us that means "drove around and looked at clothes." yes, in this day and age, the crass materialistic behavior that is cultivated in the minds of the people by a neo-imperialist economic superstructure has led two doods each with a class consciousness--who also never really had money to spend on high-end gear--to get really into freshness manifest in cloth. the art and design are just too filthy to ignore.

you see, there's a reason i don't rap.

at the recommendation of the homie Macklemore, one of the spots we hit up yesterday was a place called Sureel in Kirkland. Kirkland (or as Khingz calls it, the "K") is an interesting spot. we can see the california-ization of washington state as certain things like clothes and live music--things that used to be only available in the city--are making their way out to the near-eastside suburbs.



if you live near Kirkland and you're into snowboarding, or just really want a $225 Greedy Genius hoodie, hit up the folkers at Sureel.


new music

Volume 1 of the MASSLINE mixtape is almost finished. not sure when we'll have it out, but rest assured, it'll see the light of day.

it features MCs and beatmakers from all over the town including the MASSLINE artists, Khingz, Jerm, Asun, Hollis, Chev, Angel, Budo, Cin7, and many more. as the homie Vitamin D said -- something about little time and too many songs to do -- we get approached by many folks from all over wanting to collab. sometimes we don't have time, sometimes the schedules just don't fit, sometimes the music just wouldn't work for an album, and sometimes, the collabs would just be plain wack if we did them.

either way, many would say that these reasons are not good enough to keep people from hearing these tracks, so we decided to put together a mixtape.

MASSLINE TV Volume 1 is also in the works, scheduled for a release sometime this summer. if you see us around the town with a camera, give us a holla!

stay posted!

saba
