2007-03-17 | Saturday

watt it is, folkers.

big up to Big Rob Milliron for organizing the 2nd installment of the PUSH MC Battle last nite. it was an entertaining event that was judged by Jake One, Larry Mizzel, Jr., and myself. what made this battle unique from the other ones that i've been to in the town was the judging format. the two MCs would go at it for two 1-minute rounds, and then the attention would be focused on the judges for an American Idol style critique. folks would sometimes say that it was really the judges who won the battle cuz they had the best punchlines.

one of the better lines from the MCs that evening was:

"this dood is wearin Air BumFights"

and from the judges:

"that sounded like a bunch of drunken fraternity rap."

"MC1 here has a better flow, better delivery, and much filthier voice... but since this is a battle, which has more to do with capping and jokes, i'm gonna have to give it to MC2 even tho (*turns to MC2) homie, your flow is worse, your delivery is bad, and you straight up have a wack voice. i'd never buy your record. but hey, you got jokes."

2007-03-16 | Friday

STOP THE KILLINGS in the PHILIPPINES

The nationwide Stop the Killings tour organized by BAYAN-USA and Geologic of Blue Scholars ends in Seattle on March 23, 2007. Since 2001, over 835 activists of BAYAN member organizations and progressive party lists like Bayan Muna have been assassinated or forced to "disappear". The national Stop the Killings tour was organized to raise consciousness around the killing of progressives and to raise money to support travel expenses for the families of victims to testify at Permanent Peoples Tribunal at the Hague.

The show is to inspire active participation in finding and defending Justice and Peace in Seattle, the Philippines, Iraq and the world.

2007-03-13 | Tuesday

Another big Massline announcement is coming. Soon.

But for now, all you get is an update from me (Geo), sipping the sexy beast at the airport en route to Chicago (tonight!), Milwaukee (tomorrow) and New York (Friday, 3/16) for the second half of the Stop the Killings Tour reppin my Bayan-USA shirt. It's getting heavy back in the PI with the government's attempts to crack down on the progressive movement. I'll be heading out solo for this half of the trip (Kiwi will be holding it down back in the West... but will be in town for the Seattle show 3/23!).

Tonight I'll be making a return to Subterranean, where my peoples The Pacifics have been holding down the longest-running hip-hop weekly in the Windy City. It's been a minute since I've been back, and it's only fitting that I break bread with the folks who put me on when I was just getting started with this rap shit.

Blue Scholars will always have a connection with Chicago - our first two shows happened when The Pacifics, Typical Cats & Primeridian let us open up for their tour back in '02. Before working with Sabzi, I was working producers Bay Root Productions, who provided beats for Pacifics' first album. Through the isangmahal-Yellowtech bridge, Ms. Manager LuvJones flew me out to rock shows in Chicago when nobody in Seattle would put a brotha on. Damn, has it really been seven years?

It was these excursions into the underground Chicago hip-hop scene from 2000-2003 that I saw folks holding their own on stage, on the page and building together. It'll be good to be back.

So if you dig our shit, do yourself a favor and check our fam in Chicago. You can start with The Pacifics' The Case EP, produced entirely by Illmind (Pinoy beatmaker extraordinaire!).
