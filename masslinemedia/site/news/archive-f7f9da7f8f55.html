2007-06-19 | Tuesday

RIP Vincent Chin
1955-1982

Today marks the 25th anniversary of Vincent Chin's beating and subsequent murder. The incident is referenced in the second verse of "Morning of America" (track #14 on Bayani). Many incidents of racially-motivated violence against Asians in America have occurred before and since June 19, 1982 but Chin's murder stands as a monumental injustice - the two Detroit men who beat Chin to death admitted and even boasted about it and were acquitted of all charges. Check the 1989 Academy Award-nominated documentary "Who Killed Vincent Chin?" for some insight.
