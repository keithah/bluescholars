      On the List: Blue Scholars at Unity Fair, Garden of Hedon, farmers market (+23 more)

      By jseattle
      Share   Image not recovered (0 votes) ( report abuse ) ( add your comment )
      Historical form — submissions are disabled.
      Image not recovered Previous | x of x | Next Image not recovered
      Image not recovered

      How about a free Capitol Hill block party? How about a knuckle sandwich? Here's our latest weekly rundown of things to do. Add Capitol Hill events (for free!) to the CHS Calendar and let our friends at TheSunBreak.com know about your events elsewhere in this fine city we call home. Happy Memorial Day.

      Wednesday, May 25

      • Pandora Cellars tasting party at Poco Wine Room
      • Purr's 4th Annual Bartender Auction -- benefit for Rise n Shine
      • Steve Earle at Trinity Lutheran Church across from Cal Anderson. Free. First come, first seated.
      • Pie & Politics with Sally: Pike/Pine community fundraiser for Sally Clark
      • Dian Ferguson campaign kick-off at AME

       Thursday, May 26

        Watch on YouTube

      • The heart and soul of Capitol Hill's youth culture will be on display and, sorry kids, rockin' the mic at Thursday's annual Unity Fair at Seattle Central. It's free. It's about community. And organizer Sam Chesneau is expecting this seventh year of the campus festival to be the best Unity Fair yet. Blue Scholars, Supreme LaRock, Massive Monkees and other perform. There will be student booths and hot dogs to buy. There will be hula hoops. There will be ring toss. There will be unity. And veggie dogs. Party starts at 10 AM.Image not recovered

       

      • Meet the editors of Seattle Lesbian at Lobby Bar.
      • The Familiar -- A Ghost Story starts at 10th Ave's Odd Duck Studio
      • Anomie Bell at Chop Suey
      • Vocalpoint Seattle's Sweet Soul Music at Broadway Performance Hall (all weekend)
      • Citizens Transportation Advisory Committee workshop at Washington Middle School
      • East Precinct Crime Prevention Coalition monthly community meeting

      Friday, May 27

      • Northwest Folklife Festival at Seattle Center (all weekend. take the bus. don't eat that mushroom.)
      • Hello Kitty party at Varro
      • Late Night Improv returns to Theater Schmeater
      • Expanded Spinasse debuts

      Saturday, May 28

      • Street sweep in the CD at 23rd and Union
      • Garden of Hedon at FRED Wildlife Refuge. Benefits gardens for low-income and marginalized groups throughout King County.
      • Ecuador faces Mexico at Qwest
      • Help Capitol Hill Alano Club get the word out at the Bat n Rouge XIII Poster Party
      • Cirque Du Gay: Under the Big Top at Neighbours
      • Cat-tacular Benefit Party at Catalyst Gallery
      • Magnitude 15 gay rugby tournament

      Sunday, May 29

      • Sign up and get your stuff ready for the June 11th Capitol Hill Garage Sale Day at http://www.capitolhillgaragesale.com/
      • Onyx Chamber Players at Town Hall Seattle
      • The Broadway Farmers Market continues 11a-3p at Broadway/Pine

      Monday, May 30

      • Firefly/Serenity marathon starts at 10a at Central Cinema
