FOOD & SH*T + HOOD FAMOUS BAKESHOP SUPER BOWL PARTY PACK

January 27, 2015 in Hood Famous Bakeshop
By Geo

Looking for something different for your Super Bowl party spread? We got you. We've taken some finger food favorites from our previous Food & Sh*t pop-up menus, plus that new new Buko Pandan flavored cheesecake from Hood Famous Bakeshop, and put together a Super Bowl Party pack available for pick up on game day. 

Whole Ube & Buko Pandan Cheesecakes also available for order & pick-up at hoodfamousbakeshop.com.

LIL' WOODY'S & FOOD & SH*T SPECIAL OF THE WEEK: PHILLYPINO CHEESEBISTEK

January 22, 2015
By Food & Sh*t Fam

Last year, we teamed up with the homies at Lil' Woody's to present the ADOBOBURGER special of the week. And now, we've teamed up again to bring you the PHILLYPINO CHEESEBISTEK, a Philly Cheesesteak style sandwich with calamansi-soy marinated sliced beef, caramelized onion rings, queso fresco, scallions on a butter-toasted hoagie bun. It's available starting Tuesday, January 20th through Monday, January 26th at both Seattle Lil' Woody's locations in Capitol Hill and Ballard.

HOOD FAMOUS BAKESHOP LIMITED EDITION "12" BUKO PANDAN CHEESECAKE

January 15, 2015 in Hood Famous Bakeshop
By Food & Sh*t Fam

Following up the great response to its flagship item, Chera's Hood Famous Ube Cheesecake, the Hood Famous Bakeshop is rolling out a new cheesecake flavor: Buko Pandan. 

In addition to debuting as the dessert course at our next pop-up (Makibaka!, January 19th), the bakeshop is rolling out a limited edition "12" batch of whole buko pandan cheesecakes this weekend. Go to hoodfamousbakeshop.com to order yours in time for the big game on Sunday.
