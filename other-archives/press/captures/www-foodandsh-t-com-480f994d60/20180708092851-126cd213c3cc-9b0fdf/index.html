Food & Sh*t is back. One night only. May 21 at HEYDAY. (SOLD OUT!)

 
Image not recovered — IMG_0485.JPG

FOOD & SH*T is hosting a prix-fixe pop-up 5-course dinner (appetizers, main course, dessert) on Monday, May 21, 2018 at HEYDAY (1372 31st Ave S, Seattle, WA).

There will be two dinner services: 5-7 p.m. and 8-10 p.m. Reservation tickets are $75/seat and online advance payment required (ticketing link below). Seating is limited to 60 per dinner service.

All-ages welcome, with bar & alcoholic drinks available for 21+. Patrons are welcome to hang out at the bar.

The menu will be a surprise! See our previous menus for an idea of what we'll be serving and follow us on instagram for sneak peeks. Think seasonal, soulful, fresh.
