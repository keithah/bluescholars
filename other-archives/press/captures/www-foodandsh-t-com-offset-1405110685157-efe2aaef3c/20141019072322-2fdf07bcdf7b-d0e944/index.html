SEATTLE: JUNE 3 - JUNE 9 ADOBOBURGER SPECIAL AT LI'L WOODY'S

June 03, 2014
By Food & Sh*t Fam
Image not recovered

Seattle fam: we've collaborated with our friends at Li'l Woody's to bring you the ADOBOBURGER: Adobo marinated/injected/roasted pork loin, Calamanslaw (calamansi, red cabbage, radicchio, mayo, red onion, garlic), fried egg, Adobo reduction mayo, fried garlic crumbs, and green onion on a Hawaiian sweet bun. It's the special of the week from June 3rd to June 9th, as we gear up for the 2nd Adobofest Seattle cook-off coming this August!
