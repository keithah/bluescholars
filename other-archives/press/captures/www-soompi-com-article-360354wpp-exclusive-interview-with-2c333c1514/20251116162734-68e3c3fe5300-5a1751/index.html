Exclusive Interview with Geo of Blue Scholars

Music
May 22, 2010
by Soompi

We scored an exclusive interview with Filipino American MC Geologic of the underground rap duo Blue Scholars at the University of Washington in Seattle.  Watch as Geo talks about a variety of topics including: Meeting his partner Sabzi at UW, the importance of politics, being the most well-known Seattle rapper, the Seattle rap scene, having a diverse fan base, the Soompi Top Ten Asian American rappers list, working with Bambu on ‘Slow Down’, why there are so many Filipinos in the arts, having dedicated fans, the effect of the internet on musicians careers, being true to yourself, the labeling that goes on in hip-hop.
