DVD: Raymond Red’s Sakay

Posted on February 8, 2009
Filed Under Philippine Cinema |

Raymond Red’s Sakay (1993), a historical epic about Macario Sakay and the last revolutionaries in the struggle against the U.S. invasion and occupation, is finally seeing a DVD release.

Already in stores in the Philippines, available online at CineFilipino.com late February/Early March. CineFilipino is emerging as our own Pinoy version of The Criterion Collection, having re-released classic joints such as Lino Brocka’s Tinimbang Ka Ngunit Kulang (1974) and Insiang (1976), and Ishmael Bernal’s Manila By Night/City After Dark (1980). And Sampaguita films from the 50s and 60s, too. And their transfer quality is above-average for FIlipino DVDs. 

Anybody out there seen Sakay? Thoughts?
