Video: “Love” dir. Patricio Ginelsa

Posted on February 12, 2009
Filed Under Video |

Archived embedded media is not available for offline playback. Open its archived source

You might’ve seen it during a commercial break in Sunday’s Grammy telecast. Yes that’s Bambu (as a Cupid Agent) in a Greyboy music video (not rapping) for “Love.”  Now you can vote for it in the Lincoln Music Video Challenge right here.

Usually, I’d just vote for my peoples regardless. But I heard that we’re in a post-racial America now, so I went and watched all of these videos to assure myself that I wasn’t voting along racial lines that don’t exist anymore. It was painful. So take my word for it. Vote for love. And then vote for “Love,” directed by Patricio Ginelsa — the only video of the bunch that looks like people had fun making it.
