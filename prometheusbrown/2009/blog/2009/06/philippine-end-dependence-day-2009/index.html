Philippine End Dependence Day, 2009

Posted on June 12, 2009
Filed Under Film Reviews |

Kinda weird celebrating an independence that hasn’t happened yet but fuck it, I’ll take a celebration with my people any way I can get it. Two years ago we (Blue Scholars) released Bayani on this very day and I wrote a reflection on that moment, which was also a reflection on the country I more than just claim lineage from. Revisiting that piece reminded me that things have changed. For the worse.

On the 111th anniversary of the Katipunan’s betrayal, the Philippines remains a neocolony of the United States, which uses our tax dollars to fund military exercises (aka war on “the opposition”) in a supposedly sovereign country. So for Filipinos living in America who transmit funds, that’s 50% of your paycheck going back home to your family and 20% going to some goons who might try to kill em.

Now you’d think that an American citizen abducted by a shady government would be a big deal in the media and government. Unless that American citizen is Melissa Roxas and that country is a strategic US ally/colony.

You’d think that a no man is exempt from facing trial in a rape charge. Unless that man, Daniel Smith, is a member of the most powerful military in the world and the woman is just another poor third worlder.

You’d think that someone in the Seattle Chamber of Commerce would know better than to over-enthusiastically celebrate the 1909 Alaska Yukon Pacific Expo, where indigenous Filipinos were put on display in Seattle in “authentic village huts” for amusement and justification for the Philippine-American War.

Shit, you’d think more people would know that the Philippine-American war even happened.

You’d think president Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo’s Ph.D-level education would’ve gave her a history lesson on what happened the last time a dictator killed the constitution and tried jailing and killing off their opponents, as she’s doing now.

And you’d think with all that bullshit, we’d have little to celebrate. But that’s not us. Life’s too short and GMA’s making it shorter. We’ll celebrate victory in advance cause it’s gonna happen one day anyway, perhaps even sooner with all the bad moves the elite has been making lately. So this is all just rehearsal for the big party. Plus, like my dude DV-One told me last week, it’s a great time to be Filipino. I say it’s always been a great time. It just took the rest of yall a few dancers and a boxer to make it official.

Big up to my Bayan-USA folks and all allies and concerned people pushing back. I raise this cold bottle of Red Horse malt liquor to every one of you. Mabuhay!
