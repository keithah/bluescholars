RIP Alexis Tioseco (1981-2009)

September 1, 2009

Such a senseless tragedy.

It was Alex Tioseco’s writings on Philippine film on his blog and as editor of Criticine.com that guided me through my rediscovery and love of Philippine cinema over the past couple years. We lost a giant today–may his memory live on and the many wishes he left us one day come true.
