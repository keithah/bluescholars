Watchmen Trailer

Posted on July 17, 2008
Filed Under Films, News |

Alan Moore and Dave Gibbons‘ 12-part limited comic-book series, Watchmen (DC, 1986-87), was a deconstruction of Superhero lore in 80’s comics and a damn good story. We can only hope that director Zack Snyder’s (300, Dawn of the Dead) adaptation gives a similar treatment to this decade’s wave of paint-by-number Superhero flicks and clowns on, rather than emulates, that formula. That’s the only way it’s going to work, and no amount of great casting, faithfulness to dialogue, special effects and art direction will save the film version if it waters down writer Alan Moore’s grit and politically sharp satire, aimed not only at comic book norms but also at government and capitalism.

So, the rumors that the first teaser trailer would accompany the opening of Batman: The Dark Knight this weekend are not only true, but the trailer has already hit the web a day early. Shit looks bananas.
