IN HAWAII THIS SATURDAY (6/28)

06.24.08 | 7 Comments

Honolulu, to be exact. In Chinatown, at club NEXT DOOR, brought to you by the good folks at GeniuneHI doing it big, as always. This is our FIRST show in the 808, and a homecoming for Geo, who grew up in Honolulu (1982-1990) before moving to the Pacific Northwest!

BLUE SCHOLARS in Hawaii
Saturday, June 28, 2008
Doors @ 9pm
Show @ 10pm
21 & Over

Next Door

Honolulu, HI 96817
