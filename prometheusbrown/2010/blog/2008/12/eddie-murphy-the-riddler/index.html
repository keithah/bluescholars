Eddie Murphy, The Riddler?

Posted on December 24, 2008
Filed Under Upcoming |

Read today on The Obenson Report about an interesting casting rumor: Your boy Eddie Murphy as The Riddler in the upcoming follow-up to The Dark Knight. Everywhere, fanboys are hollering hell no. While I’m skeptical myself, I recall another certain casting rumor that had everybody up in arms: Heath Ledger as Joker. 

The Sun reporter Gordon Smart stands by his story though Warner Bros. has officially denied the report. Smart claims that his anonymous source is the same person who tipped him off that Daniel Craig was going to be the next 007  - another rumor that was thoroughly mocked at the time.

Such a delicate topic, this casting game for comic-book and franchise films, where everyone and their moms seem to have it all figured out. Some roles are no-brainers, like Patrick Stewart as Professor X. But for the most part, it’s hit or miss, depending not so much on actors and costumes but on the director and writer’s aesthetic framework. Robert Downey Jr. was dope as Tony Stark/Iron Man, but imagine if Michael Bay did it instead of Jon Favreau. As long as Christorpher Nolan and not some other hack is directing, I’m all for whoever actor in whatever role, whether A-list celeb or community theater extra.
