Posted on February 10, 2009
Filed Under Upcoming |

A SONG FOR OURSELVES 2.28.09 Premiere Trailer from Tadashi Nakamura on Vimeo.

Going going, back back, to Cali Cali…
 

Tadashi Nakamura’s (Pilgrimage, Yellow Brotherhood) new documentary, A Song For Ourselves, is gearing up for its premiere at the end of the month. Again, we (Blue Scholars) will be in the building for the premiere screening and afterparty in Los Angeles on February 28th. Check the trailer.

A SONG FOR OURSELVES
The World Premiere of a film by Tadashi Nakamura
Live performances by: Blue Scholars, Bambu & Kiwi
& special guest artists Nobuko Miyamoto and Charlie Chin
Saturday, February 28, 2009
Aratani/Japan America Theatre | 244 S. San Pedro St. | Los Angeles, CA 
$10 General Admission, Limited $50 VIP Tickets
For tickets and info visit www.jaccc.org or call (213) 680-3700
