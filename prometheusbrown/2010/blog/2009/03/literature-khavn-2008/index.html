Literature (2008) | dir. KHAVN, wr. Joel Toledo | 10 min | Philippines

This is not a film by KHAVN. 

From the streets of Manila, literally. A man posts up in the middle of a busy road with a chair, a book and a gun. Could’ve been called Unpoetic Justice. Or Poetic Injustice. Either of those titles reek of indie film title snobbery FAIL, so Literature is just fine. Real creative low-budget shit right here. Written by Joel Toledo and directed by Khavn De La Cruz.
