My Top Ten Films of 2008 (Finally)

Posted on March 24, 2009
Filed Under Lists |

I’d invoke the Late Pass, but while folks who get paid to watch films (or those with the privilege to do it all day everyday) made their best of 2008 lists before 2008 was even a wrap, I and the rest of us were/are still catching up. Plus, the new year doesn’t usually start for me until Winter’s over anyway…

But somewhere between late nights at the crib not working on music, various film festivals and a crazy touring schedule last year, I managed to watch over 60 new movies released last year — the most I’ve ever viewed in one year.

This list was actually finalized about a month ago, but I wanted to watch a few more 2008 films that I missed to see if any of em would dent the list. None did. An honorable mention goes out to The First 40 minutes of Wall-E,  Che: El Argentino (Part One), Medicine for Melancholy, Mongol, and The Last Mistress. Films that I didn’t get a chance to see but wanted to include: Ballast, Trouble the Water, The Order of Myths, The Reader, Hunger and The Visitor. 

So with that, I present to you:
