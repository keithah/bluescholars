POLL RESULTS: TOP TEN FILMS OF 2008, ACCORDING TO PEOPLE I KNOW OR KNOW ME OR BOTH

Posted on March 13, 2009
Filed Under Polls |

Thank you to everybody who participated in this poll. It was conducted on the fly over the previous couple weeks, gathering 83 participants whose top five movies of 2008 lists I solicited personally via text, email, Facebook/Myspace/Twitter, and this blog. Nearly all reside in the U.S. and generally between the ages of 16-40. Before getting to the list, some observations: 

  • Compared to other polls: 5 of the top 6 (minus Iron Man) in this poll are the exact top 5 films in Film Comment’s readers’ poll. Lowbrow is the new highbrow (middlebrow?). 
     
  • Sausagefest at the cinema: 70 male responses to 13 female responses.
     
  • People who make rap music are heavily inclined to like any movie whose source material is Marvel or DC.  
     
  • Films that made many other best-of lists that were not mentioned at all in this poll: Rachel Getting Married, Vicky Cristina Barcelona, My Winnipeg, A Christmas Tale.
     
  • Pineapple Express. Really?
     
  • Number of total films mentioned with 2 or more votes: 40. Number of foreign-language films among that list: 3.
     
  • The next 10 that didn’t make it (honorable mention): Step Brothers, Gran Torino, In Bruges, Forgetting Sarah Marshall, The Wackness, Zack & Miri Make a Porno, Burn After Reading, Frost/Nixon, Che, Let the Right One In.
     
  • Highest domestic grossing film on this list: The Dark Knight (#1) - $531 million. Least grossing film on this list: Religulous (#9) - $13 million. 
     
  • Commercial success, critical FAIL: Will Smith’s two 2008 movies (Hancock - $228m, Seven Pounds - $62.9m) get a total of 2 votes (2 for Hancock, 0 for Seven Pounds).

And now, Prometheusbrown.com presents:
The Top Ten Films of 2008,

According to People I Know or Know Me or Both:

1. The Dark Knight - 53 votes (out of 83 ballots)

2. Slumdog Millionaire - 34 votes

3. Iron Man - 23 votes

4. The Wrestler & Milk (tie) - 19 votes

6. Wall-E - 18 votes

7. Tropic Thunder - 14 votes

8. Pineapple Express - 12 votes

9. Religulous & The Curious Case of Benjamin Button (tie) - 9 votes

Thoughts?
