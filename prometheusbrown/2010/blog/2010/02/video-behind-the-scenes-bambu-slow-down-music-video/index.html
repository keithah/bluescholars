VIDEO: Behind the Scenes: Bambu “Slow Down” Music Video

February 3rd, 2010 | 4 Comments »

Archived embedded media is not available for offline playback.

Some behind-the-scenes footage of Bambu’s “Slow Down” music video shoot in L.A. last week at Imix Bookstore. The track, which I’m featured on, is the lead single off Bam’s upcoming Paper Cuts EP and is produced by MTK. The filmmaker-homie Tad Nakamura (Pilgrimage, A Song For Ourselves) came through and shot some behind-the-scenes footage.

Paper Cuts drops on February 23rd!
