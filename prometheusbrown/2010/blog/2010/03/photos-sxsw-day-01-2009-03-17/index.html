PHOTOS: SXSW, Day 01 (2009-03-17)

March 22nd, 2010 | 2 Comments »

The date on the Yashica T4 reset back to 1987 and I forgot to change it. It was St. Patrick’s Day and Macklemore performed “Irish Celebration” to a packed, sweaty bar. Sabzi DJ’d for Das Racist. Green and drunkenness everywhere, and somehow, very little of that made it into these photos.
