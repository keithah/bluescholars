June 28th, 2010 | 1 Comment »

Archived embedded media is not available for offline playback.

I rolled through Fice’s “Two Five Three” video release/birthday party at The Cool in downtown Tacoma last Saturday and had a blast (and a few shots of Don Julio). Cats from all over the 253 came through to support their own, repping their scene proudly. Logics, DJ Semaj, Dance Broomz, Rockwell Powers, to name a few, all in the building. It was a beautiful thing.
