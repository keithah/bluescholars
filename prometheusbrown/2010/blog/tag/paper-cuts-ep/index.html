PHOTOS: Bambu “Paper Thin” Music Video Behind The Scenes

June 9th, 2010

Hella westcoastin’ in NYC.

Cruised around the L.E.S. in a three-Cadillac caravan last Saturday before the STKxNYC show to check out the shoot for Bambu’s “Paper Thin” (off the ...paper cuts… EP) music video featuring Chace Infinite. Directed by Andrew Graziuso of LightWork Media, the same team who did Bam’s “Quit” video, shot last year during the Philippine Independence Day Parade. Photos were shot with a Canon AE-1 Program, Canon 50mm f/1.8 lens & Ilford HP5+ 400 B&W film @ 400 ASA. (more…)
