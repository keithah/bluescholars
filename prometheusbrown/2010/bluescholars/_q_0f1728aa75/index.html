Sasquatch! Festival Reviews

05.27.08 | 3 Comments


photo by Christopher Nelson, from The Stranger - Line Out

While Saba and I finally take a breath to write up our own Sasquatch! festival highlights and photo recap, here’s some of the blog/press looks we’ve gotten since:

Seattle Post-Intelligencer:

It was great to hear hip hop booming out of the Gorge’s spectacular main stage sound system. Blue Scholars MC Geologic made the same observation telling the crowd he loved hearing DJ Sabzi, one of the brightest undiscovered gems in hip hop, play his beats on the massive Gorge system.

Rolling Stone (via Rainn Wilson aka “Dwight Schrute” from The Office)

I got to meet many of my favorite bands and had lovely chats with some of my favorite bands, the National and Cold War Kids and Rogue Wave. I got turned on to many new bands like What Made Milwaukee Famous, and Seattle’s own Blue Scholars…

Paste Magazine:

Giving big props to their Pacific Northwest home, Blue Scholars rocked the Sasquatch! stage with a one-MC-one-DJ dynamic. If I hadn’t seen it for myself, I would’ve been convinced there was a massive crew up there

Tacoma News Tribune:

Over on the main stage, George “Geologic” Quibuyen and Saba “Sabzi” Mohajerjasbi, aka Blue Scholars, showed why they’re the new kings of Seattle’s hip-hop scene.
