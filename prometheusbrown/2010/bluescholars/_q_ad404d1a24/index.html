Shows, The Travels

Bellingham - RIP Nightlight Lounge

01.28.08 | Comment?

Bellingham has been good to us. And still got love in 2008 with back-to-back sold-out shows at Western Washington University. A big part of any Bellingham trip is clowning wack rap with Mr. Surge Spittable, who gave us news that The Nightlight Lounge was closing and that its last shows were happening the same weekend we were in town. The Nightlight is one of the first places we performed at outside of Seattle (with Lifesavas, April 2005). The staff kept it pro and the sound was always on point (which we cant say for far too many mid-size venues). They even made sure to keep copies of XXL and The Source in the green room for us rapper types. According to Surge, after C-Rayz Walz rocked the Nightlight, he declared Bellingham as his favorite city and adopted hometown (as listed on his Myspace). From 2004 til now, Nightlight became a must-stop for many independent artists - many of whom left their mark on the wall behind the stage:

With the Nightlight closing and our two shows at Western, it was like a 206 rapper convention in the Ham: Common Market, D. Black and the Sport N Life fam, Dyme Def, Grynch, DJ Nphared, Macklemore, Kublakai, DJ Swervewon, Nam and fam rolled through. We’ll miss Nightlight no doubt, but with the constant opening and closure of venues of the years in Seattle, we’ve grown accustomed to it. Word from Surge is that another hip-hop venue will likely emerge by spring. Or if anyone is down to pitch in to collectively buy the Nightlight, count me in. I’ll put down $50. Now let’s get a few more thousand people to pitch in.  

Message to all NW hip-hop artists (at least on the independent label/self-released level) - if you’ve never rocked Bellingham, you still haven’t done shit. 

RIP Nightlight (2004-2008)
