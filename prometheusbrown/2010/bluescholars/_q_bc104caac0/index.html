The Stranger: Zia Mohajerjasbi

06.05.08 | 3 Comments

Our filmmaker comrade Zia Mohajerjasbi is featured in this week’s The Stranger. You may know Zia not only for looking like a younger Sabzi, but for his fine work on our videos (Back Home, Joe Metro and Loyalty), as well as with Gabriel Teodros (No Label, Don’t Cry For Us) and The Physics (Ready For We).

“I consciously avoid every popular image that’s been seen of Seattle on a national scale,” says Zia over lunch at Cafe Presse, the most European scene on Capitol Hill. “A shot might be nice, but I will not use it because I don’t want to see the Space Needle and I don’t want to see Pioneer Square.” What one wants to see and not see, that is the meaning-motor of Seattle’s new cinema. And all local directors have this agreement: What must be seen is what is not seen on a “national scale.”
