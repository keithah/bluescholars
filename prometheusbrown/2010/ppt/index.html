People Power Tour - Northwest (Part 1)

April 28, 2008
Filed Under Recap | Leave a Comment

So here we go again… The ‘meat’ of the People Power Tour begins… in Spokane.

Day 1:


Chev and Daps @ the telly

About a 3.5 hour drive east of Seattle, we (daps, chev, geo, and myself) arrived in Washington’s ’second most populous city’ around 4pm, tired and slightly hungry. Thankfully, the extremely hospitable folks at Raw Sushi (where we were performing) greeted us with a hotel room, a reserved table, and our choice of items on the menu. And I must say, the food was excellent! Not just that, but the place is run (and perhaps owned?) by (i’m assuming) Filipinos! Holla!!!

Not having ever been to Spokane before, and probably having my own prejudices (where the colored folks at?), I honestly wasn’t expecting much. But, that said, it turned out to be a crackin show! A handful of Blue Scholars fans, of course, were in the house, as well as some folks from Gonzaga (University), with the rest being people who were just there to drink/eat and party on a Friday night. The energy was GREAT, and everyone ROCKED it!

One challenging thing about performing in these kinds of settings is that it’s hard to sell merchandise. I made a feeble attempt to hustle a few CDs, selling only a couple. One guy actually gave me $20 and told me to keep the change ($15)! Usually i’ll insist on giving back whatever I owe them, but in this case, i was like, “Okay!” Fuggit! I mean, damn, the only other person who was interested in a CD had the nerve to ask for for FREE copy (why do people do that?), leaving me in the awkward position of being like, “Umm… bro, it’s FIVE DOLLARS…” Yup, the grind.

Day 2:


We had a few hours to kill before heading out to Pullman for the WSU show, so after brunch at this random Vietnamese-owned diner, we decided to hit up United Groove Merchants, perhaps the dopest used record shop in the Northwest.


Honestly, it’s been a minute since I’ve bought wax. Partially to blame on Serato, but also I realized I had other things I needed to prioritize my money on. I still got a pretty thick record collection in my studio, but it hasn’t grown much in a while. Still, it was nice to finger through old records and even contemplate copping a couple, including:


The D.O.C record was still in the shrink wrap!

After all that, we STILL had time to catch a flick…

OHHHH yeeeeaaahhh…

The movie is hilarious. But that’s for another blog. All I gotta say is, I will never look at a bag of weed the same ever again!

PPT Day 1: Milwaukee, WI

April 21, 2008
Filed Under Media, Recap | Leave a Comment

so… the PEOPLE POWER tour has officially started! first stop: MILWAUKEE! university of wisconsin - milwaukee to be exact.

big up to the comrade carlo for scoopin us up from the chi, driving us cross-state and basically being our chauffeur for the weekend. 

so anyways, we walk into the student center of UWM and low and behold…


yeah, that’s what we said….

so, you know what we had to do, right?


YESSIR!

so as some of y’all know, the People Power Tour is a nationwide effort to 1) raise funds for Bayan USA and 2) raise awareness about the human rights crisis and government corruption in the philippines. for those of you who might remember the Stop the Killings tour from last year, this is kind of an extension of that.


i used to not be a big fan of using projectors during shows, but i’m warming up to it…

the space we were performing at, the Fireside Lounge, was literally a ‘lounge’ area with a fireplace as its centerpiece. so we adapted our setup to make it a little more ‘intimate,’ set up the comfy chairs and sofas all nice and feng shui-like, basically did whatever we could with what we had. the fact that the speakers were part of the room’s PA system had me slightly worried, especially since i just refused to do a show a day before for that very reason (mental note: include something in my tech rider stating that a room’s PA system doesn’t count as an adequate sound setup!). regardless, it worked for the space and size of the audience, and shoot, we came all the way from the west coast so, it would have to do for this one night.

and off we went!


umm… i think that’s geo…

overall, it wasn’t a bad night. the folks that came through (about 50 or so, on a saturday night mind you) were into it and showing love. and the big thing was that the crowd was like, 9 times the size of the one from last year. and we definitely started on a positive note on the fundraising side as well!!!

so all in all, it was a GREAT kickoff to a very important tour. lots of kinks to work out in terms of logistics and organizing, but we’re applying those lessons day by day. our tour manager brian is hustling especially HARD to move this forward, and it’s definitely a plus to have him on board. give that guy a hug the next time you see him!

next up, SPOKANE!!!

(chicago was excellent too, but for some reason i forgot to take pics. boo!)

  • ABOUT

    BAYAN-USA is is an alliance of progressive Filipino groups in the U.S. representing organizations of students, scholars, women, workers, and youth. As the only international chapter of Bagong Alyansang Makabayan (BAYAN-Philippines), BAYAN-USA serves as an information bureau for the national democratic movement of the Philippines and as a campaign center for anti-imperialist Filipinos in the U.S.
