This is an open message; to rally up groups of people, to explore music which strikes a common purpose and release vocals which aim higher. Beyond rap for rap's sake, and beats designed for clubs. Entry By Troops is one effort to let two ordinary guys in to your eardrums with room for you to take on your own understanding of what they are about, with the hope that you will feel an extraordinary vibe.
About Me:
 
The duo formed in 2006 after meeting through friends, Entry By Troops started out by collaborating over the Internet, sending back and forth mp3 files of tracks for potential songs. In 2007 E.B.T. began to work on their sound, which blends indie, hiphop and electronica music. They are now in the process of making their first EP titled Comfort In Conflict, with a selection of demo tracks available to listen to on the web, and the full release to be announced in 2009.
