Happy 199th Birthday, Abraham

Abraham Lincoln, born 1809, today.

My aunt used to live in Springfield, Illinois. Illinois is one of my two home states. Both sets of grandparents come from Illinois, but I was raised in Wisconsin. But I lived in Canton, Illinois for a while as a teen. Long enough to get my ass pounded into a gravel road. (There's a story for another day.)

Anyway, I remember visiting the cabin where he lived, which is in Springfield, followed by the capital, when I was a young boy. I was too young to remember much in the way of stories. But I can still see the cabin, and feel the excitement I felt standing in front of it.

And it's interesting. Both the leading contenders for the Democratic nomination are from Illinois, and which ever one gets the nomination, history will be made. Just feeling a little Illinois pride, and I thought I'd give it a shout out.

(Nods to Kentucky and Indiana, too, Lincoln's birthplace and childhood home.)
