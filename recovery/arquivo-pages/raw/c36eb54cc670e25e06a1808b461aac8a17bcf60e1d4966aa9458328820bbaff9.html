06:55 pm - Elizabethtown
why is that movie so damn adorable...

it's disgusting....yet, I love it....

this waspy version of garden state is certainly one of my favorite modern romantic comedies; it's jewish counterpart, however, has a slightly drier wit and i think a better sound track. I wouldn't expect anything else from Zach Braff. Garden State also has much more drug use than Elizabethtown.

Just my thoughts....

I am quite glad 2008 is over and through. Endika commented on my change in mood, and the Buddhist attributed my improved attitude to a change in the year and thus my outlook on life. Tee Hee. He told me today that I walked through doors smiling with an aura of happiness. Only 3 things have changed in my life the last few days.
1. the year is new!
2. I have made some wonderful new friends
3. I have a date on friday, with a real live woman. This is going to be interesting...
The both of us will be trying to figure out if we want to continue and see each other again. And this will happen with each consecutive sighting, as each consecutive "date" will get exponentially closer in time to each other, until one of us decides that She, or I, no longer want to be involved in this relationship.

I can see the entire relationship in my head, i just have no idea over how long it will last.

it's really a silly practice.

When I think about all the work it entails, id really much rather just sit around with my friends....and no worry about it.

What I think is funny is that of most of my friends, of which they refer to me, along with Shannon, as the pure extroverts, are all in romantic relationships or chasing after them as they want to be in one.
Yet, I hate being alone, don't necessarily want romantic attachment with anybody at the moment. I just want a solid friend base. I am not opposed to the idea if it occurs, but I'm not really looking for it. Then again, perhaps I am a hypocrite by having gone on a few private outings with some people over the last few weeks.

Well, I am starving...so I am going to find some food.
