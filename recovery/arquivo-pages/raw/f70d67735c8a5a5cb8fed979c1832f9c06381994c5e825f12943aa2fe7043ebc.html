July 26th, 2009

even though i can't beatmatch

  • Jul. 26th, 2009 at 4:38 PM

i applied to be a dj on the university of washington's online radio station for fall. i hope they accept me! i miss my record store job so much and being surrounded by music again would make me happy.

they asked you to submit some examples of tracks you might play. a lot of the ones i like i couldn't find streaming music samples of online. in the end i just grabbed some random stuff from my itunes library. i didn't want to put entirely obscure stuff in the event that it discouraged them, so i tried to make it a mixture of some well known producers/more accessible stuff and some local producers or lesser known ones. i figured i'd throw them up here in case anyone feels like listening.

Apparat - Hailin from the Edge

L'usine - Counting

Harmonic 313 - Word Problems

Foscil - Ran

Hudson Mohawke - Polkadot Blues

Let's Go Outside - I see you dancing

Burial & Four Tet - Wolf Cub

Caro - Sea of Hands

Bjork - All is full of love (Plaid remix)

Blue Scholars - North by Northwest

Dorian Concept - Fort Teen

Express Rising - Ithaca Summer

Gui Boratto - No Turning Back

Jon Hopkins - Light Through The Veins

Junior Boys - Like A Child (Carl Craig remix)
