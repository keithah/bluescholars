Blue Scholars
Genre: Hip-Hop Hometown: Seattle WA
www.bluescholars.com
Wednesday, March 18   10:20 p.m.
Back Alley Social   (303 W 5th St)

Blue Scholars, a hip hop duo created in 2002 and based in Seattle, consists of DJ, Sabzi (Saba Mohajerjasbi) and MC, Geologic (George Quibuyen, a.k.a. Prometheus Brown). As momentous as 2007 was for Seattle’s Blue Scholars, 2008 is already looking to be even bigger for the duo as they released their second digital EP and third music video since the release of their critically acclaimed album Bayani on Massline/Rawkus. Since Bayani hit the streets in June 2007, Geologic and Sabzi have toured relentlessly, headlining sold-out shows up and down the West Coast and most recently spending the Summer touring the country with West Coast hip-hop legends Hieroglyphics.
In December 2007, Blue Scholars curated the first ever all-Northwest hip-hop festival The Program – headlining a five-night run of sold-out shows at Seattle’s Neumo’s, and are expanding that in 2008, bringing The Program to Portland, OR and Bellingham, WA. While aiming to bridge the emerging Northwest music scene to the rest of the country, they have answered any doubts about making an impact beyond their region. In New York City, Blue Scholars generated a buzz after opening up for Q-Tip at the CMJ Music Festival in October 2007, returning in February 2008 to headline a sold-out show at the renowned Mercury Lounge.
Despite their rigorous touring schedule, Geo and Sabzi haven’t missed a beat in the studio, continuously cranking out new Blue Scholars tracks as well as expanding their catalog to include collaborations with local and national artists. Geo and RA Scion (of Common Market) were recently featured on Guru’s recent “Jazzmatazz: Back to the Future Mixtape.” In addition to their collective work, Geo and Sabzi have also pursued individual endeavors and remain integrated in the political organizing and community work that defines the essence of their music and message.
