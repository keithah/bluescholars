"Sagaba Remix" Blue Scholars is a Seattle-based duo - Geologic's the MC, Sabzi's the DJ (he also works with RA Scion to make up the duo Common Market). The Long March EP came one year after their 2004 debut. Personally, I think it's amazing how the duo was able to carefully craft this EP into a sort of precursor for their more recent full LP, Bayani , from 2007. Their style is political, kind of following in the rebellious style pioneered by Immortal Technique, but less in-your-face. The result is a somewhat dark political tale that invokes a feeling of determination and high spirits. To me, this is one of the few albums that can tackle social issues ("Commencement Day", "Cornerstone") in both a global and personal method ("Wounded Eyes", "La Botella"). You'll see what I mean if you try it. Not for everyone, but if you've enjoyed Bayani , you'll love this release, as it sounds like a continuation.
