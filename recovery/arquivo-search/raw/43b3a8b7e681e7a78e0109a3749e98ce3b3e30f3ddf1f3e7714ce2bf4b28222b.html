Welcome to Abcreole.com! The official website for Abyssinian Creole, Khingz (a.k.a. Khalil Crisis), Gabriel Teodros, and Kitone Productions. Lookout for full websites for all artists involved over the next few months. This is your online source for all upcoming shows, releases, side-projects, exclusive songs as well as online store for past and future releases... and on that note...



Debut 12" singles from Abyssinian Creole & Khazm 247 will be dropping February 25th, 2005! Lookout for "Sexy Beast" and Khazm's "Diaries of a M.A.D." in record stores throughout the greater northwest as well as online, here and at Madkrew.com. You can catch Abyssinian Creole rockin it live this week on Hip Hop 101 TV alongside Klose, Khazm, WD4D and members of the World Council for the Universal Zulu Nation; Lucky Strike, Righteous and Yoda. This episode of Hip Hop 101 is the kick-off event for a 4-day celebration of hip hop culture, and the 1 year anniversary of the Seattle chapter of the Universal Zulu Nation. This Saturday Ab Creole will be rockin it at the Festival Sundiata as another part of the Zulu Anniversary. Check us out in Larry Mizell Jr.'s column in the Stranger last week too. Shoutouts to Angel 179 and Alfie Numeric for blessing us with the illest artwork. Also shoutout to all the myspacers for your love and support. The full-length debut album from Abyssinian Creole will be out in time for summer, with guestspots from Moka Only, Geologic (Blue Scholars) and Professor Macklemore!
