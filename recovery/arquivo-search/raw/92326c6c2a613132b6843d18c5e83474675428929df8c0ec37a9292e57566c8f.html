Video: Blue Scholars "Hi-808"

Posted by Shamz on Aug 10, 2009



Last week you got the audio, and now here's the vid for Blue Scholars' "Hi-808." The song will serve as the first single off of their upcoming EP, OOF! (8/25). The project is their ode to Hawaii. Says Geo of the vid: "Earlier this year, we headed out to the old stomping grounds of Oahu, Hawaii to rock a show. We linked up with Kai Media to film a music video for HI-808, the song that inspired the rest of the OOF! EP. It was barely a week after we filmed the "Coffee and Snow" video, which is why we look so damn happy here."

Audio: Blue Scholars "Hi-808"

Posted by Shamz on Aug 05, 2009



Seattle based rap act, Blue Scholars (MC- Geologic, Producer/DJ- Sabzi) will be releasing their new EP, OOF! on August 25th. Here is a leak off the project entitled "Hi-808." As you would guess from the song title, this one bumps. OOF! is part of the Blue Scholars’ artist-owned label MassLine, underwritten through a strategic marketing partnership with Seattle’s independent coffee company Caffe’ Vita, and marketed in collaboration with Duck Down Records.
