Blue Scholars Seattle

2008 October 12
tags: blue scholars, Current TV, fashion, Food, Music, positivity, Rap, Seattle, society
by Afshin Rohani

(c) JORDAN NICHOLSON

The influential Hip-Hop duo Blue Scholars, were recently featured on the user content based television channel Current TV, if you haven’t heard of the group, I would describe them as thoughtful musicians reporting the ins and outs of life in Seattle, vividly and poetically in their tracks.

This short documentary has a great pace and is incredibly endearing to the cause their music represents, Geologic who provides the vocals for Blue Scholars is heavily involved in community projects and delivers a message deeply rooted in the struggles of working class American immigrants. Partnering with Geo is the avant-garde producer Sabzi, who is a member of the Baha’i Faith. He is trained in jazz piano, though nowadays his palette delves in Hip-Hop, which he uses to instill a positive message for young people.
