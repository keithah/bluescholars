VIDEO: Missy Tone

August 16th, 2010 | No Comments »

Whilst digging for obscure 90′s R&B online, I came across this video and got introduced to the world of Missy Tone. Maybe it was the stifling heat, but browsing through her 40+ videos of her singing was a cinematic experience. You never see Missy Tone, you only hear her singing. What we see is what she sees: the ground, the sky, a laptop computer, a table, the other side of the room. She lives somewhere in Seattle, where her thick Spanish accent would stand out in most places other than a few parts in West Seattle and the Southend. She must be around my age or older, because most of the songs she sings are early 90′s classics. Her it’s-not-that-serious personality bookends the short acappellas but when she gets into a song, she goes in. She always starts with “What it do?” and always ends prematurely with a giggle.

Everybody had or has a homegirl who just sings whenever she can. Watching these videos was like a Being John Malkovich type trip through that homegirl’s head.


MISSY TONE – “So In Love” (by UNV)

Here, she’s chillin at work, on a break, singing the song 2/3 of all Filipino couples of my generation chose as their wedding song. The opening shot is a swish pan away from a lighter and an ashtray with a cigarette in it towards the sky and the rail that separates her from it. Her singing is the painkiller that mediates this contradiction.


MISSY TONE – “Come With Me” (by Shai)

Missy Tone sings mostly in front of a notepad or laptop. The composition in this one is pure accidental poetry. The shallow depth of field blurs whatever she’s written (lyrics? poetry? grocery list?) while a small section of the mirror occupies the very top right of the frame, reflecting only a partial ceiling light and a desktop-less laptop screen with the Microsoft Windows operating system. She clearly favors analog over digital, and older R&B over the shit all the young kids are singing all over YouTube.


MISSY TONE – “From This Moment” (by Shania Twain)

Here we find out Missy Tone gets off work real late. She’s waiting in the parking lot for her ride. Perfect setting for belting out bars from a white woman power ballad. I loved the surprise ending.
