81st Academy Awards Nominations

Posted on January 22, 2009
Filed Under Awards |

Few surprises here, except perhaps a blackfaced Robert Downey, Jr. (Tropic Thunder) for Best Supporting Actor and the miraculous rise of The Reader despite a nonexistent buzz (has anyone besides an Academy member seen this?).
