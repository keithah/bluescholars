MUSIC: Native Guns “Handcuffs”

July 7th, 2010

Today, the jury in the Johannes Mehserle trial begins deliberations over whether or not he is guilty in the shooting death of Oscar Grant. Everywhere, particularly in Oakland and Los Angeles, people are on edge wondering if justice will be upheld. While we wait for the verdict, my comrades Kiwi and Bambu have reunited to release the first new Native Guns track in four years, “Handcuffs.” In it, they speak on witnessing the 1992 L.A. uprising and urge everyone to take it to the streets, but to channel their anger into organized efforts rather than spontaneous destruction. Hella westcoastin’ in NYC.

Directed by Andrew Graziuso of LightWork Media, the same team who did Bam’s “Quit” video, shot last year during the Philippine Independence Day Parade.
