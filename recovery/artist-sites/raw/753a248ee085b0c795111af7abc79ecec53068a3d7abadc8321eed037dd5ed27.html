4 FREE Classic Philippine Films Screening in Seattle.

Posted on February 11, 2009
Filed Under Philippine Cinema, Upcoming |

For the next four Thursday nights starting tomorrow (Feb. 12), you can catch Turumba (pictured above), Ora, Plata, Mata (Feb. 19), Insiang (Feb. 26) and Manila By Night/City After Dark (Mar. 5) at the University of Washington for the special stimulus-era price of FREE.  

Brought to the people by Makipag, the UW-based arts collective who, last month, screened Himala and La Visa Loca, as well as Kidlat Tahmik’s Perfumed Nightmare last year. If I wasn’t sliding in and out of town for the next month I’d hit up all these screenings. I want to see what Insiang is like on a big screen in the dark. With people in the audience and onscreen who look like me.

FREE Screenings of Films from the Philippines
presented by MAKIPAG
7pm on Thursdays in the UW Allen Library Auditorium 

FEB 12: TURUMBA (1981) dir. Kidlat Tahimik
- A comedy of manners about a family’s efforts to fill an extensive order for their handmade dolls.

FEB 19: ORO, PLATA, MATA (1982) dir. Peque Gallaga
- Two aristocratic families struggle to deal with the Japanese invasion of the Philippines during World War II.

FEB 26: INSIANG (1976) dir. Lino Brocka
- In the slums of Manila, a young girl seeks revenge against her abusers. Insiang was the first film from the Philippines to enter the Cannes Film Festival.

MAR 5:  MANILA BY NIGHT (1980) dir. Ishmael Bernal
- A multi-stranded narrative that tracks the sex, drugs, and passions of city dwellers after nightfall.
For more information, please contact makipag@u.washington.edu or visit http://students.washington.edu/makipag.
