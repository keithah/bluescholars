The Music

Slick Corea - Children of the Idiom

06.06.08 | 3 Comments

Our dude Phil Chang aka Slick Corea just released CHILDREN OF THE IDIOM - a digital album/book featuring FIVE tracks produced by Sabzi and a guest verse from Geo, as well as contributions from homeboys and all-stars alike. The project’s scope and execution is impressive, especially considering this was his senior project (Darmouth stand up!), done on a shoe-string budget with a hustler’s drive and a student’s vision.

Peep these tracks:

“A Sound Supreme” feat. Geologic, J-Incite, Skyzoo & Young Ivy
produced by Illmind

“The Art of Blurring Weekends Together” feat. 8th W1 & Mickey Factz
produced by Sabzi

And, don’t forget to download the shit (FOR FREE) now at WWW.LAUNDROLARGE.COM.
