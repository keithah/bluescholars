Off to Bellingham, WA

12.05.08 | 1 Comment

Ah, Bellingham. We shall meet again.

Closing out 2008 back where it started - home, in the Pacific Northwest, with six December shows to cap off the year before heading back to Honolulu, HI for New Year’s Eve (details forthcoming). Part one goes down tonight in Bellingham, where the crowds go bananas every time. Going all the way back to kicking it with Surge Spitable and the good ol days at the now-defunct Nightlight Lounge.

This is the first official show in more than a year featuring Blue Scholars, Common Market and Gabriel Teodros all on one bill. 360, stand up!
