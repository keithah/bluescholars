THE FRESHLY DIPPED MOVIE THEATER TOUR

10.07.08 | Permalink | 6 Comments
Posted By bluescholars

via Geo’s blog at Prometheusbrown.com:

Three months (47 cities) on the Freshly Dipped Tour circling the US, we carried on the tradition of catching a flick either on a day off or during one of those extra-long gaps between soundcheck and our set. Which added up to 10 theaters in 10 cities. I will never again take it for granted - Seattle has its movie theater game locked down. Here’s a recap of the movies we watched and where we watched them (and links to my reviews).

  • Hellboy: The Golden Army - Mann Chinese 6, Hollywood, CA. Not quite the historic Grauman’s Chinese Theater, but close (a block away).

  • The Dark Knight - AMC Northpark 15, Dallas, TX. The bigger the movie, the bigger the movie theater. This multiplex was fucking huge. Perfect for TDK, wack for almost everything else.

  • The X-Files: I Want to Believe - Regal Kingstowne 16, Alexandria, VA. Another non-descript multiplex. Did get to visit the now-defunct Hippodrome Theater in Baltimore, tho (pictured above).

  • Step Brothers - Regal Charles Towne Square 18, Charleston, SC. Regal again, the McDonald’s of movie theaters.

  • War, Inc. - Midtown Cinema, Harrisburg, PA. This place reminds me of the small, independent movie theaters I went to in the 1980’s (when we weren’t doing the drive-in thing). The screening room seated probably about 25 and the screen was on the same wall as the entrance door.

  • Gonzo: The Life and Work of Hunter S. Thompson - Merrill’s Roxy, Burlington, VT. Burlington kinda reminds me of parts of (the White parts of) Seattle, and the Merrill’s Roxy was kind of like the Landmark Metro in the U-District.

  • Burn After Reading - Sundance Kabuki Theater, San Francisco, CA. The Sundance Kabuki Theater, located in SF’s Japantown is nice. As in, hella bourgie-type nice. The individually cushioned chairs (you can choose your seats on a touch-screen when you purchase your tickets) and drinking area alone are worth it. Still, there’s something hella weird about sitting next to people in suits and dresses drinking wine at a movie.

  • Traitor - Regal Old Mill 16, Bend, OR. One day, the Regal empire will fall. And we will rejoice.

  • Man on Wire - Bijou Art Cinemas, Eugene, OR. The best theater on the whole tour is a remodeled church near the University of Oregon. $6 admission all day. Courtyard w/ fountain, foliage and benches. Small screening rooms that don’t diminish the quality of viewing.

  • Tell No One - Regal Fox Tower 10, Portland, OR. OK, maybe Regal isn’t all bad. This one’s a hybrid Indie-plex for all those movies that Regal doesn’t show in its million other theaters.

BAMBU “EXACT CHANGE”

10.05.08 | Permalink | Comment?
Posted By bluescholars

Our dude Bambu, fresh off a show last night at LA’s Knitting Factory with Common Market and UNI, just dropped a new album: Exact Change (Beatrock). And if you like good shit, then you’re probably going to like it.

And not just because we’re on it. On separate tracks. Sabzi did the production for “Life Line” and Geo is featured at the end of “Quit” (produced by Illmind).

So do yourself a few favors: cop the album, peep the short film Bam featured in this summer, and add this fact to your stack of rumors: Bambu and Sabzi are collaborating on a “mini-album.”

*NOTE TO SEATTLE FOLKS: Catch Bambu with D. Black, Khingz and Nam at The Vera Project on Oct. 18.
