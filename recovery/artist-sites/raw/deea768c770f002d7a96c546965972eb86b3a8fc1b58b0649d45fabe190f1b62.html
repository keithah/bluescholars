ILL-LITERACY + THEOPHILUS LONDON IN NEW YORK NOV 12

11.09.08 | 2 Comments

Throughout our travels, the one crew whose paths we cross most often is ILL-LITERACY. We’re both currently touring the East Coast, but fate took a break and this time, we’ll be barely missing each other. Like when we arrived in DC on election day mere hours after they left. So if you’re in New York on November 12th, go to their show with the Hi-Lifes and Theophilus London at the Crash Mansion, download Dahlak’s new Live From the Boondocks mixtape, cop Ruby’s new book Miss Universe, and tell them I said wassup for me.

NOVEMBER 12TH
Crash Mansion 8 PM
199 Bowery @ Spring St. 
$8 presale $10 at the door

Tickets and info at: www.ill-literacy.com/nyc

www.myspace.com/illliteracy
www.myspace.com/londonwave
www.iLL-Literacy.com
 
iLL-Literacy has covered much ground in a short time, rocking stages from off-Broadway New York to London’s Picadilly, to its home base in the California Bay Area.  News of the amazing talent has drawn attention from HBO’s Def Poetry and MTV, as well as crowds who have witnessed the collective share the stage with the likes of Common, Mos Def, KRS-One, and Yeah Yeah Yeahs.
