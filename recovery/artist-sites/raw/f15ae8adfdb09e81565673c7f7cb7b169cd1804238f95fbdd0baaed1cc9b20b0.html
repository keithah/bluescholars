Globalista Day! SEATAC!

04.24.08 | 8 Comments

this morning we hit up Globlista Day at Global Connections High School. for those who aren’t knowin, GCHS is one of three schools in the Tyee Educational Complex.

Globalista Day is a day of an assembly, workshops ranging from sex education to spoken word and indie music, and no class otherwise. also commonly known as a “hit the blunt and go to the park instead of school” day when we were in high school, but these folk were clearly more evolved.

we played a few cuts. we wonder if people liked it. we think they did.
they had an “Auction For Action” that raised money for “those in need.” we wondered what this meant. either way, we applaud students making the effort to change the community for the better.

Principal Rick Harwood putting the “pal” back in “principal.”

Thomas Goldstein with the Washington Bus.

the homies Khalil (Khingz) and Gabriel (Gabriel) were doin the spoken word workshop after the assembly.

we had a good time folks! to all the students that smiled for the camera: we’re sorry but Sabz’s amateur photography skills made all your flics turn out blurry. next time we’ll bring a real photographer.
