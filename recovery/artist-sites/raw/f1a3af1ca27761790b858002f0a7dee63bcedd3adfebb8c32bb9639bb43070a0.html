The Travels, Tour

Grinnell College, Iowa

04.05.08 | Comment?

“I rock like Herbie Hancock with prosthetic limbs” - Morning of America (off Bayani LP)

Day 9 of 10 on the road. We’re in Grinnell College tonight before heading back home. Turns out one of GC’s distinguished alumni is jazz/fusion legend Herbie Hancock, one-time bandmate of Miles Davis and later an innovator in fusing jazz with contemporary sounds of his day. Put on a copy of his 1973 classic LP Headhunters and hear the influence he’s had on today’s music (including ours). 80’s babies (particularly DJs) will recall his music video for “Rock It” - hence the reference in Morning of America.
