MUSIC: Prometheus Brown “Mga Kababayan” (Tribute to Francis M)

Posted on March 7, 2009
Filed Under Music |

Mga Kababayan (Tribute to Francis M) - Prometheus Brown | Download MP3 (Myspace)

Rest in Power, Francis M (1964-2009). A lo-fi tribute to The Man From Manila, the King of Filipino Rap. Ang lahat ng aking musika, dahil sa likhang impluensya ka. Isulong mo, isulong ko!

Francis M. was down with being Brown with lyrics such as “Mga kababayan ko, dapat lang malaman nyo, bilib ako sa kulay ko, ako ay Pilipino!”. “I am the Man from Manila. Kami ang tinig ng Kayumanggi”. He brought a sense of national pride into Pinoy hip-hop.

Francis M. was looked up to even by Fil-Am hip-hop artists who also strived to grasp their roots while living in the US.

But more than this affirmation of national identity is the serious effort at commenting on social ills. There’s the remarkable “Kaleidoscope World”, reminding us that “some are friends, some are foes, some have some, while some have most.”
