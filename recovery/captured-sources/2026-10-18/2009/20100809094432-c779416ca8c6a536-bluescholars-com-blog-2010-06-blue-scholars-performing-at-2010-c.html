Blue Scholars Performing at 2010 Capitol Hill Block Party (July 23-25)

Posted on June 3, 2010
Filed Under Events | Leave a Comment

Seattle! The complete Capitol Hill Block Party lineup has been announced, and Blue Scholars will be playing the mainstage on the festival’s third and final day (Sunday, July 25). MGMT, Atmosphere and The Dead Weather are some of the big draws, but it’s dope to see the biggest showing for Seattle hip-hop at CHBP, with Macklemore, Victor Shade, Shabazz Palaces, THEESatisfaction, Grynch, Spaceman, State of the Artist, Helladope, Mad Rad, Fresh Espresso, Champagne Champagne, Stahi Bros. (Vitamin D & Maineack B), The Physics, Candidt, and Truckasauras all scheduled to perform.

For full lineup and ticket purchases go hither: www.capitolhillblockparty.com.
