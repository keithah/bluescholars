Das Racist / Mad Decent Mixtape + Video Game

Posted on September 2, 2010
Filed Under News | Leave a Comment

Our incredibly-intelligent-but-pretend-to-be-slobs homies Das Racist are busy as ever with a new video game and forthcoming mixtape. You can try playing the game by clicking on the pic above… The goal is to get to the show before the end of our collab tune “Who’s That Browwn” by running, jumping, ascending elevators, and dodging what appears to be Hawaiian girls throwing leis at you. When I played it, I died in like 30 seconds.

Below is the trailer for the new Das Racist X Mad Decent X Greedhead mixtape “Sit Down, Man” that highlights a track I did for them called “All Tan Everything.” This one’s about to blow people. Away.
