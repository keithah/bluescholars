PHOTOS: Eugene, Oregon (10/21-10/22)

Posted on November 5, 2010
Filed Under Tour | 4 Comments

Aside from this show over the summer, it had been six months since we hit Eugene when we returned to WOW Hall a couple weeks ago. The venue holds historical significance as the home of the first of many stagedives. Cats be crowdsurfing during the interludes. Nobody’s to cool to not sweat. Lots of trees in the air. This past two-night run with Grieves & Budo was no different.

The first night, we had the unfortunate scheduling luck of booking a show on the same night of a nationally-televised University of Oregon football game against UCLA, but the crowd was no less energetic than any other Eugene crowds. The green room has no restroom, and the venue has just one, which made for some interesting conversations with fans every time your boy had to go unload. The second night, however, sold out in advance and the WOW Hall faithful lived up to their rep. It was the first time we’ve done back-to-back shows in Eugene, giving us a chance to peep the city more than we usually get a chance to. Saw Jackass 3 in 2D (fuck 3D!) for $6, hit up Tasty Thai, and got lots of friendly donations of that homegrown Northwest tree. The kind that makes you take the randomest pictures of the littlest things. Like these.

Photos taken w/ Leica M3 50mm f/2 Summicron, Ilford HP5+ 400 (ASA 800) & Fuji Neopan 1600 (ASA 1600)
