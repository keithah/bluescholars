Description
I have a stinking Throat infection so can't enter the MC competition right now, so I thought I'd attempt the DJ comp instead :) Was talking to DJ Moon who said he liked Drum'n'Bass so I attempted to make my own Drum'n'Bass style Remix of the track, although the vocal mixing is more similar to FatBoySlim style Big Beat. anyway I reckon it sounds pretty good, I keep listenning to it over and over. I hope Geologic doesn't mind me speeding up his vocal! This mix took around 3 hours to make, and was great fun, hope you all like it :) Thanks, Sam
