Listen to Prometheus Brown’s (Geo of Blue Scholars) Seahawks song

By
Travis Hay
– Posted 14 hours agoPosted in: News

This is pretty cool.

To celebrate the Seahawks going to the Super Bowl local MC Prometheus Brown (aka Geologic of Blue Scholars) put together “This Ain’t A Seahawks Anthem.” The song uses Marshawn Lynch’s insta-meme of an interview with Deion Sanders as a backdrop over a dope Spekulation beat. Take one listen to the track above and you’ll be all bout that action too.

RELATED: Listen to PUSA’s Seahawks song “This is a Blitz!”

 

If that’s not enough celebratory Seahawks music for your ears, take a listen to the Presidents of the USA Seahawks song from last year “This is a Blitz!” over here or click on the link above. And as Russell Wilson would say “Go Hawks!”
