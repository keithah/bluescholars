LIL' WOODY'S & FOOD & SH*T SPECIAL OF THE WEEK: PHILLYPINO CHEESEBISTEK

January 22, 2015
By Food & Sh*t Fam

Last year, we teamed up with the homies at Lil' Woody's to present the ADOBOBURGER special of the week. And now, we've teamed up again to bring you the PHILLYPINO CHEESEBISTEK, a Philly Cheesesteak style sandwich with calamansi-soy marinated sliced beef, caramelized onion rings, queso fresco, scallions on a butter-toasted hoagie bun.
