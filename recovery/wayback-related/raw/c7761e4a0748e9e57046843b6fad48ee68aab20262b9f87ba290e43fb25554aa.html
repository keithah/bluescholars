Hillydilly.com is proud to present Prometheus Brown of the Blue Scholars & Bambu (The Bar) w/ special guests RA Scion & Rebel live in Vancouver, BC on June 15th, 2012.

*This show is an early show that will run from 7PM to 11PM

Purchase 10$ advance online tickets HERE.

Hard copy tickets are available at Zulu Records & Beatstreet Records.

HEADLINER: The Bar (Promtheus Brown & Bambu)LISTEN: Walk Into A Bar (debut EP)

The Bar is lead by the commanding raps of Seattle’s Prometheus Brown (of the Blue Scholars) and L.A’s Bambu who has built a reputation for being an intense live performer. The combination of these two level headed emcees has created music that you can admire and look up to. Too often these days we find rappers elevated within the public eye that don’t correctly represent that ‘good shit’. Luckily for us Prometheus Brown & Bambu are exactly what rappers should be; a voice for the people. Walk Into A Bar, was their first release that dropped back in the summer of 2011.
