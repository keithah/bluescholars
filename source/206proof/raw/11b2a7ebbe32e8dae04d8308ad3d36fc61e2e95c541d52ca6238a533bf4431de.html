    So within the emerging (and I do mean EMERGING) structure of occupy actions taking place, various open participation working groups are being established as a means to more efficiently interact with the general assembly for democratic decision-making in this very new evolving process. Being that many artists are already involved in this, both directly and indirectly, we proposed last night, to overwhelming support from the assembly, that a Hip Hop working group be established in this structure to:

    1) Spread practical awareness to broader community on how the occupy actions are beginning to organize and collectivize

    2) Provide a channel for artists, crews, organizations to participate and/or shape and/or help define and give clarity to the occupy movement

    3) Through endorsements, performances, or whatever, help spread awareness and keep occupy actions in public consciousness

    ...thats my Julie C off the dome quick answer, Ifthen, but like I said, we just got the go ahead last night and we are meeting tonight at Westlake, 11pm to discuss further and hone in on the specifics of how this will look. Anyone interested, shoot me a text at (425) 223-7787.
