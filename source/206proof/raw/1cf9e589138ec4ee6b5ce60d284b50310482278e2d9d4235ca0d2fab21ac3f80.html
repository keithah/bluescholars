    these guys are some of the reason why I took rap serious and do it to this day:

    Sir Mix-A-Lot: most everybody I knew growing up had a Mix-A-Lot tape but once he put out Baby Got Back, people around the town stopped listening

    Electroshock (aka E-Dawg): I remember seeing "Drop Top" on The Box one day non-stop.. it played about 10-15 times that day

    Kid Sensation: everybody should know the Ken Griffey song, c'mon..

    Self Tightld: first rap group from Seattle where I had seen their flyers & stickers all over the place.. I remember the Pen & Pixel cover with the skeleton in the sky.. I used to carry their flyer around at school and tell people they were from here..

    Black Caesar (Foul Play): I never knew em as Foul Play, but the 1st time I heard em was on the "Nothin To Lose" soundtrack and one of my boys told me they were from Tacoma, I had no clue.. I used to see their sticker posters up all over 84th St. and over on the Hill

    Criminal Nation: first Tacoma cd I ever saw in Sam Goody & Towers.. still fuck wit Wojak to this day, he's a good dude..

    Street Level Records (FTS, IK, Syko): first Seattle acts I saw in Sam Goody & Towers.. back when they had a local section in all of their stores.. IK was the first Street Level cd I ever bought.. "Soggy" is my shit even though I don't smoke wet

    Mr. D.O.G. : first Tacoma rapper I ever saw with a vehicle wrap and I used to get excited when I saw his burban coming down my street past my middle school.. at the time, Bow Wow Records was #1 in the state, even got the key to the city for hip-hop by our mayor..

    Funkdaddy: I remember bein at the homie's house awhile back and the only 2 albums they ever had playin in the background was Master P's "Ice Cream Man" & E-40's "Hall of Game".. I used to look at the booklet and ol boy told me Funkdaddy was from Seattle..

    Felony Entertainment: "Welcome To My City" was the 1st local cd I ever bought and I bumped it damn near everyday.. I was 16

    Ananize (Way Out Records): bought his cd at the B&I in like 2000 and bumped it on the regular.. he had Yukmouth on the album and I was into Yuk's shit at the time so it made sense..

    Kazy D: first transplant rapper out in the 6 that showed me a lot of love.. matter fact, he introduced me to Fatal Lucciauno when dude was only like 16 and we were kickin' bars at a park in the Central District.. Kazy D let me sleep on his couch when I didn't have nothin..

    Drekno: 2nd local cd I ever bought "Marks On My Soul".. I thought it was hot cuz he was one of the only local rappers I heard that was SPITTIN at the time.. one of the producers on there (Rick Ross, the real one) happens to do filmwork now and I work pretty close with him

    DMS: heard of them on a trip out to Portland awhile back and a cat I worked with used to bump em all day in the back room.. I still see Dee.Ale out here doin his thing.. that's a good dude right there

    Azarel: I saw his commercial on BET back during the 2nd Source awards and would see him at the mall handing out flyers & promo cd's.. that shit inspired me

    KutKrome (aka Sonny Bonoho): him & his sister used to have the biggest house parties in the Tac over by 90th St. I was like 11 or 12 the first time I got in one.. he started doing Glitta later on around 99, 2000 and used to tell me about it in Mr. Rags at Tacoma Mall..

    I can't think of any other names at the moment, but I will later.. this thread brings back a couple memories.. people that don't know about these dudes and what they've done for our scene need to get wit the program and know some of the NW hip-hop history
