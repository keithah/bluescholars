    Real industry standard gear, not the mediocre bullshit you buy at Guitar Center. Unfortunately, music has started to sound so bad, most fools can't tell the difference between a cheap mic running into an Mbox and a $7k mic running into a Neve 1073 mic pre and Apogee AD converter.

    I regularly see cats touting their "studio" as being "industry standard' because they went to Guitar Center and got talked into dropping $3k on a Mac, ProTools, some rinky dink microphone and some Auralex foam by some kid just trying to make a commission. Yeah, OK. Half the time those dipshits at GC don't know what they're talking about either.

    The front end on my studio (that would be the mic, cables, mic pre, compressor, mixer and AD/DA converter) is about $20k. THAT IS INDUSTRY STANDARD.

    If you walk into a studio and don't see names like Neve, API, Avalon, Neumann, Apogee, Sony, Manley, high end Focusrite, DBX......then it's probably a joke. Along with the so-called "engineer" running it. Of course, just because somebody has all the tools doesn't mean they know HOW to use them, but most likely if they take their craft seriously enough to have spent that much, then they probably know what they're doing.Indie record company mogul Street Level Records / Hip-Hop producer & engineer Not 2 Sane Productions
