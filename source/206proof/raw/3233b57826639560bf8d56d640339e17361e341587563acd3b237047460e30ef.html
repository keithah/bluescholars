    I have used a Neumann TLM 103 for over 10 years. Really dope microphone in the $600-$1000 range (used/new). I just bought a used Neumann U87 which is the industry standard for recording. What the majority of rap records you listen to are recorded on. They range from $1600-3k (used/new). In my opinion if you're gonna spend $400 on a Rhode or something in that range, save an extra couple bills and get a Neumann. Well worth it in the end.
