    RAPPERS






    5. Avatar - has consistently put out enough quality projects to show he has skills. i have heard him on different types of tracks and seen him still deliver a steady sound while not getting monotonous and rehashed (a common problem for most hood rappers). he knows how to stay in his lane while at same time not staying in a box.

    4. Anonymous - A dope gangster rapper who i haven't heard from in a
    while, used to just drop classic after classic. had a good voice for hip
    hop, didn't have the nasally rapper voice.

    3. Jpinder - I'll be honest, when i first seen pinder rapping he looked like just another local  emohop seattle rapper hanging around the pharmacy during other peoples sessions. much of backpack wax sounded like tracks vita could do in his sleep combined with pinder trying to do his best yeezy impersonation and running out of gas on the beats. Most local guys would be happy with being just that, but pinder wanted something more. I know from that go far track that he was planning something big and was really gonna turn it up, codered was a watershed project for him. I feel like he
    has finally gotten out of his "larvae" stage and is now developed into
    not just a emcee but an artist. those who have seen him work know that
    everything is a calculated move to develop his sound and his brand. it
    is working for him now and the sound seems a lot closer to what he has
    intended. This city is seasoned with the salt of failed rappers vowing to leave the supposed circle jerks of town and make it big, he is actually about to do it.

    2. Props - I honestly don't know why you guys aren't talking about this
    dude. he has all the skills, delivery, subject matter and panache of a
    young jay z (yes i just said that). his approach to songs is his own
    unique style but all his songs have commercial appeal.

    1. Type - probably the balziest artist out of seattle, this is probably
    the guy i would point to as the dude who should be on right now. has
    always delivered quality songs/videos with a message but does it in a
    way that doesn't contradict himself, or make him look snobby. he seems
    totally in on the joke the entire time (see backpack). To write him off
    as simply a comic rapper would be a mistake. Less we forget, before
    there was same love there was the man crush video; a lighter take at the
    same topic. he's also beaten everyone to the punch about addiction
    (always fuckin drunk)  and dv (my girlfriend beats me). sometimes its
    complete tongue in cheek and sometimes its played as such but you can
    tell by the lyrics that its something else. he is the andy kaufman of
    seattle hip hop.


    PRODUCERS: all you niggas sound alike. its not your fault. you make different stuff and the local rappers your working with just say "thats coo, you got any beats that sound like what everyone else is doing?"

    work with new people.
