    Bio
    Animal Farm is one of the hottest groups to burst upon the northwest hip-hop scene, creating a significant buzz in just three years after joining forces. The group has quickly developed a large regional following after performing live throughout the west coast with the likes of KRS-One, GZA and RZA from Wu-Tang Clan, Brand Nubian, Aceyalone, Mike Jones, 2 Live Crew, Tha Alkaholiks, and many others. Animal Farm's new album, "The Unknown", is currently receiving radio play throughout the country and peaked at #9 this week on the CMJ Hip-Hop Charts.

    The group is a collaboration of MCs from Portland and Eugene, OR, and was voted the Eugene Weekly's best hip-hop group in 2006 and 2007. Animal Farm was also recently praised in Urb Magazine's Next 1000, which claimed "The animated Animal Farm achieves a classically feel-good tone."

    The much anticipated debut album will be available in stores on July 15th, 2008 and will feature some great surprises and collaborative efforts, including the legendary KRS-One, as well as Nightclubber Lang from Rhymesayers' Boom Bap Project. The album has already been featured on numerous prominent websites, including hiphopdx.com, urb.com, hhnlive.com, and 2dopeboyz.com, among others.

    Animal Farm is quickly gaining popularity due to a live stage show and group camaraderie. In promoting the new album worldwide through Focused Noise Productions and Burnside/ IODA Distribution, the whole world will soon find out about Animal Farm.

    Animal Farm Feat. Krs-One – Peace

    I know it's easy for people to come on and criticize, so I figured I would get on and explain a few things, as a producer and MC from Animal Farm.

    1. I thought the "Peace is better than whores" line was ridiculous myself. It's funny because it's true.

    2. This song is definitely not the best song on the album. Not to say that I'm not proud of it, because I still like the track. However, I'd say there are 7 or 8 songs that are better on the album. We put this song out there because people will check it just for the KRS feature, which has worked to our advantage. And you can talk shit all you want about KRS... the man's still a legend and can rock a crowd like no other MC.

    3. I highly suggest you check out some of the rest of the album (myspace.com/animalfarmrecords). I think all 4 of the songs on there are really strong. If you check that out and still aren't impressed, then I guess you just have shitty taste in music. If you feel it, you can buy it through Amazon, iTunes, ughh, Circuit City, Easy Street, Sonic Boom, etc.linky
