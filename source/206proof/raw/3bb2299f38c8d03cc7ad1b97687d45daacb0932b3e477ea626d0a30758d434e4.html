    May 25th at Chop Suey, One Night Only:

    ONE BE LO x BLU x MACKLEMORE x THE PHYSICS

    ONE BE Lo is an absolutely vicious MC, a stylist like few can claim- and a longtime favorite of the underground. After dropping a couple megatons on the independent scene with the debut of his former group Binary Star, Lo went on to release a string of LPs that highlighted his immaculate flow and stark storytelling. No wonder Seattle can’t ever seem to get enough of this dude.

    LA-based emceeBLU in one year went from “virtual unknown” to “godsend”- his LP with high-powered producer Exile Below The Heavens was a much-needed breath of freshness that scraped the top of many a critic’s annual Best Of lists- and was widely proclaimed an instant classic by heads worldwide.

    MACKLEMORE is a Seattle institution- a humble, humorous MC with more humanity than most I-practice-lookin-hardrocks could muster in 12 life bids. Mack’s The Language Of My World not only made him one of Seattle’s favorite MC’s- it was one of those albums that set new standards for local hiphop music.

    THE PHYSICS are a crew everybody can agree on- THIG NATURAL’s witty, whip-smart MC’ing meshed with JUST D’AMATO’s sparkling sonics. Last year’s Future Talk sounds barely removed from classic Seattle hiphop as put forth in the essential 206 compilations of the 90’s.

    This show promises to be one of the year’s best lineups, get your tickets now.

    All 206Proof Members will get tickets for $8 (25% discount) for both you AND one guest.

    To get your discount:
    1) Email: marc@integralmusicgroup.com with you and your guest's first and last name.
    2) When you arrive at the show, let the door person know you are on the 206Proof Discount List.

    This is NOT presale, you pay the $8 at the door.Batter Up
