    This summer PGP motorsports park in Kent will be hosting a monthly drifting showcase featuring some of the northwests best drivers. The last couple events had drivers from Washington, Oregon and Canada with between 15 - 30 cars participating. They've been a blast so far, and the last one earlier this month had a suprermoto demo also. The park charges $15 for spectators. There is a beer garden for the 21+ crowd, concessions, vendors and a DJ to keep the energy up.

    The next ones will be this Saturday - 7/3, and the first Saturday in August - 8/7.

    Gates open at 4pm with drifting going on from 5pm to about 9pm.

    Pacific Grand Prix
    31001 144th Ave SE
    Kent, WA 98042
    253.639.7223

    www.pacificgp.com
