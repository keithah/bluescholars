    Mark your calendars, Seattle: on October 20-23, 2010 the inaugural City Arts Fest goes down. Four days, 18 venues, 40+ music acts

    NW hip-hop takes over Seattle's historic Paramount Theater on Oct. 20 with a show headlined by BLUE SCHOLARS, plus special guest Brother Ali, and featuring Macklemore & Ryan Lewis, Fresh Espresso, Mash Hall, & hosted by Tilson.

    This show marks the first time a Seattle hip hop act has headlined the historic Paramount theater, a major accomplishment for Blue Scholars and a break-out show for the whole exploding Seattle hip hop scene.

    Passes starting at $60 will go on sale on August 7th at www.cityartsfest.com. Passes are limited, so get them asap!

    Tickets to individual shows will be available starting August 28th.

    See www.cityartsfest.com for the full line up and schedule.

    Get the latest on Blue Scholars including new songs for free download at www.bluescholars.com
