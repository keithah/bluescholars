    Tacoma Hip Hop Music Videos 2012 - article by D. "Sic ill" Jackson
    Read official article here - http://www.examiner.com/article/tacoma-hip-hop-music-videos-2012


    The Tacoma area (right beneath Seattle, WA) has an unpcoming scene of hip hop talent, much of which is promoted through music videos on youtube and vimeo. 

    Some rappers even connected with mainstream artists. Young Meez features both Guce and Philthy Rich, two artists from the bay considered influencial in the current westcoast rap scene. Young Fame features Nipsey Hussle of southern L.A. in his new video. Leezy Soprano has guest features with westcoast legends Kokane and Kurupt of "Doggpound" fame. 

    In 2012 almost every significant artist in the area made a music video or put something on youtube. Some artists made a large amount of videos this year, namely Leezy Soprano, Cameron Couch, Lil Ripp and Sic ill.

    The Criteria was that the video be uploaded on youtube January 1, 2012 or later and had to be an artist that "reps" Tacoma greater area. The list below is in no particular order.
