    My advice on tattoos.
    Research your artist. There are so many different styles of tattoo (portrait, abstract, color, black and white, traditional, even more traditional, graffiti, etc)
    You wouldn't want to go get a pin-up devil girl from someone that is really good at doing koi/dragon/cherry blossom/ bs. I've gotten tattoos from people who generally were REALLY good artists, but they weren't interested in what I wanted so it looked shitty and ended up getting covered up.

    If you ask an artist to do something specifically and they don't either because they aren't able to or they refuse to, they may not be the artist for you. My friend didn't shop around and ended up with a 2 inch in diameter black dot on her leg. We told her to find another artist and she didn't and she looks like a fool because of it.

    Once you find an artist that could do what you want, take your ideas and make them feel like you, talk about drawing something up for you. Don't pay too much for the drawing, I feel that a decent artist would just effin do it because it is part of the job description. You shouldn't have to pay 50 bucks on a piece of shit outline that you don't want tattooed on you.

    Don't listen to other peoples shit about how stupid a skyline is. Do what you want to do. A tattoo doesn't have to mean anything, and it certainly doesn't have to mean shit to anyone else. (I say this because I am tired of people wanting me to define my tattoos to them. I am also tired of being groped by junky bitches at the gas station that feel they have the right to pull by shirt down to stare at my chest.)
