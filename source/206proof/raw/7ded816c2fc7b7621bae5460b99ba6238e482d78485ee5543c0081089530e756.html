    -Mr. Dibia$e -

    Hailing from LA's very own Watt's, Dibiase a.k.a Diabolic's productions run the gambit from 8 bit video game classics to seductive soul to raw grungy bangers this guy has no limitations no sample is safe. This 12 time beat battle champion and runner up of the Los Angeles RED BULL BIG TUNE BATTLE.

    "Originally 1 started makin beats in 95 with an 8 second sampler and dr. rythm drum machine to compliment his ryhming which I still do from time to time equally as sharp.Around late 97 I got my hands on a mpc 2000 and a tascam 4 track and made so many dusty projects with the homie Ant Dog and SWAMP 7 crew. Spendin endless nights in the garage crankin them out learning from mistakes i had no tutors or wings to get takin under. Around 98 i felt comfortable exposin my music at PROJECT BLOWED even tho i was around at GOOD LIFE ,and THE O.G. UNITY which started the whole madness. But i was gettin a decent response at BLOWED in the golden age which was super cut throat at the time so that helped in my development. then from 2000 to 2004 i linked up with Aspect 1 and we was in this camp MISSIN PAGE CREW which had a strong run openin up for acts like MF DOOM , GZA , LIVING LEGENDS, J ZONE just to name a few. Then 05 i started focusin more on production enterin beat battles and going to this weekly function SKETCHBOOK where the mecca of this l.a. beat scene started. I roll up there with the trusty boombox and we have a roundtable of beats outside from RAS G, KUTMAH, FLY LO, GEORGIA ANNE MULDROW, TAARACH , TAKE , GAS LAMP, SACRED would come with tapes or cds with there efforts of the week.I was also workin with the PUSHER PRICE camp at this time makin joints, doing showes. Then in late 07 i got hit up DECAY a m.c. out chicago about rockin to this MARIO GAXALY joint i had on the myspace page. He laced it up with SELFISH and we done many joints after that throughtout the year. Then they found out i spit cause i let them hear this BLACK MANTIS EP.A little after that i got put on the GREEN LLAMAS which is scatter all over the globe from n.y. to florida to midwest and now cali. And i also did pro duction for U-N-I yeah im the dood who made BEAUTIFUL DAY which is gettin major buzz on M -TV and numerous mixtapes,andCASTLEVANIA .But in a time where heads is relyin on who they know to get a buzz i only relyed on my talent and skill strictly throughout the years, and finally its startin to pay off well somewhat."
