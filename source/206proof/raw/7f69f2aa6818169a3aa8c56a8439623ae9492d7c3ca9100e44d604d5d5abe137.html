    How To Find Us...

    The Cornerstone is held every first Saturday of the month at The Faire Gallery and Cafe around 7:30 to Midnight. The Faire Gallery and Cafe is located on Capitol Hill at:

    1351 East Olive Way, Seattle, Washington 98122
