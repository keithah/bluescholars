"Hey guys, I know Macklemore is about to blow up...but our stance is going to be that we are butt-hurt because the music that we have been championing is way better and is not blowing up."

    Despite the fact that I basically agree with Grandy's opinions of Macklemore's music (albeit not so vehemently) It is very easy for me to look at Mack objectively and decide whole heartedly that what he is doing is GOOD for the Seattle scene.
