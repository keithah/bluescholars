    That opening beat is a slapper. I like the minimalism in the drums and the reverb on the snare. I think your flow is a little awkward sounding on the first verse but then you get comfortable on the second verse. I think Dice's hook is super sick.

    Nothing To Prove is dope, but I was hoping that it would be a different type of beat because it's similar to the first beat in style. Hook is alright. Notion kills it on his verse but he's probably one of the best in town on a track like this to be honest. Grynch closes out the track nicely as well.

    On Wake Up you sound the most comfortable, and I think the BPM of that track and the drums really help you sound good. You also use a less repetitive cadence on your verse. I like the second verse but whoever finishes it sounds the best over this beat.

    Factor is a little better because the beat sounds way different from the rest of the album. Know Choice has a pretty dope verse though it has a couple corny lines. This is the best hook on the album besides Dice's to me. Ripynt spits pretty sick on it too. You sound good on this one, but there's a change-up in your verse you could've emphasized by switching up the cadence you were rapping in, but didn't and it just made your verse draw out a bit.

    Let's Talk is obviously filthy and a good way to finish the track. Spaceman's verse is a standout. Again though I think you sound a little awkward again on this track and it's emphasized by putting yourself between Space and Brain

    Overall I like it. I probably wouldn't bump it on the regular, but the production is tight and the tracks sound polished. I wish there was a more varied soundscape overall to the project though.
