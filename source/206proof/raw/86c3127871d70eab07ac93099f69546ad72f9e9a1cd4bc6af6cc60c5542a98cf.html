    good news first or bad news first?

    GOOD NEWS:

    *if "easy listening" is your thing, then this is the album for you...

    **the album was mixed well, and sounds professional overall...

    ***the featured artists played their positions well, and the production fit Grynch almost perfectly.

    ****the album isn't OFFENSIVE...(perhaps the reason why one of these was nowhere to be found:



    *****a "clean cut" album in general...

    BAD NEWS:

    *Grynch sounds like kinda like NAS to me...(i don't think lyrically he has distinguished his delivery from that of "basic" emcee...in other words, he don't got much "style"...)

    **the album is exactly what i DON'T LIKE hearing from Seattle hip hop artists...

    meaning...

    in general...

    IT SOUND JUST LIKE A TYPICAL "SEATTLE" HIP HOP ALBUM...e.g., Seattle Sound

    ***I doubt other cities (L.A., ATLANTA, NEW YORK) would find this album "exceptional"...or even "pretty tight" for that matter...in other words, i don't see anybody else besides Seattle actually "embracing" "Chemistry"...

    FINAL WORDS / RATING:

    2 mics out of 5

    Personally? This one is a "DD" to me...not meaning "Dyme Def"; but DOWNLOAD AND DELETE...
