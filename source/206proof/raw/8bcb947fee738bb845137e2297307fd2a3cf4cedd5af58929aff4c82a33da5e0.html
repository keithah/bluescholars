    REVIEW: Very dope lyrics and the samples add a "funk" feel to this. J. Dilla & 9th Wonder fans will feel their beat selection. EvergreenOne's sarcastic approach contrasts nicely with Todd Sykes' straight to the point way of rhyming. Even with minimalist beats like "Hell No" they keep your ear prepared to hear them spit bars, which in today's beat driven culture is rare. Track 7 "Untitled" shows that they can get deep with great songs for the women also. The climax is when "90's shit" appears and reminds you their style is also catchy. 12 tracks is enough to show you what they're about - solid rhymes - and as in track 12's title "Feel Good Music". These guys got a unique feel to them, that matches perfectly with the northwest conscious rap movement. I'll give this a couple tweets and make sure everyone in Darxiide got a copy. "Sic ill"
