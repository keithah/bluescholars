    (from The Stranger) - Silas's ironclad reputation throughout the town—as part of Silent Lambs Project (with the one and only Jace Ecaj), formerly of Blind Council, and as a soloist—is one of a lyrical iconoclast, perhaps Seattle's most original MC, who sports an utterly unique, jagged flow like lesser cats would a gold chain. Sentinel will serve only to put a varnish on that rep, as it's easily as strong as any of the material that he or SLP have put out. The sound—provided expertly by Dropcast Music, Specs One, BeanOne, Vitamin D, and Amp Fire—is street level yet subterranean, laced with the kind of millennial-dread lyrical exercise that wouldn't sound out of place on a Def Jux release, but could hardly be classified as Jansport material.
