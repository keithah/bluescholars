    @MrDOG

    I went to the Art Institute for video, but I don't really do anything. I just like the art of music videos. I mean its washed out because the sun was too bright so there's too much light on the subjects of the video which makes things appear super bright white and devoid of their color. It also makes some colors at the red end of the spectrum very bold and garish. Generally when shooting professionally in a really bright environment, the lighting people on set will have some sort of shades to absorb light and reduce the amount in the frame, and modern cameras have some adjustments you can make to help this as well. It's why when you are on a low budget you try not to shoot right in the middle of the day when its super bright.

    Some people may not even notice, but I'm really picky when it comes to music videos. However, props on the vid because they take a lot of work, and it's good to see so many people making them here in the northwest recently.

    Even though I am not this song's target audience, I can appreciate it because for what it is, it is clean and polished and the people who regularly listen to rap like this will like it a lot.
