    We're excited to bring the Bloody Radio to you live. Our album drops Sept. 11th, 2007 on Rhymesayers Entertainment. Thanks to all our fans that have supported from the jump. See you soon!


    After a year off the road, Atmosphere will perform twenty seven shows in the U.S from September 14 - October 16, 2007 with fellow Rhymesayers label mates Grayskul and Mac Lethal, and special guest Luckyiam of Living Legends. Bringing over four hours of music with four independent Hip Hop artists, “Everybody Loves A Clown” is sure to be fun for the whole family!


    Also, for the first time ever, a limited number of tickets for each show have been held exclusively for Atmosphere's fans. By purchasing tickets through the presale, fans will be able to get tickets before the general public on sale date and save money on service charges. In addition, $1.00 from every ticket sold through the presale will go to a relief fund for victims of the I-35 bridge collapse in Minneapolis. The special presale tickets will be available this Wednesday, August 15, 2007 at 12:00PM local time at: http://atmosphere.ducatking.com


    The national public on sale for all “Everybody Loves A Clown” tour dates will be Saturday, August 18, 2007.


    All artists will be supporting new projects: Atmosphere, Sad Clown Bad Fall Number 10 (limited tour series EP) and dont forget Atmosphere also has a new album on the way When Life Gives You Lemons... coming next spring, Grayskul's Bloody Radio available on September 11, Mac Lethal's 11:11 out October 9, 2007 and Luckyiam’s Most Likely To Succeed in stores now.


    Everybody loves a clown, coming to a venue near you.
