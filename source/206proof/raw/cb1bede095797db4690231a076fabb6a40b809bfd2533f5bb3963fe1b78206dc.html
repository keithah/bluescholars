    Thanks for posting this Billy. I thought it was an interesting read. Not likely to be discussed in anything like a worthwhile way on the proof, but...
    It's a minor quibble, but I feel like conversations about this stuff would be so much easier if we used the word "prejudice" to refer to people treating and thinking about one another differently based on race, and the word "racism" to refer to systems of oppression. In this article (and most everywhere else) "racism" is used to refer to both things, making the intended meaning unclear. They're obviously related, but definitely different from one another, and calling both racism leads to people talking past each other, as in

    Person A: "I don't see color all I see is people"
    Person B: "shut the fuck up asshole that's hella racist."
    Person A: "What are you talking about that's the opposite of racist"
    and they're both right but unlikely to have any kind of productive conversation
