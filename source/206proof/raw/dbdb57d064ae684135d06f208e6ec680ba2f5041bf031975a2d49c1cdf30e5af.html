    It's not unreasonable to point out the gender disparity in hiphop. It's a legitimate question, and even if the disparity has a legitimate, completely non-sexist explanation, avoiding the issue won't help. Skill is skill, yeah. But somehow it's harder for our culture to properly recognize and acknowledge professional skill in a female... Women still get paid less than men do, for doing the same work (in general). There are definitely female performers who make me wince... but women are playing a rigged game in hiphop, and in our culture. It doesn't make women subject to less rigorous standards of talent... but it does call into question the rules of the game.
