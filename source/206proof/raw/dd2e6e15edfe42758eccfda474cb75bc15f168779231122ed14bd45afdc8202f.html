    Great Opportunity & Exposure For NW Artists:


    Some of you may (or may not remember) a DVD released around 2000 called "I-5 North The Hiphopumentary" (the reason all the "I-5 North" pendants and "I-5 North" references are so flagrant today). Apple sponsored the project, the content was licensed to MTV2, I had worldwide distribution, and sold a decent amount of product internationally, and stateside in Borders, Barnes & Nobles, Circuit City, Best Buy, etc... It really opened a lot of doors for me (Littleton "Little Tha Soundbwoy" Miller), others involved, helped me establish myself as a Director/Cinematographer/Editor, and paved the way for other similar projects/products. The 1st DVD also featured an audio track with Tupac Shakur (produced by me) and is currently available in iTunes and the original DVD still has a pretty good Amazon.com ranking as well - it still sells after over 10 years. I've finally decided (after much debate) to do another "I-5 North" - this time with an iPhone App (with ongoing content), and a soundtrack. I will have a music video section in the iPhone App for Artists and Directors to showcase their work as well.

    If any NW (or Westcoast) Artists, Labels, or Directors are SERIOUS about being involved in this project, please make it known in this forum and I will reach out to you.

    Little "Tha Soundbwoy"
