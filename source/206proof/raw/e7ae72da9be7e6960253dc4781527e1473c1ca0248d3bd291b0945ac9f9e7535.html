    I definitely remember the Ice Cube show. I rolled with Supreme & I can remember being outside on the side in a cipher with Scarface freestylin'. We really wanted to see Del & as we walked in he was gettin' boo'd off stage. I'll never forget that night because E-Rok said that was the first time he saw me & of course it was Carhart suit, Black Timbs, Gucci Links & Bracelets, Gold fronts, & Skully (Beanie). I think I kinda stuck out like a sore thumb in a sea of Gz. It was a pretty dope show though & I remember checkin WC & them & Coolio was there as the hype man in MADD Circle. Devon.. I wasn't around for that NWA, but I heard alot of stories. I remember going & it being kinda hectic & some craziness poppin off at DJ Quik in 91 at Paramount too. The funny thing is I spun the sold out HBO Def Comedy Jam show hosted by Bernie Mac (RIP) & it was a totally different vibe. Folks all dressed up. I got stories for days from that, but back to the subject.


    As far as the incident. We were outside when it popped off. I think we had already went to the whip for the burner so we weren't trippin' when they started bustin' & everybody started runnin' (in retrospect we should've been smarter). We just stood there actin' hard. You gotta understand this was a different time. You practiced lookin' hard like E40 said plus you kinda stayed strapped just in case somebody tried to jack for dumb shit like a Starter jacket. There were a lot of little knuckleheads on a mission back then. Now you can slip & just mob down to Cheesecake Factory dolo without a care.

    The thing was out of all the shootouts & funk I've seen this one was the wildest in terms of so many people being out in front of the venue & watching the sea of people just switch tides like 3 times as it went down. It was really kinda hectic, but nothing comapred to the Washington Dance Academy party in 94. We were promoting our Black Moon show & the whole parking lot was full outside & when the gunfire started I swear you heard every known weapon let off & the popo came from around every corner within 30 seconds like 200 deep. I'll never forget that one. They were like in stand by mode.myspace.com/djbmello
