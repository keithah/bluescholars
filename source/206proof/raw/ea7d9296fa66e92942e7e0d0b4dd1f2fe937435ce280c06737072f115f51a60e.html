    i was about 20 blocks away, not even, when it happened. Right down 5th ave, which went straight through manhattan to the trade center. I was on the subway with my homegirl, we were late for class ( was in college then), and we went over the bridge into manhattan (coming from brooklyn) and the full view of the skyline was right there, like every day - and the towers were there, in check.

    We get in the tunnel, and immediately, I mean like, the train wasnt even fully pulled INTO the tunnel, and the train just STOPS, like, as if the conductor slammed on his breaks. We sit there for almost 20 minutes. Me and my girl are freaking cuz we are already late.
    We get off the train at Union Square, 14th street, and dont bother stopping to see why all these people are out of their cars, buses,windows, staring south, up at the sky, almost like in War of the Worlds. I have the distinct memory of almost getting onto 5th ave and seeing every person in my school hanging out of the windows staring south on 5th. We RUN 2 blocks to school, and when we get onto 5th ave on the corner, I just see it...the second plane had JUST hit...the mushroom cloud was breaking up...I spun my girl around, and we just stared...'

    it didnt look real, it looked like a movie...you just saw these GAPING black holes in the buildings, and they were explanding, RAPIDLY - it was the floors just collapsing on each other...you saw things coming off the buildings, everything...and at this point, up 5th ave, you saw business people covered in white power just walking dazed pasted you uptown.

    No cell phones were working because the plane knocked out the gigantic antenna on top of the trade center, which supplied almost all of manhattan with cell service...No one could get ahold of their fam/ friends - so i hear my girl on the pay phone with her fam going "they hit the pentagon too?" and we just freaked, like Independance Day, like, they are going to start dropping bombs from the sky...so we run into my schools building, and our floor was the top floor, which faced South right onto the towers..."perfect" view if you can call it that

    and thats when tower 1 fell - all you see is the top of the building dropping out, and a white cloud come up at the same time. You had no idea what was going on cuz the smoke covered the view immediately...we run back downstairs to the street...and by the time we got there, there was only one tower. Now, as a new yorker, thats like suddenly waking up with one leg...or if all of a sudden the rings on Saturn were just...gone one day. Seeing only ONE tower from the TWIN towers? Very shortly after, the second tower fell, and then there was just..nothing down 5th ave...in NY, you orient yourself by 5th ave...Twin Towers = South, Empire State = North, and you can see them from anywhere. Before the 2nd one fell, it looked, literally, like the building was going to break in two, the gigantic, gaping hole in the side of the building had just become immense, from the floors dropping out, and it took up the corner of two sides of the building.

    We walked home that day, to brooklyn, no cell service, no subways, they closed the bridges to cars... along the williamsburg bridge with 20000 other people - the skyline was just overtaken with smoke billowing out over brooklyn across the water, it looked like the entire downtown area was just crushed and destroyed...and for about the next year, you smelled "Burning world trade center" in brooklyn and in manhattan, a smell i could never get out of my nose.
