    Peace Everyone,

    My name is DJ Pete Marriott and my new radio mix show Generation Next airs for 20 hours, 5 nights a week. I’d like to think what makes my new show special is the fact that I’m the only radio DJ with a daily time slot that plays mostly indie and unsigned hip hop/soul/electronic music. Let’s all be honest, the best urban music comes from the underground and as a DJ I want to break nothing but the best music to the people.

    Generation Next is a positive step for any artist from any background to get heard by the people and we’re preparing to launch an impressive guerilla marketing campaign to promote this particular show.

    My number one rule is; I don’t practice payola, so if you submit your music, and I actually like it, I will spin it. By not accepting money or gifts to play music, it insures fair airplay for those of you who seek exposure to a wide audience. So the better your music is the more spins you’ll get from me.

    I’m not only a radio mix show DJ but I’m also the PD (Program Director) for Fudge FM, a new 24-Hour Radio Station that’s making it’s debut broadcast Election Day 2008 (November 4th, 2008). If you’re a Artist, Producer, Manager or Label Owner I strongly suggest you read my blog carefully and follow the directions to insure your success for airplay. Thank you all for participating, good luck and I look forward to listening to your music in the very near future.

    Respectfully,
    DJ Pete Marriott
