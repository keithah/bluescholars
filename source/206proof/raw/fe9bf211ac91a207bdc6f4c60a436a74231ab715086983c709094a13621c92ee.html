    If you want to put a certain plug in on only a group of tracks, you create an aux track, then you select the input of that aux track to bus 1 (or 1/2 if its stereo). Then you have to change each of the outputs in the group of tracks you have to bus 1 (or 1/2). Then all the plug ins you apply to that aux track will apply to the tracks that are bus'd to that aux track. If you're trying to have an EQ and a Compressor on your whole mix, you need to create a Master Fader and apply it to that. Your Master Fader will tell you if your mix down is clipping and let you add what ever plug in you want to the entire mix.
