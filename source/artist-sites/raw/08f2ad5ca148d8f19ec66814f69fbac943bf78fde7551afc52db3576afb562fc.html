HAWAII: SECOND (ALL-AGES) SHOW ADDED!

06.30.08 | Comment?

Thank you to everybody who came out Saturday night for our first (and sold out) show in Hawaii at Next Door. It was a good type of hot, the vibe was undeniable and we got photos and video forthcoming courtesy of our folks at Honozoolu. Big up to Jake Miyasato (GenuineHI), who put us on for his last show in Hawaii before he comes out to do his thing in the Northwest.

So… for those who couldn’t make it on Saturday or for anyone under 21, we’ve decided to do it again on Monday night!

DUE TO POPULAR DEMAND AFTER ROCKING A SOLD-OUT SHOW AT NEXT DOOR ON SATURDAY,
WE HAVE ADDED A SECOND SHOW FOR BLUE SCHOLARS TOMORROW.

THANK YOU FOR HELPING SPREAD THE WORD. ONLINE PUBLICATION IS REQUESTED IF POSSIBLE. PLEASE SEE ATTACHED FLYERS.

Monday, June 30th, 2008
6pm – 9pm
ALL AGES WELCOME
$10 at the door

University of Hawaii at Manoa
Campus Center Ballroom
UH Campus Center (3rd Floor)
