EMP Pop Conference

04.11.08 | Comment?

Don’t be fooled by the bubble-gum sounding conference title - the EMP Pop Conference kicked off Thursday, April 10 and runs through Sunday at the almost-historic and massively expensive Experience Music Project. Panels, workshops, networking and all that industry goodness - but with a focus on social and economic impact. Sounds like a very Seattle thing to do. So if you’re in town, peep the conference. It’s free and open to all-ages - just register at www.empsfm.org.

This one sounds good:

Colorism
Moderated by: Oliver Wang
Venue: Level 3
Featuring:
Jalylah Burrell, “’No Time For Segregatin’: African-American Colorism in Pop Music”
Wilfredo Gomez, “Mas Negro Que Los Negros: Bordering Blackness and the Politics of the Imagination”
Lauren Onkey, “’No Carnival in Britain’: Black Immigration and the Rise of Rock & Blues in 1950s England”
Peter Scholtes, “Hi Yo Silver, Purple Rain: The Color of Minneapolis Rock and Roll, From Integrated Bands to Segregated Clubs”

And so does this:

Lunch Session - KEXP Presents Blue Scholars

Seattle’s Blue Scholars are at the forefront of politically radical Northwest hip-hop. Filipino-American emcee Geologic and Persian-American DJ/producer Sabzi are University of Washington graduates grounded by their backgrounds as second-generation sons of working-class immigrants. These partners balance militant, yet personal lyricism with a sound that updates the classic boom-bap; Marxist theory with Baha’i spirituality; and musical influences ranging from Thelonius Monk and Aphex Twin to Marvin Gaye and J Dilla. Their most recent album is Bayani (Massline/Rawkus).
Venue: Sky Church
Featuring:
Sabzi
Geologic
