SIFF Blog: Young People Fucking (Martin Gero, 2008)

Posted on June 2, 2008
Filed Under Film Reviews, SIFF 2008 |

Young People Fucking (2008)
dir. Martin Gero
Canada
90 min

Martin Gero (writer, Stargate: Atlantis) makes his directorial debut with Young (White, Hetero) People Fucking (parenthetical description added) - a borderline-typical sex and dialogue, dialogue and sex vignette film. But it mostly works because its witty (enough), the couples (and one threesome) are believable and, most of all, witty believable American sex films are in short supply. Oh wait - it’s a Canadian film. No wonder.

What also made it work, like good stand-up comedy, is that it drew most of its laughter from familiarity rather than shock-value (except for maybe one scene, which I won’t describe… it hurts to even think about it). One couple was fucking to what I believe to be Kool Keith’s Black Elvis/Lost in Space album. Made me wonder if anyone out there fucks to our music (besides the homies who volunteered such information without my asking).

Walking around before it started, I was curious about the demographics of the crowd that packed out the Egyptian Theatre to watch Y(WH)PF. There isn’t a big marketing campaign around the flick, so whoever showed up must’ve had a spark go off in their brain when they saw the title and description (guilty). Many were Y(WH) (male/female) couples, and I spotted a few perv-looking loners, especially up near the front. I’m also starting to notice that indie relationship flicks attract a disproportionately large number of White guy-Asian girl couples (I’m not judging, I’m just saying).

Though there’s nothing innovative or groundbreaking that Y(WH)PF has to offer, a non-porno grown-people sex flick is still entertaining when done right, or at least adequately. Or maybe I’m being too generous after catching the occasional rom-com with the lady (we take turns choosing movies) and always wishing there was more fucking to make up for the cheese.
