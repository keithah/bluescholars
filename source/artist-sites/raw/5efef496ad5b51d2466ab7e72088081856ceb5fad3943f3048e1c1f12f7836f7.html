Inception: 5-Word Reviews, By You

August 4th, 2010 | 2 Comments »

As far as summer mega-multiplex fare goes, I dug Inception. The overly-epic Hans Zimmer score (the Jock Jams of cinema) and the ensuing flood of overananalysis among many of the film’s champions? Not so much. Most of what I’d want to say was already written in this dope review by Devin Faraci (CHUD.com) anyway. Other than that, I was simply entertained, and nothing more. So instead of writing the billionth review, I incepted the idea into your brain that you’d send me a five-word review of the film. Which led to me receiving an overwhelming grip of responses via Facebook and Twitter. Here are a few of them.
