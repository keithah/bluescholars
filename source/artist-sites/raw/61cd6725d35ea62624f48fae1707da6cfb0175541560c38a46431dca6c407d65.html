Blue Scholars do SIFF

05.29.08 | Comment?

Our spring touring schedule is finally wrapping up, and we’re off for most of early June before we head to Hawaii (late June) and on tour this summer with Hieroglyphics. Just enough downtime to hit up the Seattle International Film Festival (May 22 - June 15). So, I’ll be putting this press pass to use, watching 20 films in 20 days and writing about it on my blog.

Check Prometheusbrown.com for Geo’s SIFF blog. The first film review is up now.
