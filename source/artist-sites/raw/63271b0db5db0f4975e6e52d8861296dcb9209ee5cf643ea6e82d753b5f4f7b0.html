Trailer: Hoodrats 2

Posted on December 23, 2008
Filed Under Trailers |

I’ve never seen Hoodrats, but I’m on the lookout for a DVD copy for Hoodrats 2. Why? Mostly… okay, only because it stars the Seattle homie and former Supersonics dancer Donnabelle Mortel, who was in the middle of filming during our stop in LA on the Freshly Dipped Tour this past July.

Plus, I’ve always rooted for more dark-skinned Pinay sisters to appear onscreen. Any screen. To balance out all the pasty ones. Even if they gotta wield bats to do it.
