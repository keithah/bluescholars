BIO CONTACT
BIOCONTACT
 
 

Prometheus Brown aka Geo fka Geologic began rapping before the Kingdome was demolished. Around the time the USA Patriot Act was signed into law, he formed the seminal Seattle hip-hop duo Blue Scholars with DJ/producer Sabzi, releasing 3 full-length albums and touring extensively between the beginning of the US-led invasion of Iraq and end of the 13th b’ak’tun of the Mayan calendar. He officially joined the Beatrock Music family during the height of the Occupy Wall Street movement, joining with label mate Bambu that same year to release the album Prometheus Brown and Bambu Walk into a Bar. With the addition of DJ Nphared, the trio became The Bar and subsequently released the Barkada LP and Son of Barkada EP shortly before Marshawn Lynch’s first retirement. When not rapping, which, these days, is most days, he co-runs Hood Famous Bakeshop and Hood Famous Cafe + Bar along with his wife, Chera. On the 856th day of Donald Trump’s presidency, he will release his first solo Beatrock Music project: Tagsibol, the first of a series of four three-song EPs to be released each season throughout the year.
