SEATTLE SHOW CANCELED (THURSDAY, DEC 18)

12.18.08 | Comment?

One foot high and rising. Stay warm, people.
Neumo’s and Massline regret to inform you that tonight’s Blue Scholars, Common Market, Physics show has been canceled due to the snow. So stay home, play in the snow, drink hot chocolate, but don’t come to Neumos ’cause the show is off (though Moe Bar will be open).
 
The shows for Friday and Saturday night are ON. If you have tickets for tonight, you can refund them at point of purchase and purchase new tickets for tomorrow or Saturday, but get them fast. If you purchased your tickets for tonight online, please contact webmaster@ticketswest.com for exact instructions or call (800.992.TIXX (8499) or 206.632.TIXX (8499) or 503-224-TIXX (8499)). Have available your order number information so they can process your refund as quickly as possible.
