MUSIC VIDEOS: J. Cole “Who Dat” (& A Brief History of the One-Shot Rap Video)

June 30th, 2010

When done right, the one-shot music video is the purest and highest expression of the form (when done wrong, nothing screams “amateur” louder). No artificial effects, no ADD-inducing editing, no unnecessary bullshit. It’s such a fitting video style for a genre of music as straightforward as rap is. A juxtaposition of visual continuity with lyrical randomness. You’d think there’d be hundreds of one-shot rap videos like there are for other genres. Maybe, despite every rappers fantasy (including mine) to do a music video in one take, it’s actually rare to find a director who can pull it off. J. Cole & director BBGun (who also directed Asher Roth’s one-shot video “Muddy Swim Trunks”) do just that, following this cinema-rap tradition with “Who Dat.” Here’s a few of my favorite one-take hip-hop videos.
