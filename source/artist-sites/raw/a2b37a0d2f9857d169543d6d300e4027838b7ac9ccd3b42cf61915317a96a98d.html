Recently stumbled across this listening session Kiwi and I did at the imeem.com headquarters last year when Blue Scholars was on the Cali leg of the Liquid Swords tour with GZA. What does this have to do with cinema? Only that Liquid Swords is pretty much noir on wax. Listen to it in one sitting and you’ll hear, maybe even see, what I mean.
