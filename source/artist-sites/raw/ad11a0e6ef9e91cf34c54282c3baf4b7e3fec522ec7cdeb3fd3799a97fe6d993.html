This Is It (Kenny Ortega, 2009)

December 18, 2009

Michael Jackson’s concerts might’ve been theatric multimedia spectacles, but This Is It (2009), a collection of footage constructed from rehearsals for what was to be his last concert run, keeps it thankfully simple. No special effects, no attempts to overly emotionalize (except towards the very end, where it’s rightfully expected), no reality-TV like “drama.” Watching MJ just do his thing reveals how much manpower goes into giving a world-class performer a world-class show. Its as much a documentary about the folks in the background–the band, the dancers, the sound engineers, the videographers–as it is about the dude at the very front. MJ is insistent on all aspects of his show being precise, adding “do it with love, god bless” to his gentle demands. It was hard not to watch this and trying to find anything that would foretell a soon-coming demise. He appears physically and mentally sharp, especially for a guy who’s been performing for about 46 out of his 50 years, who’s about to do 50 straight sold-out concerts. A concert that will now only exist in an imagination assisted by a fitting tribute documentary that gives us glimpses of what might’ve been.
