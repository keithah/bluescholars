MUSIC: DLRN – The Bridge

July 21st, 2010 | No Comments »

This week, Sacramento’s DLRN (formerly Delorean: emcee 5th Ave & producer Jon Reyes) have returned with The Bridge, their follow-up to last year’s debut album No More Heroes. I’m featured on the track “Reset,” which we recently shot a music video for a few weeks ago with director Sami Abdou (who also helmed DLRN’s “Dear Langston” music video).
