NEWS: We have a winner!

- Sabzi News

About a 11 days ago, we put out the call for votes on which t-shirt design to print up for the shows we had last week. After counting about 400+ votes cast thru comments on the the blog, Twitter, and Facebook, it was clear that design #4 was the most popular among all of you. #1 came in close second, followed distantly by #2 and #3.

We only printed up about 200 pieces and, after our trip to Oregon, they’re almost all gone.

However, we did save one shirt in particular for the person who could make us laugh the hardest when answering the question “Watcha gon do today?”

The responses were many. Let’s take a look at a few after the jump…

Some people took the question seriously and shared with us how they were hoping to make the world a better place:


then there were…

I’ve always been more of a Protoss guy myself. (Btw, it’s BIG BANK HANK not big bang hawk)


not sure if that’s to be read literally or figuratively. Also not sure which interpretation is more disturbing.

There’s a consistent theme in these three:

A few people picked up on some meaning behind design #2…

Maybe we should do a short run of #2 for the die-hards. Moving on…

It’s really the guy’s profile picture that takes that comment to the next level.

Sounds like a pretty full day, Ian.

Sorry to hear that, Janet. Maybe you can get some tips from Ian.

At the end of the day, there were many votes, several decent responses to “Watcha gon do today,” and a few chuckles.

However, it wasn’t until we found the following gem that we laughed out loud.

Now, at the time this was sent, we were pretty sure that the comment’s intended meaning was missing a few words, or at least put out of order. It looks like he meant to say something like “spit in [the face of] assholes” or “spit on all of the assholes who” but instead put:

Michael: Why you would have any desire to get smart and strong simply to (vengefully) spit inside of another person’s asshole is beyond me. If you did do that, would you wear this t-shirt while doing it? Would your victory pose look something like your profile picture? Were you really the 42nd person to pick “jetpack” as your Twitter username?

Either way, whether you meant to say that or if it was some sort of typo, we’d like to award you the free t-shirt. Even if not for making us laugh, then at least it can be a reminder to work hard everyday to get smarter and stronger. That’s great.

Just remember that it isn’t necessary to hold grudges against those who “oppose you.” Our worst opposition is found in our own limited thinking and doubt in our capacity to do great things. Well, that and institutional oppression, but mostly the first thing.  Either way, a vindictive attitude just wastes energy.

On the other hand, if you really did mean what you said, just remember that the opportunity to literally do some inner-asshole spitting is likely closer to you than you think.

Now hit us back up on Twitter and get yo’ shirt! (Sorry it isn’t the one you voted for!)
