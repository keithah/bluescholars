‘The Future Is Not What It Used To Be’ Photo Contest

Posted on March 17, 2010
Filed Under Contests, The Music, photos | 1 Comment

If you missed yesterday’s post with our new joint “Paul Valéry“, you still have time to catch up, download it, and be a part of “The Future is not what it used to be” Photo Contest.

Our vision of the Future is described as a woman looking “hella booty when she cruise the streets”.  But everyone has their own vision, right? The opening acts for the DOUBLE FEATURE have shared their vision of Future with us, too.

Now it’s your turn: Submit a photo inspired by the phrase “The Future is not what it used to be”, we’ll select a few, post our selections, and choose a winner who will receive a pair of tickets to one of our upcoming shows (Seattle DOUBLE FEATURE or Eugene/Portland, anyone?).  If you’re not on the left coast (or in the Northwest for that matter), no need to worry; we got you.  We’ll bundle a package of music for your listening pleasure.

Deadline is this Friday, March 19 @8:00 PM EST.  We’ll announce a winner on Monday, March 22nd.

Send all original photographs/images (no ripping from the interwebs, please) to paulvalerycontest@gmail.com. What does your Future look like?
