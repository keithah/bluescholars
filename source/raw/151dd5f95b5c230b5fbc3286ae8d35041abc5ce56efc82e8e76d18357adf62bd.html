80′s (and 90′s) Babies (Not So) Secret Show

Posted on February 21, 2010
Filed Under Events | 7 Comments

our friend Jordan hit us up a little while ago about doing a mini shindig as a fundraiser for Haiti.  when he used the words “themed house party” and “basement in the U-District” to describe what he had in mind, we said “yes please!”

it was Friday night at Theta Chi.  the house was packed and we raised a lot of money.  the line up included Macklemore, Brothers From Another, Antbeezy, and INGLISH.

take note of the mutli-vintage nature of my jacket.

i must say that was arguably one of the most fun and energetic shows i’ve had the privilege of being a part of.  the space was small, the crowd was super hype, and we debuted a bunch of new material.  i even played some solo joints.

keep an ear open to the literal, digital, and proverbial streets this year because we plan to do more unpublicized themed house party type events in dank basements.  the next one may be in Renton, Kent, Shoreline, South End… hmm…
