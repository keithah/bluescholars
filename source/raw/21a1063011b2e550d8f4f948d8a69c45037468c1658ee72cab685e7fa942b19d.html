FREEWHEELIN 12"

DJs and analog music enthusiasts, wait no longer. Here it is, the first Blue Scholars wax offering in the history of ever. A limited edition 12" featuring three tracks from the self-titled album: Freewheelin, Bruise Brothers and the 2005 re-release track No Rest For the Weary, with instrumentals for each. Sitting in boxes at the homeboy's crib until yall start copping em right here in THE STORE.

EP RECORDING FINISHED

If you've been paying attention at a live show or heard through the grapevine, you've known that an EP has been in the works for months. Once the tracks were decided, Geo and Sabzi went ahead and recorded the vocals for all of them on one Saturday night near the ambiance of backyard barbeque in the Southend Seattle summer. At about 230 am, THE LONG MARCH EP was born.

Before beginning production on the second LP, we felt it was time to drop an in-between project, to mark this transition between the tracks recorded in early 2003 and the evolving and emerging next shit! THE LONG MARCH EP will be available online and in Seattle music stores in late November/early December. Stay tuned for details on a possible record release party...
