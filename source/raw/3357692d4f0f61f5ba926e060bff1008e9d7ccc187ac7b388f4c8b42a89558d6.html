The CINEMETROPOLIS Listening Party, part 1

- Geo Cinemetropolis, photos

A couple Sundays ago, we unveiled the 15 new tracks off Cinemetropolis at a listening party with fam in Seattle. It was a homecoming and reunion for us, as it was held at the Theater Off Jackson upstairs room, formerly the old Wing Luke Asian Museum, where I worked from 2002-2006 — the first four years of Blue Scholars’s existence. It was there that we finally announced who we decided to sign with for our next record, officially launching the Cinemetropolis campaign with new music and videos to drop in the weeks leading up to the album’s May digital release and June physical release.

Shoutout to Greg Nissen, who took these filthy photos (except, of course, the one he’s in at the bottom of this page). Hit up Greg’s tumblr for more photos from the listening party and check out what Guerilla Candy, Seattle Show Gal, and The Stranger have to say bout the party and first impressions of the new record.
