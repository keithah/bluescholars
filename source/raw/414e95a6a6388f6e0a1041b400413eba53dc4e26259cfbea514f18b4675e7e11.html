Macklemore + Ryan Lewis = Vs.

Posted on December 1, 2009
Filed Under The Music | Leave a Comment

While prepping the release of his music video for “The Town” (check the behind the scenes footage at Loveandshoestrings.com), our guy Macklemore has dropped an EP with producer Ryan Lewis under the name Vs. They rocked two back-to-back release shows in one night last Friday at Nectar in Seattle, where we did a surprise performance of all new material. You should’ve been there. But if you weren’t, you can make up for it by downloading these tracks from the EP (via 2dopeboyz.com):
