PHOTOS: GO! MACHINE Part 1

Posted on December 7, 2009
Filed Under photos | 30 Comments

I hit up GO! Machine last Friday at The Crocodile (Seattle) with a Konica Minolta Zoom 160c that I copped at Goodwill for five bucks. Loaded with Kodak Portra 800 color film. Developed and scanned at Walgreens. The photos seemed to get better the more drinks I had in my system. Whoever can correctly count how many visible cans of PBR are in this series of pics gets a free Angry Woebots OOF! Poster (we have a winner). More photos after the jump.
