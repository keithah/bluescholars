“SLICK WATTS” Short Film Premiere – May 18

- Geo Cinemetropolis, UPCOMING

As we’re ticking down the final hours of the Cinemetropolis kickstarter campaign, we’d like to announce the arrival of the first short film spawned from the album: Slick Watts, directed by Jason Reid and the Sonicsgate team, starring Blue Scholars and the OG himself, Slick Watts, featuring the song Slick Watts.

You can catch the premiere screening of the short in Seattle on Wednesday, May 18th 8pm at SIFF Cinema as a preview for the narrative film series Every Day is a Journey.

Big thanks to Sonicsgate and The Watts Foundation for collaborating on this project. More info to come.
