WE GOT SIGNED! THANKS FOR MAKING CINEMETROPOLIS A REALITY

- Hollis Cinemetropolis


We made the call. You overwhelmingly responded. On March 6, Blue Scholars launched a Kickstarter campaign to help fund the release of our next album, Cinemetropolis. Our goal was $25,000, which we reached in just over one week. The campaign ended after 45 days on April 21, with a final total of $62,371 raised from 2,242 people. Daaaang.

This was way more than we expected and confirms one thing we’ve suspected all along: we have the filthiest fans in the universe. With your contributions, we’re hella pleased to announce that Cinemetropolis is on its way, with the first round of video projects and new merch already in production. The album itself will drop on June 14, with two shows to celebrate the release in Seattle, June 17 and 18 at Neumos. Now, on to your questions:

When will I get my advance digital copy?
Those who pledged to get the advance digital copy will receive a one-time download link via e-mail on May 15.

When will I get my CD/Vinyl/Poster?
The CDs, vinyl and posters will be released to the public and delivered to people who pledged on June 14. The deluxe package CD will be available in July (see below).

When will I get my limited edition deluxe package CD?
Due to the awesome nature of its packaging, including a photo and lyric booklet, the deluxe package CD will be coming later in July.

Oh no, I missed the deadline! Can I still pledge?
Unfortunately, no. Kickstarter freezes the campaign after the deadline, and is currently processing all payments. But sit tight for the launch of our new web store on BlueScholars.com, where you will be able to purchase digital and hard copies of the album, as well as items from the Cinemetropolis product line. (Yes, including the snazzglasses!)
