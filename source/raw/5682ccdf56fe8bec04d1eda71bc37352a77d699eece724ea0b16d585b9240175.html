BERKELEY: Blue Scholars Headline Hip Hop in the Park This Saturday

Posted on April 28, 2010
Filed Under Events, News, Shows, Tourdates, UPCOMING | Leave a Comment

The annual Student’s for Hip Hop presents Hip Hop in the Park on Saturday May 1st, 2010. Come chill on the grasses of People’s Park in Berkeley as Blue Scholars headlines a day of extraordinary music. Macklemore is also on the bill, check the lineup (below) or visit Calhiphop.net for more info. See you there!

WHERE:
People’s Park, Berkeley
2556 Haste Street
Berkeley, CA 94704
- Half a block east of Telegraph Avenue between Haste Street and Dwight Way
MAP

WHEN:
Saturday, May 1st 2010
12:00 PM – 5:00 PM
Free Entry
