NEW SHOWS: The Showbox presents ‘Blue Scholars DOUBLE FEATURE’

Posted on February 17, 2010
Filed Under DOUBLE FEATURE, News, Press, Shows | 7 Comments

After taking a short trip to Lincoln, Nebraska and Raleigh, North Carolina for a few college shows (one of which was canceled because of the arctic storm that annihilated the city of Raleigh), we’re hype to bring it back home for a very special event.

We’ve got a few projects on the horizon, and we’re excited to present ‘Blue Scholars DOUBLE FEATURE‘ at The Showbox — back-to-back shows for a new, refreshing dose of cinema art rap.

Get your tickets and check out the opening lineup, with performances from Macklemore, Das Racist, Gordon Voidwell and more.

For tickets purchase them here or visit The Showbox box office: 1426 1st avenue, Seattle, WA.
