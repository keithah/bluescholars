VIDEO: Green River Community College

Posted on September 14, 2010
Filed Under Video | 8 Comments

Something like this has been long overdue.

Now, thanks to affordable, bite-size technology, we are able to show you some of the story that goes on off-stage.

It also seems appropriate to share this now considering that we’re about to hit up 4 East Coast cities with our townmates, Macklemore & Ryan Lewis. If you’ve never seen us all rock together, you’re in for a treat.

Green River Community College is a modestly sized educational institution located about 45 minutes from Seattle. About 2 hours if the traffic is bad. The good folks at GRCC invited us to do a show so we took the camera along and captured a little bit of a very typical Blue Scholars day.

I was still learning how to use the camera’s basic functions when this was filmed (still trying to learn actually) so I hope you enjoy all the overexposed shots.
