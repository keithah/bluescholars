#CINE RELEASE SHOW SPECIAL GUESTS: MACKLEMORE, EL MIZELL, DJ SOSA

- Hollis Uncategorized

To help ring in Cinemetropolis at our release shows on June 17 and 18 at Neumos, we’ve hand-picked four of the best emerging Seattle acts and a couple of town-favorite hosts that we can’t wait to party with. We’ll be rolling out our supporting acts and hosts over this week to let you know who will be joining us to launch the new new.

We are stoked to announce that Macklemore and El Mizell of Mash Hall will be hosting our release shows on Friday, June 17 & Saturday, June 18 respectively, and that DJ Sosa will be joining us as DJ for both nights.

Do yourself a favor and cop tickets, now.
