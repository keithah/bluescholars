Grynch / My Volvo Remix

Posted on August 31, 2010
Filed Under music | 2 Comments

Not long ago, our buddy Grynch hit me up to do a remix for his local hit, My Volvo. I said “sure buddy, on 3 conditions:
1. you permit me to make the tune sound even more pop-like,
2. you let me follow you around your neighborhood for an afternoon with a camera, and
3. you write an essay about something Ballard-y.”

He agreed.
