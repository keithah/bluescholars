WE’RE IN NYC PLAYING THE LIGHTUP & AAIFF MUSIC NIGHT OUT PARTY (FRI AUG 12)

- Geo Events

We’re back in NYC this weekend for the Asian American International Film Festival. Come party with us at Drom in the East Village - we’ll be screening our latest music video, Fou Lee, and rocking a set of new joints off Cinemetropolis.

LIGHTUP FILM’S MUSIC NIGHT OUT

Friday, August 12
10:00pm

Drom
85 Ave A (btw 5th & 6th Sts)
New York, NY

FEATURING BLUE SCHOLARS & JESSE BOYKINS III

Come out to the best party of the Festival! The perfect union of music and film at AAIFF’s annual music video showcase plus live performance.

Featuring new music videos from independent artists and performances by the acclaimed hip hop duo Blue Scholars and Jesse Boykins III, a BET award nominated world soul singer. The evening is curated by LightUp Film, a full service independent media and production company.

*This event is 21+*
