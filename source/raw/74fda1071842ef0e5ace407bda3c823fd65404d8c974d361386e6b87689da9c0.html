Thumbs Up or Down? M.I.A.’s ‘Born Free’ Video

Posted on April 30, 2010
Filed Under Arts/Culture, Video, music | 22 Comments

Since April 25th – the day the video launched online – M.I.A.’s new ‘Born Free’ video has received approximately 1.5 million views on Vimeo.

Blue Scholars fam has mixed opinions about the song and the accompanying visuals (we’ll keep our opinions to ourselves, for now).  That said: what are your thoughts / feelings about this viral attempt? Please, we’d love to know.

Watch it (below) and enjoy your Friday, wherever you might be! (and if you’re in the Bay Area don’t forget: we’re at Hip Hop in the Park tomorrow in Berkeley).
