TOUR BLOG: DAY 7 – ALBUQUERQUE, NM

- Sabzi Freshly Dipped Tour 2008, photos

the last time i was in Albuquerque at the Sunshine Theater was with Common Market on Dan The Automator’s 2K7 tour. a certain person in Hiero was talking about how much they couldn’t stand this venue cuz bad things always happen whenever they play there. one time Tajai got his camera stolen by security, another time Casual knocked out a security guard, and another time somebody called Hiero’s fan’s “hippies” and then they got themselves beat down pretty hard and couple people got shot… no good.

it’s only been a couple weeks since we left Hawaii, but Brian and i missed the island so much we had to go to the sushi place next door and ask the dood to custom make us some ahi poke. it wasn’t bad at all.

in spite of the bad karma the venue may have had, the crowd was cool and the show jumped off…. with the exception of the sound guy that was so high out of his mind he kept adjusting the delay on Geo’s vocals so that they overpowered the crowd.


the graffiti was notable. i believe there are a lot of Seattle hip-hop groups that follow the advice scrawled on this exit sign, particularly in the green room at Neumo’s.



this gizmo you see above is Verifone Nurit 8020. it’s a machine we use to take credit card sales on the road. somehow, the charger got lost in Vegas and we have been completely unable to find a replacement. that is, unless we call Verifone directly and get one sent to us for $75. boo on you Verifone!

it’s an 8.4 volt charger that looks just like a cellphone charger. if anybody has any ideas on where to get one for cheaper on the road, hit us up!

outside, some drunk guy was trying to be a badass and punched through this window and then stormed off with a look of determination only a badass would have. he didn’t make it very far before he started holding his wrist in pain from the deep cut the glass made.

security called the cops on him. the guy made the cops call an ambulance on him.


one thing wack about the venue was that they wanted 27% of the gross sales of our soft goods. that means tshirts. and since we keep our tshirt costs low, 27% is also pretty much the whole profit margin on these goods. so we did the thing any independent group would do: we told everybody they could get gear in the parking lot. and they did.

this guy bought everything…


…and i mean EVERYTHING. he went straight to the merch table and bought every single CD that was on there. we were like “what is this? 1998?” but no, it wasn’t. it was just a guy in Albuquerque. a real dedicated hip-hop fan guy. thanks homie!

after the show, on our way out to embark on the 12-hour drive to Dallas, we talked about everything we’d seen at the show. our talking, however, was promptly interrupted with somebody saying “what the–” after seeing…


…that gas in Albuquerque is only 3.99 a gallon. i guess the difference isn’t that much, but it still seems like it’s been a while since we’ve seen a 3 in that dollar column in Seattle.

thanks Albuqueque!
