#CINE RELEASE SHOW SPECIAL GUEST: JUSMONI

- Hollis Uncategorized

To help ring in Cinemetropolis at our release shows on June 17 and 18 at Neumos, we’ve hand-picked four of the best emerging Seattle acts and a couple of town-favorite hosts that we can’t wait to party with. We’ll be rolling out our supporting acts and hosts over this week to let you know who will be joining us to launch the new new.

Having released her debut EP Ready For Life in December to critical acclaim, we are excited to include JUSMONI as a featured artist on June 17 alongside HELLUVASTATE. A spoken word artist, young mother and gifted vocalist, Jusmoni delivers uplifting, soulful R&B with a commanding stage presence.

Join us, Jusmoni & Helluvastate on June 17 by purchasing your advanced tickets at the Neumos box office or online (click here).
