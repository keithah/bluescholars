BAYANI REDUX DROPPING SEPTEMBER 1

- bluescholars News

Little known fact: our last full-length album Bayani (2007 Massline/Rawkus release) is actually, officially, out of print. The 25K copies out there are now collectors items. However, we were fortunate enough to liberate the masters for the album AND get Duck Down to re-release it for folks who may have missed it the first time.

Included on this edition, aside from the new art, are three tracks: “27″ and “Southbound” (previously released on the Joe Metro and BUTTERNGUN$ EPs) and the previously unreleased track “The Dawn Song” featuring Shad.

BAYANI REDUX drops September 1st. Hit the jump for more info & links to order online.
