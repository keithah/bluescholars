This week's Seattle Weekly music section features Blue Scholars, son! Here's a snippet:

So far, Blue Scholars--the Seattle hip-hop duo of 23-year-old DJ Sabzi and MC Geologic, 25--have avoided that hoariest of pop-music cliches by not writing bitchy songs about The Road. But maybe they're due. The first time I interviewed them for this piece, they had just returned from a weeklong road trip; the group drove me in their touring van to the Moonlight Cafe in the International District for a convivial, entertaining lunch. Two days later, they would get in it again and travel down the coast for some more shows--right around the time I discovered that the microphone I'd conducted the interview with had been dead the entire time.

Friday | 11th November 2005

Peace to Oakland: always been a fan of The Coup
@ The Casbah, San Diego


Warm militant greetings, online fam. It's day 7 on the road (halfway through the damn thing), and last we checked Iraq is still under foreign occupation and it now takes three instead of two $20 bills to fill a full tank of gas for a Honda Odyssey.

Euguene, Portland, San Diego and LA... all a blur right now. We'll let it marinate and drop an update when we get back.

Thanks to the folks who let us crash at their spot, came out to a show, or copped some merch. Peace to our fam we didn't get to see before rolling out. Yall have made some Northwesterners feel right at home, and not just because it's rained in every city we've been thus far.
