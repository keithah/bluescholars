RECAP: Slim’s in San Francisco

Posted on April 27, 2010
Filed Under Events, The Travels, photos | 3 Comments

Three years ago, Blue Scholars, Common Market, and Gabriel Teodros got together and did the first (and last) official MassLine tour.  When we made our San Francisco stop on that tour, the show was with Bambu at a venue called Pier 23.

I remember this show especially because it was 21+ and there were a handful of youth from the East Bay that had come out to see us and weren’t able to get in.  Instead of joining the festivities within the venue, the liquor laws of California forced them to watch the show from about 100 feet away behind a fence at a really awkward angle.

This was unacceptable.  The dedication these young folks exhibited had to be acknowledged and we needed to reciprocate it somehow!  Later that nigh,t we decided to call all our community contacts in the Bay area and find a venue where we could put on a last-minute daytime show before we went to L.A. that would be accessible to those of under-21 persuasion. Thankfully, we found a community center on Misson St. and put the word out to the little homies to come thru.

20 people showed up. It was the folks from the night before along with a bunch of Filipino kids from the Mission. The energy in that brightly lit building was unforgettable. Some of them joined us onstage, some of them rapped – including Rudy, the community center coordinator. We packed up afterwards, gave our daps and said our goodbyes, took the picture below, and continued with the rest of the tour.

Fast-forward to Tuesday April 13, 2010:  I’m standing at the bar at Slim’s on 11th Street in SF catching up with an old high school friend of mine… and guess who walks up and taps me on the shoulder? It was a few of the East Bay youth that were at the community center show back in ’07, except now they were all grown up and had made it out to Slim’s to say wassup to Geo and I once again.

“Yo! Remember us from the center?!” That moment definitely made my night.

The same energy and feel that these East Bay cats displayed was echoed by everybody in the crowd that night at Slim’s.  At one point, I got a little too hype during the set and jumped to hang from a pole near the front of the stage.  My apologies to the people whose drinks I knocked over, fingers I stepped on, and faces I kicked.

She’s right.  I didn’t realize it until the Twizzler informed me.  Since then, we smoothed it out (and I think I’ve been forgiven) because everybody involved in that stunt considers the damage as a necessary risk of standing at the front of a Blue Scholars show.  I’m making an official promise on the blog to orJAAASmic and her friends to buy them whatever drinks they like next time we see each other in the Bay.  =)

-Sabzi
