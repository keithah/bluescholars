Bambu …Paper Cuts… EP Drops TODAY

Posted on February 23, 2010
Filed Under The Music | 4 Comments

Featuring guest appearances by Chace Infinite, Thurzday, Diz Gibran, Malcolm N Martin, and Blue Scholars‘ very own Prometheus Brown/Geo. Production by Anon, Fatgums, Nick James, Digital Martyrs, and MTK. Pick it up now on iTunes and Beatrock Music.

And if you’re in Seattle, catch Bambu & DJ Phatrick on the same bill with us on the first night of our Double Feature, March 26th at Showbox at The Market (tickets available online and at the venue).
