7 Questions with Common Market

- bluescholars DOUBLE FEATURE

RA Scion of Common Market is a seasoned emcee.  Born in Louisville, Kentucky, moved internationally to Zambia and Greece, and then finally settled down in the Pacific Northwest,  RA has been at this game steadily, releasing LP’s since 2005 while given opportunities to tour with artists such as KRS-One.

With “abstract imagery, straight-forward slogans and…an unmistakable delivery”, there’s no need for us to convince you that Common Market is one that you won’t want to miss.

As one of our special guests for our upcoming DOUBLE FEATURE at The Showbox on March 26 & 27 (did you purchase your tickets yet?), Common Market was willing to share a few thoughts on the film ‘Belly’, their energetic stage performances and advocacy of polyandry.

Keep both eyes and ears open for a new project, titled ‘Victor Shade’, alter ego for RA Scion.

1. What’s the last movie you watched? Belly (1998)

2. A short review? It’s kinda like Coming To America in reverse – Queens get the money! Hype Williams does his thing here, and I don’t think Kurt Loder’s contribution to the film’s credibility as first-rate cinema could ever be overstated.  Must see.

3. What did you think the year 2010 was going to be like 20 years ago? I thought it would be significantly warmer than this.

4. How did you meet Blue Scholars? Dave Meinert’s Queen Anne Beer Summit, ca. 2005.

5. Describe your stage performance in three words. Out. Of. Breath.

6. If you had to pick a film genre to classify your music, what would it be? Animated Short.

7. Protagonist or Antagonist? I think women should have the right to wed many husbands, so whichever one that is, that’s what I am.
