Blue Scholars’ Final 2010 Show in Seattle – Tonight at The Crocodile

- bluescholars Events

Tonight, we’re playing our last show of 2010 back home in Seattle at The Stranger & Redhook’s Rad Winter Party at The Crocodile. Saba’s on a plane right now en route from NYC, where he was just kicking it with Khingz (townbiz). I’m chillin, waiting for the Apple Cup to start, and semi-rehearsing the new new songs we’re debuting tonight.

We announced the show only on our Facebook and Twitter accounts earlier this week and less than a day later, the show was sold out. So don’t get assed out on the next short-notice announcement and make sure you “like” and “follow” us on those things. And if you “don’t do the social network thing,” then be prepared to be the last to know about things like this show in the future!

I got a feeling that tonight’s gonna be a good night (no Will.I.Am). Everybody gets free swag and two drink tickets at the door. Everybody. We’ll be playing some new material from our upcoming album, Cinemetropolis, for the first time onstage. Lastly, tonight also marks the very last time we’ll play a 21+ venue with no 90s babies (legally) in attendance. That’s right yall. Starting January 1, 2011 there will be 90s babies all up in your bar. So to all the 60s, 70s and 80s babies in the building (or before), let’s close out 2010 and celebrate our elder status like we used to. And still do. See you all tonight.
