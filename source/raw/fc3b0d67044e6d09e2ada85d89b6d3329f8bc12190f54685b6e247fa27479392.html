MLK Day (Mon, 1/17) Workshop: End Police Violence, Deportation and Suvelliance Now!

Posted on January 13, 2011
Filed Under Events | 2 Comments

Four seconds. That’s how long it took for SPD Officer Ian Birk to gun down John T. Williams in broad daylight last August 30, as evidenced in this dashboard video that was ordered released by a judge last month. This week, the inquest into this shooting began at the King County Courthouse. Since I’ve posted this video on Facebook and Twitter and even put out this open call to the mayor, we’ve been getting hit up by folks asking how they can take action.

First and foremost: stay posted on all the updates surrounding this case on our local news outlets, particularly independent sources such as The Stranger. Second, there’s been a resurgence of community forums and workshops addressing police violence. Next Monday morning on Martin Luther King, Jr. Day, prior to the annual MLK rally and march at noon, several workshops will be held at Garfield High School. We encourage folks to hit up End Police Violence, Deportation and Suvelliance Now!, conducted by Lynn Domingo in room 217.
